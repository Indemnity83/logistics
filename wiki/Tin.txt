'''Tin''' is a metal found underground in the Overworld. It generates in tin ore, which drops raw tin when mined; the raw tin is smelted into tin ingots and crafted into components.

== Blocks and items ==

* [[File:Grid Tin Ore.png|24px|link=Tin Ore]] '''[[Tin Ore]]''' — an ore found underground, with a Deepslate Tin Ore variant in the deepslate layer
* [[File:Grid Raw Tin.png|24px|link=Raw Tin]] '''[[Raw Tin]]''' — an item that drops from tin ore when mined
* [[File:Grid Tin Ingot.png|24px|link=Tin Ingot]] '''[[Tin Ingot]]''' — an item created by smelting raw tin
* [[File:Grid Tin Nugget.png|24px|link=Tin Nugget]] '''[[Tin Nugget]]''' — one-ninth of a tin ingot
* [[File:Grid Block of Raw Tin.png|24px|link=Block of Raw Tin]] '''[[Block of Raw Tin]]''' — a block used for compact raw tin storage
* [[File:Grid Block of Tin.png|24px|link=Block of Tin]] '''[[Block of Tin]]''' — a block used for compact tin ingot storage
* [[File:Grid Tin Dust.png|24px|link=Tin Dust]] '''[[Tin Dust]]''' — produced by grinding tin ore in a [[Macerator]] (ore doubling)

== Uses ==

* [[File:Grid Bronze Ingot.png|24px|link=Bronze Ingot]] '''[[Bronze Ingot]]''' — a tin-and-copper alloy
