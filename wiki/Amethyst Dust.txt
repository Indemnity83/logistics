== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1
|name=[[Crafting Logistics Pipe]]
|B1=Iron Gear
|A2=Amethyst Dust |B2=Basic Logistics Pipe |C2=Amethyst Dust
|Output=Crafting Logistics Pipe
}}

An [[Iron Chipset]] substitutes for the [[Iron Gear]].

=== Infusing ===

A [[Transposer]] infuses it with [[Liquid Glowstone]] into [[Lumenite Dust]], the resonant rung of the power ladder:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Amethyst Dust |FluidIn=Liquid Glowstone |InAmount=1000 |Output=Lumenite Dust
|ingredients=One [[Amethyst Dust]] plus 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Glowstone]] in the tank
|notes=3,000 RF}}
