The '''Supplier Logistics Pipe''' is a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]] that keeps an adjacent inventory stocked.

== Usage ==

The Supplier Logistics Pipe checks its inventory every second against up to 9 supply targets (item + amount) and orders the shortfall from the network, tracking in-transit items so it never over-requests. A '''Supply Mode''' sets the strategy:

{| class="wikitable"
! Mode !! Behavior
|-
| '''Partial''' || Request whatever is available, up to the needed amount (default)
|-
| '''Stocked''' || Only order once stock drops to 50% or below, then request the full shortfall
|-
| '''Infinite''' || Ignore the target; continuously fill all space, one stack at a time
|-
| '''Full''' || Only request if the complete shortfall is available — all-or-nothing
|}

== Power ==

Like every [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]], it draws [[RF Energy|RF]] through a [[Power Junction]] — it renders '''green''' while powered and '''red''' when the network has no charge, in which case routing stops and items in transit drop.
