The '''Item Void Pipe''' is a [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] that destroys any item reaching it.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of {{Mcw|Glass}} flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Obsidian |B2=Glass |C2=Ender Pearl
|Output=Item Void Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

Any item that reaches the center of an Item Void Pipe is destroyed after a brief visible delay. Use it as overflow disposal at the end of a filtered line, behind an [[Item Filter Pipe]].
