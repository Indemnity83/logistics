'''Bronze Ingot''' is an alloy created from copper and tin. It is used for the [[Machine Core]], [[Sturdy Casing]], bronze valves, and gears.

'''Related forms:''' Bronze has a full storage set — '''Block of Bronze''' (9 ingots) and '''Bronze Nugget''' (1/9 ingot) — plus '''Bronze Dust''' from the [[Macerator]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Bronze Ingot is an alloy of copper and tin:

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A2=Copper Ingot |B2=Copper Ingot |C2=Copper Ingot
|A3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Bronze Ingot |OutputCount=4
}}

'''Shapeless:''' 3× Copper Ingot + 1× [[Tin Ingot]] = 4× Bronze Ingot.

Bronze Ingots can also be packed into and unpacked from their storage forms:

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B2=Bronze Block
|Output=Bronze Ingot |OutputCount=9
}}

'''From a Bronze Block:''' 1× Bronze Block = 9× Bronze Ingot.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Bronze Nugget |B1=Bronze Nugget |C1=Bronze Nugget
|A2=Bronze Nugget |B2=Bronze Nugget |C2=Bronze Nugget
|A3=Bronze Nugget |B3=Bronze Nugget |C3=Bronze Nugget
|Output=Bronze Ingot
}}

'''From nuggets:''' 9× Bronze Nugget = 1× Bronze Ingot.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Bronze Ingot |B1=Bronze Ingot |C1=Bronze Ingot
|A2=Bronze Ingot |B2=Bronze Ingot |C2=Bronze Ingot
|A3=Bronze Ingot |B3=Bronze Ingot |C3=Bronze Ingot
|Output=Bronze Block
}}

'''To a block:''' 9× Bronze Ingot = 1× Bronze Block.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B2=Bronze Ingot
|Output=Bronze Nugget |OutputCount=9
}}

'''To nuggets:''' 1× Bronze Ingot = 9× Bronze Nugget.

== Usage ==

Bronze is the basis of machine components and several bronze items.

=== Machine components ===

* [[Machine Core]] - 8× bronze ingot
* [[Sturdy Casing]] - 8× bronze ingot

=== Valves ===

Craft a [[Valves|Bronze Valve]] via a Bronze [[Cores|Core]].

=== Gears ===

Craft a [[Bronze Gear]] from 4× bronze ingot.

=== Grinding ===

Grind bronze in the [[Macerator]] for Bronze Dust (see [[Dusts]]).

=== Storage ===

* 9× Bronze Ingot → Block of Bronze
* 9× Bronze Nugget → 1× Bronze Ingot
