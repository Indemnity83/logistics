'''Cores''' are intermediate crafting components used to craft [[Valves]]. Each valve needs its matching core, paired with a [[#Quartz Crystal|Quartz Crystal]]. Every core is crafted from a base material and a binder (usually Redstone) and yields four per craft.

== Quartz Crystal ==

Quartz Crystal is not a core, but it is the other ingredient in every valve recipe. Smelt a [[Quartz Dust]] in a [[Kiln]] or furnace:

{{Smelting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|Input=Quartz Dust
|Output=Quartz Crystal
|XP=0.1
}}

Quartz Dust is ground from nether quartz in the [[Macerator]], so valve crafting is gated behind having a Macerator and a [[Kiln]] running.
