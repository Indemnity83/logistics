'''Fluid Pipes''' move and store liquids through a '''standalone''' fluid system, separate from the item-carrying [[Pipes|logistics network]]. Fluids never join the item [[Pipe Networks|network graph]] — they get their own local transport, storage, and extraction.

==How it works==

* '''One fluid per line''' — each connected run of fluid pipe carries a single fluid type. A line carrying one fluid won't accept another until it drains (no mixing).
* '''Pressure equalization''' — fluid flows from fuller pipes toward emptier ones and toward sinks, capped each tick by the pipe's transfer rate.
* '''Passive transport is free''' — stone, copper, and golden pipes (and the void and bypass pipes) move fluid without any power.
* '''Active extraction costs power''' — only the [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] (driven by an adjacent engine) and the [[Pump]] (RF) consume energy to pull fluid in.
* Works with any mod's fluid storage on both Fabric and NeoForge.

==Transport Pipes==

Passive movers that carry fluid between tanks, machines, and other pipes at increasing rates. The slowest pipe on a line caps its throughput.

* '''[[Stone Fluid Pipe]]''' - Cheap, slow transport (10 mB/t)
* '''[[Copper Fluid Pipe]]''' - Standard transport backbone (10 mB/t)
* '''[[Golden Fluid Pipe]]''' - High-throughput transport (80 mB/t)

==Behavior Pipes==

Pipes that shape how fluid enters, leaves, or connects.

* '''[[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]''' - Pull fluid from tanks and machines; driven by an adjacent engine
* '''[[Insertion Fluid Pipe]]''' - Fill adjacent tanks and machines first, passing fluid on only when they are full
* '''[[Merger Fluid Pipe]]''' - Directional check valve; fluid leaves only through a wrench-set face
* '''[[Bypass Fluid Pipe]]''' - Connect only to other fluid pipes, never to tanks or machines
* '''[[Void Fluid Pipe]]''' - Destroy any fluid that flows into it

==Storage & Sources==

* '''[[Glass Tank]]''' - 16,000 mB buffer; stack tanks vertically to merge into one larger reservoir
* '''[[Pump]]''' - Extract fluid source blocks from the world into your tanks and pipes (RF powered)
