Learn the basics by building a simple pipe network that extracts items from a chest and delivers them to another chest.

==What You'll Build==

A basic transport system:

[[File:2026-07-09 12.12.17.png|none|thumb|500x500px]]

This teaches the fundamentals: extraction, transport, and insertion.

==Materials Needed==

Craft these items first:

'''Pipes:'''
* 1 or more [[Copper Transport Pipe]] ({{Mcw|Copper Ingot|copper ingot}} + {{Mcw|Glass|glass}})
* 1× [[Item Extractor Pipe]] ({{Mcw|Planks|planks}} + glass)

'''Power:'''
* 1× [[Redstone Engine]]
* 1× {{Mcw|Lever}} (to switch the engine on)

'''Tool:'''
* 1× [[Wrench]] (4× {{Mcw|Iron Ingot|iron ingot}})

'''Test setup:'''
* 2× {{Mcw|Chest}} (source and destination)
* Some items to transport

==Step-by-Step Build==

===1. Place Source Chest===
Place a chest and fill it with some items ({{Mcw|Cobblestone|cobblestone}}, {{Mcw|Dirt|dirt}}, anything).

===2. Place Extractor Pipe===
Place an [[Item Extractor Pipe]] directly next to the chest. You should see it connect automatically (opaque connector appears).

===3. Power the Extractor===

The Item Extractor Pipe requires energy to run. Place a [[Redstone Engine]] directly adjacent to the extractor pipe, then place a lever on the back of the engine to switch it on. The engine's output face must touch the extractor pipe — use a [[Wrench]] to rotate it if needed.

===4. Configure Extraction Face===
The [[Item Extractor Pipe|Extractor Pipe]] can only extract from one chest. If you place more than one chest adjacent to it, use a [[Wrench]] to toggle which chest it extracts from — you'll see the opaque arm of the pipe move between the chests.

===5. Build Transport Line===
Place [[Copper Transport Pipe|Copper Transport Pipes]] extending from the extractor:

* Connect to any free face of the extractor
* Extend as far as you want
* Pipes connect automatically to each other

===6. Place Destination Chest===
Place a second chest at the end of your copper pipe line. The pipe should automatically connect to it (you'll see a connector appear).

===7. Watch It Work===
Flip the lever on the engine, and items automatically extract from the source chest, travel through the pipes (you can see them moving!), and enter the destination chest. With a [[Redstone Engine]], you'll get one item extracted for every pulse of the engine; as the engine warms up, it starts operating faster.

'''If items aren't moving:'''
* Check that a [[Redstone Engine]] is adjacent to the extractor with its output face touching it (use the wrench to rotate)
* Check the engine is switched on (the lever is flipped)
* Check the extraction face points at the source chest (use the wrench on the extractor)
* Verify pipes connect to both chests (connectors visible)
* Ensure the source chest has items

==What You Learned==

'''Automatic connection:'''
* Pipes connect to adjacent pipes and inventories automatically
* Visual connectors show connections

'''Extraction:'''
* [[Item Extractor Pipe|Item Extractor Pipes]] pull from inventories
* Configure the extraction face with a [[Wrench]]
* Only one face extracts at a time

'''Transport:'''
* [[Copper Transport Pipe|Copper Transport Pipes]] move items
* Items visible while traveling
* Random routing at junctions (we'll address this next)

'''Insertion:'''
* Items automatically try to enter adjacent inventories
* Works with any inventory ({{Mcw|Chest|chests}}, {{Mcw|Furnace|furnaces}}, {{Mcw|Hopper|hoppers}}, etc.)

==Next Steps==

===Controlled Routing===
Random routing is fine for single destinations, but what about multiple chests?

Use an [[Item Merger Pipe]] to guarantee direction. Its opaque arm marks the output direction and can be adjusted with the [[Wrench|wrench]]. Items can't enter a merger pipe through its output face — they'll be dropped on the ground instead.

==Common Mistakes==

'''Items drop instead of entering destination:'''
* Destination chest might be full
* Pipe might not be connected (no connector visible)
* Use an [[Item Insertion Pipe]] to prefer inventories

'''Extraction face pointing wrong way:'''
* Right-click with the wrench to cycle faces
* The opaque connector shows the active face
* It should point directly at the source chest

'''Pipes don't connect:'''
* Must be adjacent (no gaps)
* Check for [[Marking Fluid]] preventing connection
* [[Item Passthrough Pipe|Item Passthrough Pipes]] don't connect to inventories
