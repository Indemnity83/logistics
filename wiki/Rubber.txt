'''Rubber''' is a flexible crafting component used to insulate [[Cables|power cables]]. It is made in two steps: a '''Rubber Mix''' is crafted from a slime ball and [[Coal Dust]], then smelted to cure it into finished '''Rubber Chunks'''.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Combine a slime ball with one [[Coal Dust]] (shapeless) to make a Rubber Mix:

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A2=Slime Ball |B2=Coal Dust
|Output=Rubber Mix
}}

=== Smelting ===

Smelt a Rubber Mix in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]] to cure it into '''two''' Rubber Chunks:

{{Smelting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Rubber Mix
|Output=Rubber Chunk,2
|XP=0.1
}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Rubber Chunks insulate every [[Cables|power cable]] — six chunks wrapped around a conductive core, crafted '''8 at a time''':

{{Crafting|head=1|showdescription=1
|name=[[Cables|Copper Cable]] |A1=Rubber Chunk |B1=Rubber Chunk |C1=Rubber Chunk |A2=Copper Ingot |B2=Copper Ingot |C2=Copper Ingot |A3=Rubber Chunk |B3=Rubber Chunk |C3=Rubber Chunk |Output=Copper Cable,8 |description=30 RF/t — the cheapest grade.}}
{{Crafting
|name=[[Cables|Gold Cable]] |A1=Rubber Chunk |B1=Rubber Chunk |C1=Rubber Chunk |A2=Gold Ingot |B2=Gold Ingot |C2=Gold Ingot |A3=Rubber Chunk |B3=Rubber Chunk |C3=Rubber Chunk |Output=Gold Cable,8 |description=60 RF/t — the mid grade.}}
{{Crafting|foot=1
|name=[[Cables|Ender Cable]] |A1=Rubber Chunk |B1=Rubber Chunk |C1=Rubber Chunk |A2=Ender Dust |B2=Gold Ingot |C2=Ender Dust |A3=Rubber Chunk |B3=Rubber Chunk |C3=Rubber Chunk |Output=Ender Cable,8 |description=120 RF/t — the highest throughput.}}

A single Rubber Mix smelts into two chunks, so a [[Macerator]] → [[Kiln]] line — grinding coal into [[Coal Dust]] — turns out rubber cheaply in bulk.
