The '''Macerator''' is an RF-powered grinding machine that converts items into [[Dusts|dust]] and other ground materials. Dust is smelted back into ingots in a [[Kiln]] or an [[Alloy Smelter]], which is what makes grinding an ore worth more than smelting it whole.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Macerator is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

Place an item in the input slot; the output slot receives the ground result when complete, and a secondary slot catches any chance byproduct. It processes 150+ recipes covering ores (with chance byproducts), block recycling, plants, and dyes, and grants XP when processing ore-based recipes. Each recipe has its own RF cost — most ores take 2,000 RF (10 seconds), recycling recipes more. The recipe book in the GUI lists every valid recipe, and Macerator recipes also appear in the Just Enough Items (JEI) recipe viewer.

Input is accepted from the top and sides; output is drawn from the bottom.

=== Power ===

The Macerator runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 10,000 RF, accepts up to 128 RF/tick, and consumes 10 RF/tick while active. Connect a [[Stirling Engine]] or any RF source to power it.

=== Setup ===

# '''Place the Macerator''' in your processing area
# '''Connect power''' — attach a [[Stirling Engine]] or any RF source
# '''Feed input''' — pipe items into the top or sides
# '''Collect output''' — pipe from the bottom into storage or directly to a [[Kiln]]

==== Flowers → dyes ====

Any flower can be ground into its corresponding dye color (Poppy → {{Mcw|Red Dye}}, Dandelion → {{Mcw|Yellow Dye}}, etc.). Supports all 16 vanilla flower variants including multi-block plants (Lilac, Sunflower, etc.).

=== Common uses ===

'''Ore doubling:'''
<pre>
[Ore] → Macerator → [Dust × 2] → Kiln → [Ingot × 2]
</pre>
Grinding an ore block yields 2 dust, each smeltable into 1 ingot — effectively doubling your ore output compared to direct smelting.

'''Bronze alloying:'''
<pre>
[Copper Dust × 3] + [Tin Dust] → craft → [Bronze Dust × 4] → Kiln → [Bronze Ingot × 4]
</pre>

'''Quartz Crystal production (required for Valves):'''
<pre>
[Nether Quartz] → Macerator → [Quartz Dust] → Kiln → [Quartz Crystal]
</pre>
