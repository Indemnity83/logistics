'''Iron Chipset''' is a fabricated component built in the [[Sequential Fabricator]]. Chipsets are an alternative to [[Gears|gears]] for crafting [[Modules|modules]] and [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipes]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Sequential Fabricator ===

{{Sequential Fabricator|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Iron Ingot |Output=Iron Chipset |notes=20,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

An Iron Chipset can stand in for its gear when crafting:

* [[Chassis Logistics Pipe MkII]]
* [[Crafter Module]]
* [[Crafting Logistics Pipe]]
* [[Extractor Module]]
* [[ItemSink Module]]
* [[Passive Supplier Module]]
* [[Polymorphic ItemSink Module]]
* [[Process Logistics Pipe]]
* [[Supplier Logistics Pipe]]
* [[Terminus Module]]
