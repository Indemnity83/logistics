'''Tin Ingot''' is a metal obtained by smelting [[Tin Ore|raw tin]]. It's used to create bronze alloy and tin-based components.

'''Related forms:''' Tin Ingot has a full set of storage and processing forms — '''Block of Tin''' (9 ingots), '''Tin Nugget''' (1/9 ingot), '''Raw Tin''' and the '''Block of Raw Tin''', and '''Tin Dust''' from the [[Macerator]].

==Recipe==

'''Smelting:'''
* Raw Tin (from [[Tin Ore]]) → Tin Ingot
* Furnace or blast furnace

==Usage==

'''Bronze Alloy:'''
* Tin + Copper → [[Bronze Ingot]]

'''Gears:'''
* [[Tin Gear]] - 4× tin ingot

'''Dust:'''
* Grind in the [[Macerator]] → Tin Dust (see [[Dusts]])

'''Storage:'''
* 9× Tin Ingot → Block of Tin
* 9× Tin Nugget → 1× Tin Ingot

==Tips==

* Common metal, easy to obtain
* Essential for bronze production
* Stock up for gear production

==Related Items==

* '''Block of Tin''' - Compact storage (9 ingots)
* '''Tin Nugget''' - 1/9 of an ingot
* '''Raw Tin''' / '''Block of Raw Tin''' - Mined drop and its storage block
* '''Tin Dust''' - Ground form from the [[Macerator]] (see [[Dusts]])
