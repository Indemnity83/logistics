'''Seed Oil''' is a pale yellow fluid pressed from {{Mcw|Seeds|seeds}} in a [[Transposer]]. Because seeds are farmed, it is fully renewable — but it is slow to gather, at 50 mB per seed.

== Obtaining ==

=== Transposer ===

Any of the four common seed types presses for the same amount:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Wheat Seeds;Beetroot Seeds;Melon Seeds;Pumpkin Seeds |FluidOut=Seed Oil |OutAmount=50
|ingredients=Wheat, beetroot, melon, or pumpkin seeds
|notes=1,600 RF}}

Twenty seeds fill one bucket's worth. Farming the seeds is the cheap part; the [[RF Energy|RF]] is not — a full bucket costs 32,000 RF to press.

== Usage ==

No Logistics machine or recipe consumes Seed Oil yet. It can be tanked, piped, and bucketed like any other fluid, and it is published as a plant oil for other mods, so a mod that processes plant oil can take it.

=== Bucket ===

The '''Seed Oil Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}. A [[Transposer]] fills and empties it for 800 RF a time, like every other fluid bucket.

== Mod compatibility ==

Seed Oil is published as <code>#c:plant_oil</code> and <code>#c:plantoil</code> — both spellings — so other mods that process plant oil can take it even though no Logistics recipe consumes it yet. See [[Mod Compatibility]].
