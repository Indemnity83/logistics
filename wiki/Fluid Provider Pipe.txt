The '''Fluid Provider Pipe''' is a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]] that mints [[Fluid Packet|fluid packets]] on demand from an adjacent tank, advertising its contents to the network the same way a [[Provider Logistics Pipe]] advertises an inventory.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|B1=Gold Gear
|A2=Bucket |B2=Basic Logistics Pipe |C2=Bucket
|Output=Fluid Provider Pipe
}}

Substitute a [[Gold Chipset]] for the [[Gold Gear]] to craft it with a [[Sequential Fabricator]].

== Usage ==

The Fluid Provider Pipe scans all six adjacent faces for a fluid tank and, several times a second, registers its contents with the network. When a [[Fluid Supplier Pipe|fluid supplier]] orders that fluid, the provider extracts it, splits it into physical '''fluid packets''' — hidden items that carry a fluid and amount through the ordinary item pipe network — and ships them toward the requester, just like any other item.

* Each dispatch caps every packet at '''5,000 mB'''; a delivery that doesn't divide evenly ships one smaller tail packet instead of leaving a remainder behind.
* Packets never stack — one physical item per packet, so fluid delivery is visible and paced rather than instantaneous.
* Dispatch is transactional: if the network can't afford the energy for a packet, that fluid is refunded to the tank rather than lost.

== Power ==

Like every [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]], it draws [[RF Energy|RF]] through a [[Power Junction]] — it renders '''green''' while powered and '''red''' when the network has no charge, in which case routing stops. Undeliverable [[Fluid Packet|fluid packets]] are voided rather than dropped as ground items. Minting a packet additionally costs a flat RF charge per packet, regardless of its size.
