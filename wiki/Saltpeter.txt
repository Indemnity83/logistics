'''Saltpeter''' is a material recovered from sandstone in a [[Macerator]] and dropped by the Breeze. It is an ingredient for [[#Usage|crafting gunpowder]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

Saltpeter drops as a 40% byproduct when grinding sandstone or red sandstone (and their slab, stairs, and wall variants) into sand in a [[Macerator]]:

{{Grinding|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Sandstone |ingredients=Sandstone (and variants) |Output=Sand,2 |Byproduct=Saltpeter |ByproductChance=40%}}

=== Mob loot ===

Breezes drop 0–2 Saltpeter upon death. The amount is increased by 1 per level of Looting, for a maximum of 0–5 with Looting III.

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Two Saltpeter combine with [[Coal Dust]] and [[Sulfur Dust]] to make two {{Mcw|Gunpowder|gunpowder}}:

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A1=Coal Dust |B1=Sulfur Dust
|A2=Saltpeter |B2=Saltpeter
|Output=Gunpowder,2
}}

== Mod compatibility ==

Saltpeter is published as <code>#c:dusts/saltpeter</code>, and the gunpowder recipe accepts that tag rather than this item specifically — so another mod's saltpeter works here, and ours works in its recipes. See [[Mod Compatibility]].
