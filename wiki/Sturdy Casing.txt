The '''Sturdy Casing''' is a reinforced shell formed from [[Bronze Ingot|bronze ingots]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A1=Bronze Ingot |B1=Bronze Ingot |C1=Bronze Ingot
|A2=Bronze Ingot |C2=Bronze Ingot
|A3=Bronze Ingot |B3=Bronze Ingot |C3=Bronze Ingot
|Output=Sturdy Casing
}}

== Usage ==

No current machine recipe uses the Sturdy Casing.

Bronze Ingots are an alloy of copper and tin, so keep a [[Tin Ore|tin]] supply on hand.
