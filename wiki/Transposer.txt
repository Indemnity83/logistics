The '''Transposer''' is a fluid ⇄ bucket station: it fills empty buckets from its internal tank and empties filled buckets into it. It runs on no power at all — just buckets in, buckets out.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Transposer is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

Drop an item into the input slot and the Transposer converts it in a single tick:

* An '''empty bucket''' plus at least 1,000 mB in the tank becomes a filled bucket of that fluid; the tank loses 1,000 mB.
* A '''filled bucket''' — water, lava, or any Logistics fluid bucket — plus room for 1,000 mB becomes a plain empty bucket; the tank gains 1,000 mB.

The conversion is atomic: if the output slot can't hold the result, nothing is consumed and the tank is untouched. The tank holds '''16,000 mB (16 buckets)''' by default. Pipe fluid in and out with a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]], and automate the item side with [[Item Pipes|item pipes]] or a hopper. Breaking the Transposer drops its slot contents but voids whatever fluid remains in the tank.
