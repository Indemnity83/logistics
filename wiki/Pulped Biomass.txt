'''Pulped Biomass''' is a mulch made by running plant matter through a [[Sawmill]]. It melts down into [[Liquid Biomass]] and, combined with [[Slag]], recycles back into dirt.

== Obtaining ==

=== Sawmill ===

The [[Sawmill]] pulps almost any plant block — grass, leaves, saplings, kelp, crops, mushrooms, flowers, cacti, and vines. Each recipe yields one Pulped Biomass; denser plants take more input:

{{Milling|head=1 |Input=Oak Leaves |ingredients=Leaves, saplings, kelp, moss, seagrass, sweet berries (×8) |Output=Pulped Biomass}}
{{Milling |Input=Sugar Cane |ingredients=Cactus, sugar cane, vines, tall grass, dried kelp block (×6) |Output=Pulped Biomass}}
{{Milling |foot=1 |Input=Wheat |ingredients=Crops, flowers, mushrooms, moss block, fern (×4) |Output=Pulped Biomass}}

== Usage ==

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] melts Pulped Biomass into [[Liquid Biomass]]:

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Pulped Biomass |Output=Liquid Biomass |Amount=100}}

=== Crafting ===

Pulped Biomass and [[Slag]] combine into dirt — a way to turn ore-processing waste back into something useful:

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1
|name=Dirt
|A1=Pulped Biomass |B1=Slag
|A2=Pulped Biomass |B2=Slag
|Output=Dirt}}
