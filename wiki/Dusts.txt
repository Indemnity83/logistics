'''Dusts''' are ground materials produced by the [[Macerator]]. Grinding a metal ore yields more dust than smelting the ore directly would, and each dust smelts back into one ingot — so grinding then smelting an ore '''doubles''' its metal. Gem and mineral dusts are crafting ingredients for [[Cores|cores]], [[Chips|chips]], and [[Modules|modules]].

Metal dusts also grind from the matching ingot, block, and raw item — not only the ore. '''Obsidian Dust''' and '''Netherite Dust''' are consumed by their [[Cores|cores]] but currently have no grinding recipe.

== Usage ==

=== Ore doubling ===

The main use of metal dusts is doubling ore output. Grinding one ore block produces '''two''' dust, and smelting each dust yields one ingot — twice what you get smelting the ore directly:

* '''Direct:''' Tin Ore → furnace → 1× [[Tin Ingot]]
* '''Doubled:''' Tin Ore → [[Macerator]] → 2× Tin Dust → furnace or [[Kiln]] → 2× [[Tin Ingot]]

Dust-to-ingot smelting grants no experience — the ore's smelting experience is collected only on the direct path.

=== Components ===

Gem and mineral dusts are crafting ingredients rather than smelting stock. They feed [[Cores|cores]], [[Chips|chips]], and [[Modules|modules]] — for example a [[Cores|Diamond Core]] from Diamond Dust, or the carbon [[Chips]] line from Coal Dust. [[Cores#Quartz Crystal|Quartz Crystal]] is the exception that is smelted, from Quartz Dust.

The [[Macerator]] also produces non-dust ground products such as Wood Pulp and Flour; see its page for the full output list.
