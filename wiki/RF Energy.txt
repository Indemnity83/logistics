'''RF (Redstone Flux)''' is the energy system used by Logistics. [[Engines]] generate RF, [[Copper Cable|cables]] distribute it, and machines and the logistics network consume it to operate.

There are '''two separate power paths''' in the mod — they do not share a power source.

== Powering the logistics network ==

The logistics network draws power through a [[Power Junction]], not directly off an engine or battery:

# An [[Engines|engine]] ([[Redstone Engine]], [[Stirling Engine]], or [[Creative Engine]]) generates RF
# [[Copper Cable|Power cables]] carry that RF — optionally by way of a [[Battery]] — to a [[Power Junction]]
# The Power Junction is placed against the network, and the [[Logistics Pipes]] and chassis modules draw RF from it each time they route, supply, or craft

When the network runs out of power, routing, supplying, and crafting all stop, and items already in transit are '''dropped'''. Logistics pipes render '''green when powered''' and '''red when not''', so you can read network status at a glance. Keep the Power Junction fed and power is restored at once.

== Pacing extraction ==

Extraction pipes do '''not''' use the Battery. Instead, each is paced by an '''adjacent engine''':

* The [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] pull faster the more RF the adjacent engine supplies
* This is the original, mechanical power model — the engine touches the pipe directly
* No Battery or logistics network is involved

== Cables ==

Cables carry RF from an engine to a [[Battery]], [[Power Junction]], or machine. They connect to each other and to adjacent power blocks, and differ only in throughput — use a higher-throughput cable to feed a high-demand network:

* '''[[Copper Cable]]''' — 30 RF/t
* '''[[Gold Cable]]''' — 60 RF/t
* '''[[Ender Cable]]''' — 120 RF/t

== Engines ==

Every engine needs a {{Mcw|Redstone|redstone}} signal to run. They differ in output and fuel:

'''[[Redstone Engine]]'''
* Small, steady output
* Powered by a redstone signal only
* Cannot overheat — good for low-power needs

'''[[Stirling Engine]]'''
* Substantial output
* Powered by fuel ({{Mcw|Coal|coal}}, {{Mcw|Charcoal|charcoal}}, {{Mcw|Lava|lava}}, etc.)
* Can overheat if run too long — good for high-power machines like quarries

'''[[Creative Engine]]'''
* Infinite output for testing and creative mode
* Never runs out of fuel, never overheats
* Not craftable
