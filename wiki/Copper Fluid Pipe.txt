The '''Copper Fluid Pipe''' is the standard passive [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] — 10 mB/t. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Copper Transport Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Seal the [[Copper Transport Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime ball}} or [[Tar]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|showname=0
|A1=Copper Transport Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Copper Fluid Pipe
}}
{{Crafting
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Copper Transport Pipe |B1=Tar
|Output=Copper Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

Copper fluid pipes passively carry fluid at '''10 mB/t''' and form most of a fluid line's length. They oxidize cosmetically like vanilla copper, with no effect on transport.
