==About Logistics==

'''Logistics''' is inspired by the classic pipe-and-machine style of Minecraft automation mods, rebuilt as a modern standalone mod. It adds transport pipes, fluids, tanks, power systems, machines, and tools for moving resources around a world.

Supports '''Minecraft 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1, and 26.2''' on '''Fabric & NeoForge'''. Developed by '''Indemnity83'''.
