'''Block of Apatite''' is a storage block that compacts nine [[Apatite (gem)|apatite]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Craft from nine [[Apatite (gem)|apatite]] arranged in a 3×3 grid; the block unpacks back into nine.
