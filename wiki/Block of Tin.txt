'''Block of Tin''' is a storage block that compacts nine [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Craft from nine [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]] arranged in a 3×3 grid; the block unpacks back into nine ingots.
