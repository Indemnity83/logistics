The '''Power Junction''' is the single point where electrical power enters a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]. [[Cables]], engines, and [[Batteries|batteries]] feed RF into the junction, and the network draws what it needs out of the junction's buffer. A network with no Power Junction has no power; a [[Batteries|battery]] placed directly on the pipes does not feed them.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Power Junction is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

Place the Power Junction so it touches both your power grid and your [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]. It buffers the two sides:

* Holds up to '''1,000,000 RF'''.
* Accepts up to '''128 RF/t''' from adjacent [[Cables]], [[Engines]], or a [[Batteries|battery]]. This is the rate that matters when sizing a grid — how fast a junction fills is what the network's throughput is built around.
* Lets the network draw on the whole buffer in a single tick; there is no rate cap on the way out, so a burst of routing or crafting can spend the full million.

RF that has entered the junction belongs to the logistics network. The junction accepts power from the world but never gives it back, so a [[Cables|cable]] cannot pull that charge out again into a [[Batteries|battery]] or machine.

The front face shows a charge indicator that fills as the buffer charges. Run your power into a junction, and the junction into the network — without one, the network cannot be powered.
