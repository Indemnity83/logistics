# Tin Valve

The **Tin Valve** is a Tier 1 valve crafted in the [Kiln](../automation/kiln.md). It requires coal fuel and [tin ingots](tin-ingot.md).

## Recipe
**[Kiln](../automation/kiln.md) crafting:**
- 5× [Tin Ingot](tin-ingot.md)
- 2× Redstone
- 250mb Molten Glass
- **Process time:** 100 ticks
- **Energy demand:** 60 energy/tick
- **Yields:** 4× Tin Valve

**Pattern:**
```
 T
RTR
TTT
```
T = Tin Ingot, R = Redstone

## Fuel Requirements

**Tier 1 - Coal sufficient**

Energy demand: 60 energy/tick
- **Wood:** Fails (too weak)
- **Coal:** Works well
- **Blaze Rod:** Overkill but works
- **Lava Bucket:** Overkill but works

Coal recommended.

## Usage

Valves are crafting components for future features. Tin valves require mining [tin ore](tin-ore.md) first.

## Tips

- Requires tin ingots from smelted tin ore
- Coal is sufficient fuel
- Yields 4 valves per craft
- Slightly higher energy than copper valve (60 vs 40)
