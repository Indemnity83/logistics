'''Rubber''' is a flexible crafting component used to insulate [[Cables|power cables]]. It is smelted in an [[Alloy Smelter]] from a [[Natural Polymer]] or [[Synthetic Polymer]] together with [[Sulfur Dust]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Alloy Smelter ===

Smelting either polymer with [[Sulfur Dust]] yields '''two''' Rubber:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1 |Input=Natural Polymer |Input2=Sulfur Dust |Output=Rubber |OutputCount=2}}
{{Alloy Smelter|foot=1 |Input=Synthetic Polymer |Input2=Sulfur Dust |Output=Rubber |OutputCount=2}}

The two polymers give a renewable ([[Natural Polymer|slime-based]]) and a mineral ([[Synthetic Polymer|tar-based]]) route to the same Rubber.

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Rubber insulates every [[Cables|power cable]] — six pieces wrapped around a conductive core, crafted '''8 at a time''':

{{Crafting|showdescription=1
|name=[[Cables|Conductive Cable]] |A1=Rubber |B1=Rubber |C1=Rubber |A2=Caterium Ingot |B2=Caterium Ingot |C2=Caterium Ingot |A3=Rubber |B3=Rubber |C3=Rubber |Output=Conductive Cable,8 |description=60 RF/t.}}
{{Crafting|showdescription=1

{{Crafting|foot=1|showdescription=1
|name=[[Cables|Deep Cable]] |A1=Rubber |B1=Rubber |C1=Rubber |A2=Echonite Ingot |B2=Echonite Ingot |C2=Echonite Ingot |A3=Rubber |B3=Rubber |C3=Rubber |Output=Deep Cable,8 |description=240 RF/t — the highest throughput.}}

== Mod compatibility ==

Rubber is published as <code>#c:ingots/rubber</code>, and the [[Cables|cable]] crafts read that tag rather than this item — so rubber from [https://modrinth.com/mod/techreborn Tech Reborn] or [https://modrinth.com/mod/create-tfmg Create: The Factory Must Grow] insulates a cable just as well, and Logistics rubber works in their recipes. See [[Mod Compatibility]].
