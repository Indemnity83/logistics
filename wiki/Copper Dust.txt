'''Copper Dust''' is ground from copper ore (or a copper ingot or block) in a [[Macerator]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0

}}

{{Grinding|showname=0

}}

{{Grinding|showname=0|foot=1

|Output=Copper Dust,1
}}

== Usage ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt it in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]]:

{{Smelting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Copper Dust
|Output=Copper Ingot
}}

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1
|name=[[Bronze Dust]]

}}

=== Alloy smelting ===

With sand as flux the [[Alloy Smelter]] returns the ingot directly, with a chance of [[Slag]]; paired with tin it alloys straight to bronze:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1 |Input=Copper Dust |Input2=Sand;Red Sand |Output=Copper Ingot |Byproduct=Slag |ByproductChance=25%}}
{{Alloy Smelter|foot=1 |Input=Copper Dust |InputCount=3 |Input2=Tin Dust |Output=Bronze Ingot |OutputCount=4}}
