'''Sulfur Dust''' is ground from sulfur in a [[Macerator]]. It is an ingredient for [[#Usage|crafting gunpowder]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] grinds sulfur and its variants into Sulfur Dust ''(Minecraft 26.2 and newer)''. Higher-volume blocks yield more dust:

{{Grinding|head=1 |Input=Sulfur |Output=Sulfur Dust,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Polished Sulfur |Output=Sulfur Dust,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Sulfur Stairs |Output=Sulfur Dust,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Sulfur Wall |Output=Sulfur Dust,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Sulfur Slab |Output=Sulfur Dust,2}}
{{Grinding |foot=1 |Input=Sulfur Spike |Output=Sulfur Dust}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Sulfur Dust combines with [[Coal Dust]] and [[Niter]] to make gunpowder:

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A1=Coal Dust |B1=Sulfur Dust
|A2=Niter |B2=Niter
|Output=Gunpowder
}}
