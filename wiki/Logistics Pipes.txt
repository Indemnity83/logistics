'''Logistics Pipes''' are item pipes that join a logistics '''network''': instead of pushing items along at random, they advertise stock, place and fulfill requests, automate crafting, and keep inventories topped up. Every logistics pipe is built on the [[Basic Logistics Pipe]], draws [[#Power|power]] through a [[Power Junction]], and connects to the rest of a network through ordinary [[Transport Pipes]].

== Power ==

Logistics pipes are powered. The network draws [[RF Energy|RF]] through a [[Power Junction]]; while powered a pipe renders '''green''', and with no charge it renders '''red''', the network stops routing, and items in transit drop. A single Power Junction fed by an engine or [[Battery]] keeps every logistics pipe running.

== Usage ==

A logistics network is formed by connecting logistics pipes (through [[Transport Pipes]]) and giving it power. [[Provider Logistics Pipe|Providers]] advertise what's available, [[Requester Logistics Pipe|requesters]] and [[Supplier Logistics Pipe|suppliers]] ask for items, and the network uses pathfinding to route each item from a provider to its destination. The modular [[Chassis Logistics Pipe]] takes its behavior from installed [[Modules]] instead.
