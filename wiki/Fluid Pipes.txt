'''Fluid Pipes''' move liquids through a '''standalone''' fluid system, separate from the item-carrying [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]. Fluids never join the item [[Pipe Networks|network graph]] — they get their own local transport, extraction, and storage. Each fluid pipe is the liquid counterpart of an item [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]], crafted from one by combining it with a Slime Ball.

== Usage ==

* '''One fluid per line''' — each connected run carries a single fluid type and won't accept another until it drains (no mixing).
* '''Pressure equalization''' — fluid flows from fuller pipes toward emptier ones and toward sinks, capped each tick by the pipe's transfer rate.
* '''Passive transport is free''' — the stone, copper, golden, insertion, merger, bypass, and void pipes move fluid with no power. Only active extraction (the [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] and the [[Fluid Pump|Pump]]) costs energy.
* Works with any mod's fluid storage on both Fabric and NeoForge.
