'''Apatite''' is a gem obtained from mining [[Apatite Ore|apatite ore]]. It's used for apatite valves. It can be compacted into a '''Block of Apatite''' or ground into '''Apatite Dust''' in the [[Macerator]].

==Obtaining==

'''Mining:'''
* Mine [[Apatite Ore]]
* Drops 1× Apatite (more with Fortune)

'''Crafting (from block):'''

==Usage==

'''Valves:'''
* [[Materials#Apatite Valve|Apatite Valve]] - via an Apatite [[Cores|Core]]

'''Dust:'''
* Grind [[Apatite Ore]] in the [[Macerator]] → Apatite Dust (see [[Dusts]])

'''Storage:'''
* 9× Apatite → Block of Apatite

==Related Items==

* '''Block of Apatite''' - Compact storage (9 gems)
* '''Apatite Dust''' - Ground form from the [[Macerator]] (see [[Dusts]])

==Tips==

* Gem-type material (like diamond/emerald)
* Fortune enchantment increases drops
* Needed for Tier 3 valves
* Requires premium fuel (blaze rod/lava) to craft valve
