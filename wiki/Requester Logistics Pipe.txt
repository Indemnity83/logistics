The '''Requester Logistics Pipe''' is a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]] that pulls items from the network by hand.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Diamond Valve |B1=Basic Logistics Pipe |C1=Diamond Valve
|Output=Requester Logistics Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

Open the Requester Logistics Pipe with a [[Wrench]] to browse every item available across all [[Provider Logistics Pipe|providers]] (searchable, paginated), pick an item and quantity, and the network delivers it into the adjacent inventory. For recurring needs, use a [[Supplier Logistics Pipe]] instead.

== Power ==

Like every [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]], it draws [[RF Energy|RF]] through a [[Power Junction]] — it renders '''green''' while powered and '''red''' when the network has no charge, in which case routing stops and items in transit drop.
