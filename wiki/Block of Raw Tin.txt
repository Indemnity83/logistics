A '''block of raw tin''' is a storage block equivalent to nine [[Raw Tin|raw tin]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

A block of raw tin can be mined only with a stone pickaxe or better. Mined without a pickaxe it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

A block of raw tin stores raw tin compactly. It can be unpacked back into nine at any time.
