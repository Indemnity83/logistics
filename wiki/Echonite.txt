'''Echonite''' is an infused alloy — a dark teal metal with no ore of its own. It is made by infusing [[Echo Dust|echo dust]] with [[Liquid Ender]] in a [[Transposer]], then smelting the result. Echonite exists to build the '''deep''' rung of the power ladder: the [[Batteries|Deep Battery]] and the [[Cables|Deep Cable]].
