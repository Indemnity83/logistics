The '''Alloy Smelter''' is an RF-powered machine with '''two''' input slots. It combines an ore or metal with a flux to smelt alloys, double ore output, and recycle worked metal items back into ingots — producing [[Slag]] and [[Rich Slag]] as byproducts.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Alloy Smelter is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

Shares the standard machine base — a [[Machine Frame]], a [[Redstone Reception Coil]], and two [[Copper Gear|copper gears]] — with a [[Diamond Gear]] and sand on top.

== Usage ==

Place two inputs in the top slots — typically an ore or metal plus a flux such as sand. The primary output is drawn from the bottom, and a second slot catches the [[Slag]] or [[Rich Slag]] byproduct. The two inputs are order-independent, and every recipe appears in the Just Enough Items (JEI) viewer.

=== Power ===

The Alloy Smelter runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 10,000 RF and accepts up to 128 RF/t, spending 20 RF/t while working. Each recipe carries its own RF cost; connect a [[Stirling Engine]] or any RF source.

=== Recycling ===

Worked metal items smelt back into their base ingots (plus a chance of [[Slag]]), using sand as flux — anvils, cauldrons, hoppers, minecarts, rails, and full sets of iron and gold tools and armor:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1 |Input=Anvil |Input2=Sand;Red Sand |Output=Iron Ingot |OutputCount=31 |Byproduct=Slag |ByproductChance=50%}}
{{Alloy Smelter |Input=Hopper |Input2=Sand;Red Sand |Output=Iron Ingot |OutputCount=5 |Byproduct=Slag |ByproductChance=25%}}
{{Alloy Smelter |foot=1 |Input=Iron Pickaxe |ingredients=[[Iron Pickaxe]] (and other iron tools) + Sand |Input2=Sand;Red Sand |Output=Iron Ingot |Byproduct=Slag |ByproductChance=10%}}

The full recycling list — every anvil tier, minecart, pressure plate, and iron or gold tool and armor piece — is browsable in JEI.
