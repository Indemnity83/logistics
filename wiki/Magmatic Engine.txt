The '''Magmatic Engine''' burns liquid {{Mcw|Lava|lava}} as a heat source to generate [[RF Energy|RF]]. It runs hotter and more efficiently the longer it stays fed, so a continuous lava supply gets the most out of it.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Magmatic Engine is mined with an iron pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

Pipe {{Mcw|Lava|lava}} into the engine's tank (4,000 mB) with a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] — or feed it from a [[Crucible]]. It draws lava in small batches and heat-soaks as it runs: a cold engine generates about '''5 RF/t''', a warmed one '''10 RF/t''', and a fully heat-soaked one up to '''15 RF/t'''. A bucket of lava (1,000 mB) lasts roughly 20,000 powered ticks. Output flows from a configurable face; if the buffer fills, excess generation is wasted. A [[Jade]] readout shows its temperature, output, and lava level.

Its fuels are listed in the Just Enough Items (JEI) recipe browser, with the burn time and RF/t each one yields.

=== Configuration ===

Use a [[Wrench]] to rotate the output face.
