== Trivia ==

* Tin and copper make [[Bronze Ingot|bronze]], echoing the real-world Bronze Age — when smiths first alloyed tin into copper for tools harder than copper alone. The mod's copper-heavy bronze recipe (three parts copper to one part tin) is even roughly true to real bronze, which is mostly copper with a smaller share of tin.
