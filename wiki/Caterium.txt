'''Caterium''' is an infused alloy — a warm orange metal with no ore of its own. It is made by infusing [[Gold Dust|gold dust]] with [[Liquid Redstone]] in a [[Transposer]], then smelting the result. Caterium exists to build the '''conductive''' rung of the power ladder: the [[Batteries|Conductive Battery]] and the [[Cables|Conductive Cable]].
