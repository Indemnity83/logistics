'''Item Pipes''' are the [[Tier System|Tier 1]] and [[Tier System|Tier 2]] pipes that move items through a Logistics network. Tier 1 pipes perform mechanical operations without inspecting items, while Tier 2 pipes inspect items to make routing decisions.

For the complete list of every pipe type, including the Tier 3 network logistics pipes, see [[Pipes]].

==Tier 1: Mechanical Pipes==

Basic pipes that move, merge, accelerate, or remove items without item awareness.

* '''[[Stone Transport Pipe]]''' - Very slow transport; cheap early-game pipe.
* '''[[Copper Transport Pipe]]''' - Standard transport backbone at normal speed.
* '''[[Item Extractor Pipe]]''' - Actively pulls items from adjacent inventories into the network.
* '''[[Item Merger Pipe]]''' - Converges multiple inputs into a single guaranteed output direction.
* '''[[Golden Transport Pipe]]''' - Accelerates items when supplied with redstone power.
* '''[[Item Passthrough Pipe]]''' - Carries items past inventories without inserting into them.
* '''[[Item Void Pipe]]''' - Deletes items that enter it.

==Tier 2: Smart Pipes==

Item-aware pipes that inspect contents and route accordingly.

* '''[[Item Filter Pipe]]''' - Routes items by type to different destinations.
* '''[[Item Insertion Pipe]]''' - Prefers inserting into inventories that have space.
