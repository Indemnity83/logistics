The '''copper nugget''' Logistics recipes call for is the {{Mcw|Copper Nugget|vanilla item}}. Logistics added a nugget of its own in v0.8.3, before vanilla had one; since v0.8.12 it uses vanilla's instead and publishes it as <code>#c:nuggets/copper</code>, so any mod's copper nugget works in its recipes. Existing stacks of the old Logistics nugget convert to the vanilla one automatically.

On '''Minecraft 1.21.1''' the mod still adds its own nugget, because vanilla has none at that version. Everything below reads the same either way.
