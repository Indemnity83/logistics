Renders the chest-loot table for an item (Item / Structure / Container / Quantity / Chance),
powered by [[Module:LootChest]]. The mod adds the item to vanilla structure chests; the data and
drop chances live in the module.

Usage:
 {{LootChestItem|tin-ingot}}

Known item slugs: raw-tin, tin-ingot, tin-nugget, bronze-ingot, bronze-nugget,
bronze-gear, apatite.

Chance is the item's weight divided by its pool's total weight (each added pool rolls once).
