The '''Insertion Fluid Pipe''' is a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that fills adjacent tanks and machines first. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Item Insertion Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Seal the [[Item Insertion Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime ball}} or [[Tar]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Insertion Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Insertion Fluid Pipe
}}
{{Crafting
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Insertion Pipe |B1=Tar
|Output=Insertion Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

The Insertion Fluid Pipe fills adjacent tanks and machines before passing fluid further down the line. Chain several to top off a row of tanks in sequence.

It also fills a {{Mcw|Cauldron|cauldron}}, which accepts fluid a whole level at a time — 1,000 mB for lava, 333⅓ mB per water level.
