'''Echonite ingots''' are the infused alloy behind the '''deep''' power tier — the [[Cables|Deep Cable]] is wound from them, and the [[Batteries|Deep Battery]] is built on a block of them. They are smelted from [[Echonite Dust]]; there is no ore.

== Obtaining ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt [[Echonite Dust]] in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]] (RF-powered, no fuel):
