The '''Copper Fluid Pipe''' is a standard-speed [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that carries liquids between [[Glass Tank|tanks]], [[Pump|pumps]], and machines. It is the backbone of most fluid networks, the fluid-side counterpart to the [[Copper Transport Pipe]].

Fluid pipes form a '''standalone''' transport system, separate from the item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]: each connected line carries one fluid type and moves it by pressure equalization. Passive transport through stone, copper, and golden fluid pipes is free — no power required.

'''Yields:''' 1× Copper Fluid Pipe (shapeless: any [[Copper Transport Pipe]] + Slime Ball)

==Behavior==

* Transfers fluid between adjacent tanks, machines, and other fluid pipes at '''10 mB/t'''
* Carries '''one fluid type''' per connected line
* Passive transport is '''free''' (no RF)
* Connects automatically to fluid handlers on every face; use a [[Bypass Fluid Pipe]] to connect only to pipes
* Works with any mod's fluid storage on both Fabric and NeoForge
* Like vanilla copper, the pipe weathers through exposed/weathered/oxidized stages; wax it with honeycomb to stop oxidation

==Tips==
* Pair with a [[Pump]] to pull fluid sources from the world, or a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] to draw from machine tanks
* Use a [[Golden Fluid Pipe]] (80 mB/t) for high-throughput lines and a [[Stone Fluid Pipe]] (10 mB/t) as a cheap alternative
* Store buffered fluid in a [[Glass Tank]]; stack tanks vertically to grow the reservoir
