'''Sawdust''' is a wood material produced by grinding wood in a [[Macerator]] or as a byproduct of the [[Sawmill]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] grinds wood directly into Sawdust:

{{Grinding|head=1 |Input=Oak Log |ingredients=Logs (any type) |Output=Sawdust,8}}
{{Grinding |Input=Oak Planks |ingredients=Planks (any type) |Output=Sawdust,2}}
{{Grinding |foot=1 |Input=Wooden Pickaxe |ingredients=Wooden tools (axe, hoe, pickaxe, shovel, sword) |Output=Sawdust}}

=== Sawmill ===

Every [[Sawmill]] recipe also drops Sawdust as a byproduct — guaranteed from logs, at a lower chance from processed items:

{{Milling|head=1 |Input=Oak Log |ingredients=Logs / Bamboo blocks (any type) |Output=Oak Planks,6 |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=100%}}
{{Milling |Input=Oak Boat |ingredients=Boats |Output=Oak Planks,4 |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=125%}}
{{Milling |Input=Oak Door |ingredients=Doors |Output=Oak Planks |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=50%}}
{{Milling |foot=1 |Input=Oak Stairs |ingredients=Stairs / Fences / Fence gates |Output=Oak Planks |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=25%}}

== Usage ==

Sawdust has no crafting, smelting, or machine recipes — nothing currently consumes it.
