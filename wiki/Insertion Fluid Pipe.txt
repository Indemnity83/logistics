The '''Insertion Fluid Pipe''' prefers to fill adjacent tanks and machines, only passing fluid further down the line once they are full. It is the fluid-side counterpart to the [[Item Insertion Pipe]].

Fluid pipes form a '''standalone''' transport system, separate from the item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]: each connected line carries one fluid type and moves it by pressure equalization. Passive transport is free — no power required.

'''Yields:''' 1× Insertion Fluid Pipe (shapeless: [[Item Insertion Pipe]] + Slime Ball)

==Behavior==

* '''Fills adjacent tanks and machines first''', passing fluid on only when they are full
* Connected fluid handlers take priority over downstream pipes — fluid prefers to "top off" a neighbor before continuing
* Passive transport is '''free''' (no RF)
* Carries '''one fluid type''' per connected line

==Tips==
* Use to guarantee a machine or [[Glass Tank]] is kept full before any overflow continues down the line
* Chain insertion pipes to fill a row of tanks in sequence, sending the surplus onward only when each is topped off
* Combine with a [[Void Fluid Pipe]] at the end of the line to safely discard any overflow
