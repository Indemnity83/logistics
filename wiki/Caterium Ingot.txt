'''Caterium ingots''' are the infused alloy behind the '''conductive''' power tier — the [[Cables|Conductive Cable]] is wound from them, and the [[Batteries|Conductive Battery]] is built on a block of them. They are smelted from [[Caterium Dust]]; there is no ore.

=== Smelting ===
