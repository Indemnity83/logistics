The '''Netherite Gear''' is an end-tier crafting component made from netherite ingots. It represents the highest tier of crafting materials.

==Recipe==

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B1=Netherite Ingot
|A2=Netherite Ingot |B2=Diamond Gear |C2=Netherite Ingot
|B3=Netherite Ingot
|Output=Netherite Gear
}}

'''Yields:''' 1× Netherite Gear (requires [[Diamond Gear]])

==Uses==

Netherite Gears are crafting components for end-game machines and engines (specific uses TBD).

==Progression==

Netherite Gears represent the highest tier of crafting materials:
* [[Wooden Gear]] → Basic tier
* [[Stone Gear]] → Stone tier
* [[Iron Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Copper Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Tin Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Bronze Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Gold Gear]] → Advanced tier
* [[Diamond Gear]] → Advanced tier
* '''Netherite Gear''' → End tier (this item, maximum)

==Tips==

* Most expensive gear - requires netherite ingots
* End-game crafting tier
* 1:1 netherite-to-gear ratio (4 ingots = 1 gear)
* Plan netherite usage carefully
