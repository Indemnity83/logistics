'''Lumenite ingots''' are the infused alloy behind the '''resonant''' power tier — the [[Cables|Resonant Cable]] is wound from them, and the [[Batteries|Resonant Battery]] is built on a block of them. They are smelted from [[Lumenite Dust]]; there is no ore.

== Obtaining ==

=== Smelting ===
