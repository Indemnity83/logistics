'''Cables''' carry [[RF Energy|RF]] power between [[Redstone Engine|engines]], a [[Battery]], and machines, so power can be routed across a distance instead of relying on a direct face-to-face connection. They come in three tiers that behave identically apart from how much RF they move per tick.

== Variants ==

All cables are crafted '''8 at a time''' from [[Rubber|Rubber Chunks]] wrapped around a conductive core:

== Usage ==

Cables connect to each other and to adjacent power blocks, forming a power network that carries RF from an [[Redstone Engine|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or machine. Each tier moves more RF per tick — '''Copper''' 30, '''Gold''' 60, '''Ender''' 120 — but the slowest cable on a run caps its throughput, so match the cable to the demand. Use Copper for short engine-to-battery hops and Ender for long runs or high-demand batteries.
