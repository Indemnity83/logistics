Renders a furnace-style smelting recipe: the input on top, a flame, the fuel slot below, an arrow,
and the output with its experience. Styling reuses [[Template:Grid Crafting Table/styles.css]] for
the slots and arrow, plus [[Template:Grid Smelting Table/styles.css]] for the flame and XP.

Usage:
 {{Grid Smelting Table
 |Input=Raw Tin
 |Output=Tin Ingot
 |XP=0.7
 }}

Parameters:
 Input       — the item being smelted (rendered via {{Grid}})
 Fuel        — the fuel item (optional; the slot is left blank if omitted — "any fuel")
 Output      — the resulting item
 OutputCount — quantity produced (optional)
 XP          — experience granted (optional)

Depends on [[Template:Grid]].
