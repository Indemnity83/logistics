The '''Pump''' (also called the '''Fluid Pump''') extracts fluid source blocks from the world and feeds them into an adjacent [[Glass Tank|tank]] or [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]]. It is the simplest way to get water or lava into your network.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Pump is mined with a pickaxe; without one it drops nothing.

Uses an [[Iron Gear]], a Bucket, two [[Glass Tank|Glass Tanks]], Iron Ingots, and Redstone.

== Usage ==

The Pump drains fluid source blocks from the world below it into an internal buffer and outputs them to an adjacent tank or fluid pipe. It works with any vanilla or modded fluid source on both Fabric and NeoForge.

* Holds a '''16,000 mB (16 buckets)''' internal buffer
* Outputs up to '''62.5 mB/t''' into an adjacent tank or fluid pipe
* Consumes '''100 RF''' per source block drained — no power, no pumping

=== Power ===

The Pump runs on [[RF Energy|RF]] and has its own internal buffer — it does '''not''' need an engine pressed against it the way a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] does. Supply it with RF from your power system to keep it draining; with no power it stops.

=== Tips ===

* Place a [[Glass Tank]] or [[Copper Fluid Pipe]] against the pump's output to carry away what it draws
* Buffer the output in stacked [[Glass Tank|tanks]] so the pump can keep draining even when the network is briefly full
* Use a [[Golden Fluid Pipe]] (80 mB/t) on the output if you need to move fluid faster than one pump produces
* To pull fluid out of a machine's tank instead of the world, use a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]
