The '''Merger Fluid Pipe''' is a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that acts as a one-way check valve. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Item Merger Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Seal the [[Item Merger Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime ball}} or [[Tar]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Merger Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Merger Fluid Pipe
}}
{{Crafting
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Merger Pipe |B1=Tar
|Output=Merger Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

The Merger Fluid Pipe lets fluid leave only through a wrench-set output face, and never flow back in. Use it to converge several input lines into one one-way output, or at a tank's input so fluid never drains back out.
