The '''Item Passthrough Pipe''' is a [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] that connects only to pipes, never inventories.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of {{Mcw|Glass}} flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Sandstone |B2=Glass |C2=Sandstone
|Output=Item Passthrough Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

The Item Passthrough Pipe connects only to other pipes, never to adjacent inventories. Use it to run a line directly past chests, furnaces, or other storage without inserting into them.
