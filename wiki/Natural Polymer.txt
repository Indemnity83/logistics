'''Natural Polymer''' is a crafting component made from a {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime ball}} and [[Coal Dust]]. It is smelted with [[Sulfur Dust]] in an [[Alloy Smelter]] to make [[Rubber]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A2=Slime Ball |B2=Coal Dust
|Output=Natural Polymer
}}

== Usage ==

=== Alloy Smelter ===

Smelt a Natural Polymer with [[Sulfur Dust]] to make '''two''' [[Rubber]]:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1|foot=1
|name=[[Rubber]]
|Input=Natural Polymer |Input2=Sulfur Dust |Output=Rubber |OutputCount=2
}}
