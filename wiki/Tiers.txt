'''Tiers''' are the named steps that power components are built in. A higher tier holds or moves more; otherwise every tier of a component behaves the same. Logistics pipes and modules are improved in '''marks''' instead, which are not tiers.

== Energy tiers ==

[[Cables]] and [[Batteries]] share four energy tiers, lowest first:

| Deep || [[Deep Cable]] || [[Deep Battery]]

A cable's tier sets how much RF it moves per tick; a battery's tier sets how much it stores and how fast it charges and discharges. The slowest cable on a run sets that run's throughput.

== Marks ==

A '''mark''' is a numbered variant of a chassis or module (MkI, MkII, MkIII…) that raises its limits:

* [[Chassis Logistics Pipe]] — MkI to MkV, one more module slot per mark
* [[Extractor Module]] and [[Crafter Module]] — up to MkIII
* [[Provider Module]] — up to MkII
