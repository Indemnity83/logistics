'''Bitumen''' is a solid oil product ground out of oil-bearing blocks in a [[Macerator]]. It melts down into [[Crude Oil]] and burns as a long-lasting furnace fuel.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] grinds the oil blocks into three Bitumen apiece, with a chance of a secondary drop — [[Tar]] from the sands, flint from shale:

{{Grinding|head=1 |Input=Oil Sand;Oil Red Sand |ingredients=[[Oil Sand]] or [[Oil Red Sand]] |Output=Bitumen,3 |Byproduct=Tar |ByproductChance=50%}}
{{Grinding |foot=1 |Input=Oil Shale |Output=Bitumen,3 |Byproduct=Flint |ByproductChance=50%}}

== Usage ==

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] melts Bitumen into [[Crude Oil]]:

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Bitumen |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=250}}

=== Fuel ===

Bitumen is a furnace fuel and powers the [[Stirling Engine]]. Each piece burns for 3,200 ticks — twice as long as coal — smelting 16 items.
