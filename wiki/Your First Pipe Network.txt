Learn the basics by building a simple pipe network that extracts items from a chest and delivers them to another chest.

==What You'll Build==

A basic transport system:

<pre>
[Source Chest] → [Extractor] → [Copper Pipes] → [Destination Chest]
</pre>

This teaches the fundamentals: extraction, transport, and insertion.

==Materials Needed==

Craft these items first:

'''Pipes:'''
* 8× [[Copper Transport Pipe]] (copper ingot + glass)
* 8× [[Item Extractor Pipe]] (planks + glass)

'''Power:'''
* 1× [[Redstone Engine]]
* 1× Redstone dust (to activate the engine)

'''Tool:'''
* 1× [[Wrench]] (4× iron ingot)

'''Test setup:'''
* 2× Chest (source and destination)
* Some items to transport

==Step-by-Step Build==

===1. Place Source Chest===
Place a chest and fill it with some items (cobblestone, dirt, anything).

===2. Place Extractor Pipe===
Place an [[Item Extractor Pipe]] directly next to the chest. You should see it connect automatically (opaque connector appears).

===3. Power the Extractor===

The Item Extractor Pipe requires energy to run. Place a [[Redstone Engine]] directly adjacent to the extractor pipe, then place a redstone dust on top of (or next to) the engine to activate it. The engine's output face must touch the extractor pipe — use a [[Wrench]] to rotate it if needed.

===4. Configure Extraction Face===
# Take out your [[Wrench]]
# Right-click the extractor pipe
# Watch the opaque connector - it shows which face extracts
# Keep right-clicking until the connector points toward the chest
# Now the extractor will pull from that chest

===5. Build Transport Line===
Place [[Copper Transport Pipe|Copper Transport Pipes]] extending from the extractor:

* Connect to any free face of the extractor
* Extend as far as you want (3-5 blocks for this example)
* Pipes connect automatically to each other

===6. Place Destination Chest===
Place a second chest at the end of your copper pipe line. The pipe should automatically connect to it (you'll see a connector appear).

===7. Watch It Work===
Items automatically extract from the source chest, travel through the pipes (you can see them moving!), and enter the destination chest.

'''If items aren't moving:'''
* Check that a [[Redstone Engine]] is adjacent to the extractor with its output face touching it (use wrench to rotate)
* Check the engine is powered (redstone signal on it)
* Check extraction face points at source chest (use wrench on extractor)
* Verify pipes connect to both chests (connectors visible)
* Ensure source chest has items

==What You Learned==

'''Automatic connection:'''
* Pipes connect to adjacent pipes and inventories automatically
* Visual connectors show connections

'''Extraction:'''
* [[Item Extractor Pipe|Item Extractor Pipes]] pull from inventories
* Configure extraction face with [[Wrench]]
* Only one face extracts at a time

'''Transport:'''
* [[Copper Transport Pipe|Copper Transport Pipes]] move items
* Items visible while traveling
* Random routing at junctions (we'll address this next)

'''Insertion:'''
* Items automatically try to enter adjacent inventories
* Works with any inventory (chests, furnaces, hoppers, etc.)

==Next Steps==

===Controlled Routing===
Random routing is fine for single destinations, but what about multiple chests?

Use [[Item Merger Pipe]] to guarantee direction:

<pre>
[Source A] ↘
           [Item Merger] → [Guaranteed path to destination]
[Source B] ↗
</pre>

===Sorting Items===
Want specific items to go to specific chests?

Use [[Item Filter Pipe]]:

<pre>
[Mixed items] → [Filter] → [Ores] → [Ore chest]
                    ↓
              [Everything else] → [General chest]
</pre>

===Speed Up Transport===
Use [[Golden Transport Pipe]] with redstone power to accelerate items.

==Common Mistakes==

'''Items drop instead of entering destination:'''
* Destination chest might be full
* Pipe might not be connected (no connector visible)
* Use [[Item Insertion Pipe]] to prefer inventories

'''Extraction face pointing wrong way:'''
* Right-click with wrench to cycle faces
* Opaque connector shows active face
* Should point directly at source chest

'''Pipes don't connect:'''
* Must be adjacent (no gaps)
* Check for [[Marking Fluid]] preventing connection
* [[Item Passthrough Pipe|Item Passthrough Pipes]] don't connect to inventories
