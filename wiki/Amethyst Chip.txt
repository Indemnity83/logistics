'''Amethyst Chip''' is an electronic [[Chips|chip]] used to craft [[Modules|modules]], built on a [[Chips#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Wafer]] base.

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1

|A2=Ender Dust |B2=Amethyst Dust |C2=Ender Dust
|A3=Ender Dust |B3=Silicon Wafer |C3=Ender Dust
|Output=Amethyst Chip,4
}}
