'''Chips''' are advanced electronic components used to craft [[Modules|modules]]. The chip tier determines the module's mark (MK1, MK2, or MK3). All chips are built on a Silicon Wafer base.

==Silicon Wafer==

The Silicon Wafer is the substrate for all chips.

'''Step 1 — Silicon Mix (shapeless crafting):'''

3× [[Dusts#Quartz Dust|Quartz Dust]] + 1× [[Dusts|Coal Dust]] → 1× Silicon Mix

'''Step 2 — Silicon Wafer (smelting):'''

Smelt Silicon Mix in a [[Kiln]] or furnace → 1× Silicon Wafer

==Chip Recipes==

All chips use the same pattern:

<pre>
[Ender Dust] [Resin Clump] [Ender Dust]
[Ender Dust] [Core Dust  ] [Ender Dust]
[Ender Dust] [Silicon Wfr] [Ender Dust]
</pre>

Where "Core Dust" is the substance that defines the chip type. All chips yield '''4× per craft'''.

----

===Amethyst Chip===

Same pattern as Redstone Chip but with [[Dusts|Amethyst Dust]] in the center slot.

'''Yields:''' 4× Amethyst Chip

Used in: '''MK2''' tier modules (Extractor MK2, Provider MK2, Active Supplier, Crafter MK2, Enchantment Sink)

----

===Echo Chip===

Same pattern but with [[Dusts|Echo Dust]] in the center slot.

'''Yields:''' 4× Echo Chip

Used in: '''MK3''' tier modules (Extractor MK3, Crafter MK3, Polymorphic Sink, Quicksort)

----

===Carbon Chip===

Same pattern but with [[Dusts|Coal Dust]] in the center slot.

'''Yields:''' 4× Carbon Chip

Used in: [[Item Sink Module]] only

----

==Progression Note==

Chips are mid-to-late game components. To craft them you need:
# [[Macerator]] — to produce Ender Dust, Quartz Dust, Coal Dust, and other dusts
# [[Kiln]] — to smelt Silicon Mix into Silicon Wafer
# Source of Resin Clumps (vanilla item, found in mangrove swamps)
