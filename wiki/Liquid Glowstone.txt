'''Liquid Glowstone''' is a molten fluid produced by melting glowstone in a [[Crucible]]. It '''glows''' — a [[Glass Tank]] or pipe filled with it emits a full light level 15.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crucible ===

{{Crucible|head=1 |Input=Glowstone |Output=Liquid Glowstone |Amount=1000}}
{{Crucible |foot=1 |Input=Glowstone Dust |Output=Liquid Glowstone |Amount=250}}

== Usage ==

Liquid Glowstone has no processing use yet, but it is the mod's brightest fluid: stored in a [[Glass Tank]] or run through [[Fluid Pipes]] it lights the area around it. Move and buffer it like any other fluid.

=== Bucket ===

The '''Liquid Glowstone Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}.
