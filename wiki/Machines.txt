'''Machines''' perform tasks automatically using RF energy.

==Mining==
* '''[[Laser Quarry]]''' - Automatically mines the area below, requires RF power; default 16×16 or a custom area marked out with the [[Marker]] tool

==Processing==
* '''[[Macerator]]''' - Grinds items into dust; ores (with chance byproducts), recycling, and dyes
* '''[[Alloy Smelter]]''' - Two-input machine; smelts alloys, doubles ores, and recycles metal items into ingots
* '''[[Crucible]]''' - Melts blocks and items into [[Fluids|fluids]] — molten metals, [[Crude Oil]], lava, and water
* '''[[Refinery]]''' - Distills one fluid into another — [[Liquid Biomass]] into [[Bio Fuel]], [[Crude Oil]] into [[Fuel Oil]]
* '''[[Kiln]]''' - RF-powered electric furnace; smelts any vanilla smelting recipe (no fuel — uses RF), with recipe-book GUI
* '''[[Sawmill]]''' - Cuts logs and wooden items into planks, and pulps plants into [[Pulped Biomass]]
* '''[[Sequential Fabricator]]''' - Assembles [[Chipsets|chipsets]] from redstone and a tier material

==Power==
* '''[[Power Junction]]''' - Feeds RF from cables or a [[Battery]] into a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]
