==Progression==

Bronze Gears represent mid-tier crafting materials:
* [[Wooden Gear]] → Basic tier
* [[Stone Gear]] → Stone tier
* [[Iron Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Copper Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Tin Gear]] → Mid tier
* '''Bronze Gear''' → Mid tier (this item)
* [[Gold Gear]] → Advanced tier
* [[Diamond Gear]] → Advanced tier
* [[Netherite Gear]] → End tier

==Tips==

* Requires bronze ingots (copper + tin alloy)
* Same tier as iron, copper, tin gears
* 1:1 bronze-to-gear ratio (4 ingots = 1 gear)
