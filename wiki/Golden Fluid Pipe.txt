The '''Golden Fluid Pipe''' is the fastest [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]], carrying liquids between [[Glass Tank|tanks]], [[Pump|pumps]], and machines at 8× the rate of a [[Copper Fluid Pipe]]. It is the fluid-side counterpart to the [[Golden Transport Pipe]].

Fluid pipes form a '''standalone''' transport system, separate from the item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]: each connected line carries one fluid type and moves it by pressure equalization. Passive transport through stone, copper, and golden fluid pipes is free — no power required.

'''Yields:''' 1× Golden Fluid Pipe (shapeless: [[Golden Transport Pipe]] + Slime Ball)

==Behavior==

* Transfers fluid between adjacent tanks, machines, and other fluid pipes at '''80 mB/t'''
* Carries '''one fluid type''' per connected line
* Passive transport is '''free''' (no RF) — unlike the [[Golden Transport Pipe]], no redstone is needed for the speed boost
* Connects automatically to fluid handlers on every face; use a [[Bypass Fluid Pipe]] to connect only to pipes
* Works with any mod's fluid storage on both Fabric and NeoForge

==Tips==
* Use on high-throughput lines — for example, draining a fast [[Pump]] or feeding a thirsty machine
* Mix with cheaper [[Copper Fluid Pipe|copper]] or [[Stone Fluid Pipe|stone]] pipes elsewhere; the slowest pipe on a line caps its throughput
* Store buffered fluid in a [[Glass Tank]]; stack tanks vertically to grow the reservoir
