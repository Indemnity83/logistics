'''Bronze ingots''' are an alloy of copper and [[Tin Ingot|tin]], used as the base material for [[Bronze Gear|gears]] and [[Valves|valves]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Chest loot ===

{{LootChestItem|bronze-ingot}}

=== Crafting ===

Alloy three copper with one [[Tin Ingot|tin]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|name=Bronze Ingot
|A2=Copper Ingot |B2=Copper Ingot |C2=Copper Ingot
|A3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Bronze Ingot,4
}}

{{Crafting
|name=Bronze Ingot
|B2=Bronze Block
|Output=Bronze Ingot,9
}}

{{Crafting
|foot=1
|name=Bronze Ingot
|A1=Bronze Nugget |B1=Bronze Nugget |C1=Bronze Nugget
|A2=Bronze Nugget |B2=Bronze Nugget |C2=Bronze Nugget
|A3=Bronze Nugget |B3=Bronze Nugget |C3=Bronze Nugget
|Output=Bronze Ingot
}}

=== Alloy Smelter ===

The [[Alloy Smelter]] alloys bronze from the same three copper and one tin, in ingot or [[Dusts|dust]] form:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Copper Ingot |InputCount=3 |Input2=Tin Ingot |Output=Bronze Ingot |OutputCount=4}}

Smelt [[Bronze Dust]] back into an ingot in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]] (RF-powered, no fuel):

{{Smelting
|head=1
|foot=1
|name=Bronze Ingot

|Output=Bronze Ingot

== Trivia ==

* Bronze is humanity's first widespread alloy — mixing a little tin into copper makes a metal harder than either, which is why the mod gates its [[Machine Frame|machine frames]] behind it. The three-to-one copper-to-tin recipe is roughly the ratio of real bronze.
