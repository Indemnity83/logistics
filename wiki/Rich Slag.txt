'''Rich Slag''' is the higher-grade byproduct of the [[Alloy Smelter]]. Unlike ordinary [[Slag]], it can be fed back in as a flux to squeeze a third ingot out of an ore.

== Obtaining ==

Rich Slag drops as a chance byproduct when the [[Alloy Smelter]] doubles ores with sand as flux, and from a few recycling recipes. Metal ores drop it at 5–20%; gem ores (lapis, quartz, redstone) at 50%.

== Usage ==

=== Ore processing ===

Used as the flux instead of sand, Rich Slag turns an ore into '''three''' ingots — at the cost of leaving ordinary [[Slag]] behind most of the time:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Iron Ore;Deepslate Iron Ore |Input2=Rich Slag |Output=Iron Ingot |OutputCount=3 |Byproduct=Slag |ByproductChance=75%}}

The same works for gold, copper, and tin ores.
