# Power

Engines generate RF energy to power automation machines. All engines require a direct redstone signal to operate and output energy from one configurable face.

## Energy System
- **[RF Energy](rf-energy.md)** - Understanding the power system

## Engines
- **[Redstone Engine](redstone-engine.md)** - Basic engine, converts redstone signal to small steady power
- **[Stirling Engine](stirling-engine.md)** - Burns fuel for substantial power output, can overheat
