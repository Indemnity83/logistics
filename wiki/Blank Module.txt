The '''Blank Module''' is the base crafting component for all [[Modules|modules]]. It has no behavior on its own — it is only used as an ingredient in module recipes. Every module type is crafted by combining a Blank Module with specific materials in the crafting grid.

==Recipe==

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Paper |B1=Redstone |C1=Paper
|A2=Paper |B2=Redstone |C2=Paper
|A3=Paper |B3=Gold Nugget |C3=Paper
|Output=Blank Module
}}

'''Yields:''' 1× Blank Module

==Usage==

The Blank Module is consumed when crafting any other module type. Craft it in advance so you have a supply ready when building out your [[Chassis Logistics Pipe]] setup.

==Tips==

* Craft Blank Modules in batches — each module type requires one, and a fully loaded MK5 chassis needs 5
* Paper is plentiful from sugar cane farms; gold nuggets come from gold ingots (9 nuggets per ingot)
* Store a stack of Blank Modules near your logistics crafting area to streamline module production
