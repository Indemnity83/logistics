The '''Item Extractor Pipe''' is a [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] that pulls items from an adjacent inventory into the network.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of Glass flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Planks |B2=Glass |C2=Planks
|Output=Item Extractor Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

The Item Extractor Pipe pulls items from the inventory on its wrench-selected face into the network. Use a [[Wrench]] to set which face extracts — the active face is shown by an opaque connector.

Extraction must be powered by an adjacent engine: a [[Redstone Engine]] moves one item per cycle, while a stronger engine such as the [[Stirling Engine]] can pull up to a full stack.
