# Blazing Valve

The **Blazing Valve** is a Tier 6 valve crafted in the [Kiln](../automation/kiln.md). It has the **maximum energy demand** of any valve, far exceeding lava bucket capacity.

## Recipe
**[Kiln](../automation/kiln.md) crafting:**
- 5× Blaze Rod
- 2× Redstone
- 250mb Molten Glass
- **Process time:** 200 ticks
- **Energy demand:** 440 energy/tick
- **Yields:** 4× Blazing Valve

**Pattern:**
```
 B
RBR
BBB
```
B = Blaze Rod, R = Redstone

## Fuel Requirements

**Tier 6 - Maximum demand**

Energy demand: 440 energy/tick
- **Lava Bucket:** **Grossly insufficient** (~315 capacity vs 440 demand)

This is the absolute maximum energy demand valve. Lava buckets struggle significantly.

## Usage

Valves are crafting components for future features. Blazing valve represents the ceiling of current kiln recipes.

## Tips

- **Highest energy demand** of any valve (440)
- Lava buckets barely make progress
- Extreme pause/resume cycling
- Will complete eventually but very slowly
- Requires blaze rod farming
