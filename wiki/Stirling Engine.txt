The '''Stirling Engine''' is a high-power generator that burns fuel to produce substantial [[RF Energy|RF]]. It can overheat if run continuously without cooling, requiring careful management.

== Usage ==

The Stirling Engine burns fuel (coal, charcoal, wood, lava buckets, etc.) to generate RF. It requires both fuel and a redstone signal to operate, and produces far more power than the [[Redstone Engine]] — enough for the [[Laser Quarry]] and other high-throughput machines. Energy outputs from one configurable face.

=== Fuel ===

Right-click the engine to open the fuel GUI and add burnable items — it accepts more fuel types than a vanilla furnace, including coal, charcoal, wood planks, and lava buckets (which provide a long burn time). Fuel burns while the engine is powered by redstone, so keep it stocked for continuous operation.

=== Temperature and overheating ===

The engine heats up during operation, and the block changes color through visible heat stages so you can read its temperature at a glance. If it gets too hot it shuts down safely — it does not break or explode — and must cool before restarting. Right-click an overheated engine with a [[Wrench]] to reset it. To prevent overheating, run in intervals, toggle the redstone signal periodically, or rotate between multiple engines.

=== Configuration ===

Use a [[Wrench]] to rotate the output face (cycle through directions) and to reset an overheated engine.
