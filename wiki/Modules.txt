'''Modules''' are items that are installed into a [[Chassis Logistics Pipe]] to give it specific behaviors. Each module type provides a distinct logistics function — combining multiple modules in a single chassis creates a multi-function logistics node.

All modules are crafted from a [[Blank Module]] base component.

==Base Component==

* '''[[Blank Module]]''' - Required crafting base for all modules

==Extraction Modules==

Pull items from adjacent inventories into the logistics network.

* '''[[Extractor Module]]''' (MK1/II/III) - Extract items from an adjacent inventory; higher marks extract faster or in greater quantities

==Provider & Supplier Modules==

Advertise and supply items to the network.

* '''[[Provider Module]]''' (MK1/II) - Advertise adjacent inventory contents to the network; higher mark provides larger item counts
* '''[[Supplier Module]]''' (Passive/Active) - Maintain stock levels in adjacent inventories by requesting items from the network; Active variant requests proactively

==Crafting Modules==

Automate item crafting within the chassis.

* '''[[Crafter Module]]''' (MK1/II/III) - Define a crafting recipe to fulfill on demand; higher marks support more complex or faster crafting

==Sorting & Routing Modules==

Control where items go within the network.

* '''[[Quicksort Module]]''' - Sort items by type into designated storage locations
* '''[[Terminus Module]]''' - Mark a pipe as a network terminus; items reaching this pipe are held or redirected

==Sink Modules==

Accept and store specific items or categories.

* '''[[Item Sink Module]]''' - Accept a specific configured item type and deposit it into an adjacent inventory
* '''[[Polymorphic Sink Module]]''' - Accept any item that matches an already-stored item type in the adjacent inventory (fills existing stacks)
* '''[[Enchantment Sink Module]]''' - Accept any enchanted item and deposit into a book or chest
* '''[[Mod Item Sink Module]]''' - Accept any item from a specified mod namespace
