'''Quartz Crystal''' is a crafting component — the frame of every [[Valves|valve]] recipe. It is smelted from [[Quartz Dust]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt [[Quartz Dust]] in a [[Kiln]] or furnace:

|foot=1

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Quartz Crystal forms the frame of every [[Valves|valve]] — eight crystals around a [[Cores|core]], with iron and gold nuggets along the bottom. See [[Valves]] for the full recipe.
