'''Raw tin''' is a raw metal dropped by [[Tin Ore|tin ore]]. It is smelted into a [[Tin Ingot|tin ingot]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Mining ===

[[Tin Ore]] and deepslate tin ore drop 2–5 raw tin when mined with a stone pickaxe or better; the count is increased by Fortune. See [[Tin Ore#Breaking|Tin Ore]] for the full breaking table.
