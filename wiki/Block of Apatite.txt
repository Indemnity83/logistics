A '''block of apatite''' is a storage block equivalent to nine [[Apatite (gem)|apatite]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

A block of apatite can be mined only with a stone pickaxe or better. Mined without a pickaxe it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

A block of apatite stores apatite compactly. It can be unpacked back into nine gems at any time.
