'''Recipes''' describe how to craft Logistics blocks and items. Every craftable page shows its recipe inline under an '''Obtaining''' section.

== How recipes are shown ==

A single recipe is drawn as a Minecraft-style crafting grid with the [[Template:Grid Crafting Table|Grid Crafting Table]] template, while pages that cover a whole family of items group their recipes into a [[Template:Crafting|Crafting]] table (Name / Ingredients / Recipe / Description).

== Where to find recipes ==

The recipe-heavy overview pages are the quickest way to browse what's craftable:

* '''Pipes''' — [[Transport Pipes]], [[Logistics Pipes]], [[Fluid Pipes]]
* '''Power''' — [[Cables]], and the engines and [[Battery]] under [[Energy]]
* '''Components''' — [[Gears]], [[Valves]], [[Cores]], [[Chips]], [[Dusts]]

Individual machine, tool, and module pages each show their own recipe as well.
