'''Apatite Ore''' is an ore that drops [[Apatite|apatite]] gems, used for [[Apatite Valve|apatite valves]] and advanced components.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Apatite Ore drops [[Apatite]] gems directly when mined — no smelting needed — and requires a '''stone pickaxe''' or better. It also drops '''0–2 experience'''. The Fortune enchantment increases apatite drops.

=== Natural generation ===

Apatite Ore generates in every Overworld biome during the underground-ore step, between '''Y 60 and Y 256''' with a '''uniform''' distribution (equally likely at any height in that band, so it favors hills and mountains). It forms in unusually '''large veins of up to 48 blocks''', with about '''2 veins per chunk''', and has no deepslate variant. Veins generate normally even where they are exposed to air.

== Usage ==

Apatite from this ore feeds gem-based components.

=== Grinding ===

Grind Apatite Ore in the [[Macerator]] for '''Apatite Dust''' (see [[Dusts]]).

=== Storage ===

Nine apatite can be packed into an '''Apatite Block''' for compact storage.
