'''Apatite ore''' is a gem ore found underground. It drops [[Apatite (gem)|apatite]], used mainly to craft [[Apatite Valve|apatite valves]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Apatite ore drops 1–6 [[Apatite (gem)|apatite]] when mined with a stone pickaxe or better; the count is increased by Fortune. Mined with Silk Touch it drops itself instead. Breaking it also releases 0–2 experience.

{{Breaking
|tier=stone
|name1=Apatite Ore |icon1=Grid Apatite Ore.png |hardness1=3
}}

=== Natural generation ===

Apatite ore generates in every Overworld biome during the world's underground-ore step, in unusually large veins of up to '''48''' blocks. It places two veins per chunk spread evenly from Y 60 to Y 256, so it favors hills and mountains. There is no deepslate variant. Veins generate normally even where exposed to air, so they show on cave walls.

== Usage ==

=== Grinding ===

Grind apatite ore in a [[Macerator]] for six [[Apatite Dust]] per ore — a generous yield versus mining the gems directly:
