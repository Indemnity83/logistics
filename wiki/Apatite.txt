'''Apatite''' is a gem obtained from mining [[Apatite Ore|apatite ore]]. It's used for apatite valves.

==Obtaining==

'''Mining:'''
* Mine [[Apatite Ore]]
* Drops 1× Apatite (more with Fortune)

==Usage==

'''[[Kiln]] Crafting:'''
* [[Apatite Valve]] - 5× apatite required

'''Storage:'''
* 9× Apatite → Apatite Block

==Tips==

* Gem-type material (like diamond/emerald)
* Fortune enchantment increases drops
* Needed for Tier 3 valves
* Requires premium fuel (blaze rod/lava) to craft valve
