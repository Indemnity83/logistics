'''Chips''' are advanced electronic components used to craft [[Modules|modules]]. The chip tier determines the module's mark (MK1, MK2, or MK3). All chips are built on a [[#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Wafer]] base.

* [[File:Grid Carbon Chip.png|24px|link=Carbon Chip]] '''[[Carbon Chip]]'''

== Silicon Wafer ==

The Silicon Wafer is the substrate for every chip, made in two steps:

# '''Silicon Mix''' — combine 3× [[Quartz Dust]] and 1× [[Coal Dust]] at a crafting table (shapeless).
# '''Silicon Wafer''' — smelt the Silicon Mix in a [[Kiln]] or furnace.
