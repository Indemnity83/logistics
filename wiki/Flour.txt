'''Flour''' is milled from {{Mcw|Wheat|wheat}} in a [[Macerator]]. It doubles wheat's yield in baking: the same loaf, cake, or batch of cookies costs half the grain when the wheat is milled first.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

One wheat mills into two Flour:

{{Grinding|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Wheat |Output=Flour,2}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Flour substitutes for wheat in the three vanilla baking recipes, at half the count:

{{Crafting|head=1|showdescription=1
|name={{Mcw|Bread}}
|A1=Flour |B1=Flour |C1=Flour
|Output=Bread
|description=Three Flour, where bread normally takes three wheat — so one and a half wheat, milled.}}
{{Crafting|showdescription=1
|name={{Mcw|Cookie}}
|A1=Flour |B1=Cocoa Beans |C1=Flour
|Output=Cookie,8
|description=Two Flour and a {{Mcw|Cocoa Beans|cocoa bean}}.}}
{{Crafting|foot=1|showdescription=1
|name={{Mcw|Cake}}
|A1=Milk Bucket |B1=Milk Bucket |C1=Milk Bucket
|A2=Sugar |B2=Egg |C2=Sugar
|A3=Flour |B3=Flour |C3=Flour
|Output=Cake
|description=Three Flour in place of the cake's three wheat.}}

== Mod compatibility ==

Flour is published as <code>#c:flours/wheat</code>, and the baking recipes read that tag rather than this item — so [https://modrinth.com/mod/create Create]'s wheat flour, or any other mod's, bakes here, and Logistics flour works in theirs. See [[Mod Compatibility]].
