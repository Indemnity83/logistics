The '''Copper Transport Pipe''' is a [[Tier System|Tier 1]] mechanical pipe that provides standard-speed item transport. It is the backbone of most pipe networks: cheaper than the Tier 2 smart pipes, faster than the [[Stone Transport Pipe]], and able to be color-coded with [[Marking Fluid]] so separate networks don't merge.

== Usage ==

Copper Transport Pipes move items at standard speed and connect to every other pipe type and to adjacent inventories, making them the default choice for general transport.

=== Routing ===

At a junction with several possible exits, an item picks a direction at random. This works well for single-destination networks; for controlled routing, combine copper pipes with an [[Item Merger Pipe]] or an [[Item Filter Pipe]].

=== Network segmentation ===

Mark copper pipes with [[Marking Fluid]] to split a network by color:

# Right-click the pipe with marking fluid
# The pipe takes on the dye color
# '''Same color''' pipes connect; '''different''' colors do not; '''unmarked''' pipes connect to anything
# Sneak + right-click with an empty hand to clear the marking

This lets multiple independent networks run side by side without merging.

=== Oxidization ===

Copper pipes weather like vanilla copper, passing through Copper → Exposed → Weathered → Oxidized over time. This is '''purely cosmetic''' and does not affect transport speed or function.

* '''Honeycomb''' waxes a pipe to stop further oxidization
* An '''axe''' scrapes one layer off
* Mixing stages is handy for aesthetic builds
