A '''Fluid Packet''' is the hidden item a [[Fluid Provider Pipe|fluid provider]] mints to carry a chunk of fluid through the ordinary item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]] to a [[Fluid Supplier Pipe|fluid supplier]]. It is never crafted, never appears in the creative inventory, and can't be picked up or stored like a normal item — it exists only in transit.

== Usage ==

Fluid packets are how [[Fluid Provider Pipe|fluid provider]] and [[Fluid Supplier Pipe|fluid supplier]] pipes (and their module equivalents) move fluid across the item pipe network instead of a dedicated fluid pipe. Each packet carries one fluid type and an amount up to a configured maximum (5,000 mB by default); a delivery that doesn't divide evenly into full-size packets ships one smaller packet for the remainder. Packets never stack, so each one travels as its own visible item, rendered as the carried fluid behind a frame with a rectangular window.

If a packet becomes undeliverable — the network loses power, or its destination disappears — it is '''voided''' instead of dropping as a ground item, since it can't be picked up or stored like a normal item.
