'''Oil Shale''' is an oil-bearing rock that generates underground. Extract [[Bitumen]] from it, or liquefy it into [[Crude Oil]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Oil Shale breaks fastest with a shovel and always drops itself.

{{Breaking
|tool=shovel
|tier=none
|name1=Oil Shale |icon1=Grid Oil Shale.png |hardness1=0.6
}}

=== Natural generation ===

Oil Shale generates as underground veins replacing gravel, in '''desert''', '''badlands''', and '''savanna''' biomes.

=== Transposer ===

Soaking gravel in [[Crude Oil]] recreates the block, so it is renewable once an oil supply is running:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Gravel |FluidIn=Crude Oil |InAmount=1000 |Output=Oil Shale
|notes=4,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] liquefies Oil Shale into [[Crude Oil]]:

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Shale |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=1000}}

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] extracts [[Bitumen]] from it, with a chance of flint:

{{Grinding|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Shale |Output=Bitumen,3 |Byproduct=Flint |ByproductChance=50%}}
