Crafting components, ores, metals, and gems used throughout Logistics.

==Machine & Crafting Components==

* '''[[Machine Core]]''' - Required component for all RF-powered machines (Macerator, Kiln, Laser Quarry)
* '''[[Sturdy Casing]]''' - Reinforced bronze machine shell, companion to the Machine Core
* '''[[Cores]]''' - Intermediate components used in valve crafting (Wooden Core, Iron Core, Bronze Core, etc.)
* '''[[Dusts]]''' - Ground materials produced by the Macerator; used in module crafting and ore doubling
* '''[[Chips|Chips & Silicon]]''' - Electronic components for module crafting (Redstone Chip, Amethyst Chip, Echo Chip, Carbon Chip)
* '''[[Rubber]]''' - Rubber Mix and Rubber Chunk; insulates [[Copper Cable|power cables]]

==Valves==

Valves are crafted at the crafting table using [[Cores#Quartz Crystal|Quartz Crystal]] and a matching [[Cores|Core]]. All valves share the same recipe pattern:

<pre>
[Q] [Q] [Q]
[Q] [Core] [Q]
[I] [G] [I]
</pre>

Where Q = Quartz Crystal, I = Iron Nugget, G = Gold Nugget. The Core in the center determines the valve type.

'''Quartz Crystal''' is obtained by smelting [[Quartz Dust]] (ground from Nether Quartz in the [[Macerator]]) in the [[Kiln]] or furnace.
