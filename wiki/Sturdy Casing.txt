The '''Sturdy Casing''' is a crafting component used to build machines. It is a reinforced shell formed from [[Bronze Ingot|Bronze Ingots]], serving as the structural body that machine internals are built around — a companion to the [[Machine Core]].

'''Yields:''' 1× Sturdy Casing (8× [[Bronze Ingot]] arranged in a ring)

==Usage==

The Sturdy Casing is a structural crafting component used in machine recipes, providing the outer shell that houses a [[Machine Core]] and other internals.

==Tips==

* Bronze Ingots are an alloy of Copper + Tin — keep a [[Tin Ore|tin]] supply on hand
* Craft casings in batches alongside [[Machine Core|Machine Cores]] when building several machines at once
