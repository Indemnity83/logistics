The '''Ender Cable''' is the highest-tier [[RF Energy|RF]] power cable. It carries energy from an [[Engines & Power|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or machine at the greatest throughput of any cable.

'''Yields:''' 8× Ender Cable

==Behavior==

* Transfers RF from a connected [[Engines & Power|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or machine
* Transfer rate: '''120 RF/t''' — the fastest of the three cable tiers
* Cables connect to each other and to adjacent power blocks, forming a power network
* Use when feeding a high-demand [[Battery]] or running power over long distances

==Cable Tiers==

The three cables behave identically except for how much RF they can move per tick (Copper < Gold < Ender):

* '''[[Copper Cable]]''' - 30 RF/t
* '''[[Gold Cable]]''' - 60 RF/t
* '''[[Ender Cable]]''' - 120 RF/t
