'''Tin Ore''' is a common ore found underground. Smelt its drop to create [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]] for crafting bronze and tin components. A '''Deepslate Tin Ore''' variant occurs in deepslate layers.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Tin Ore drops '''Raw Tin''' when mined and requires a '''stone pickaxe''' or better. The '''Deepslate Tin Ore''' variant drops the same Raw Tin but is harder to break. Mining with a [[Macerator]] is not required, but grinding the ore there yields '''2× Tin Dust''' (ore doubling, see [[Dusts]]); a Fortune pickaxe increases Raw Tin drops.

=== Natural generation ===

Tin Ore generates in every Overworld biome during the underground-ore step, in veins of up to '''10''' blocks. It appears in two depth bands — the deepslate variant is far more abundant:

{| class="wikitable"
! Variant !! Height (Y) !! Distribution !! Veins per chunk
|-
| Tin Ore || 0 to 112 || trapezoid (peaks near Y 56) || 1
|-
| Deepslate Tin Ore || −16 to 0 || trapezoid (peaks near Y −8) || 13
|}

Veins generate normally even where they are exposed to air, so they show on cave walls.

== Usage ==

Raw Tin from this ore is processed into tin and bronze components.

=== Smelting ===

Smelt Raw Tin in a furnace or blast furnace to produce [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]].

=== Grinding ===

Grind Tin Ore or Deepslate Tin Ore in the [[Macerator]] for '''2× Tin Dust''' per ore (ore doubling, see [[Dusts]]).

=== Storage ===

Raw Tin can be packed into a '''Block of Raw Tin''' (9× Raw Tin) for compact storage.
