The '''Fuel Engine''' burns liquid fuels — [[Crude Oil]], [[Bio Fuel]], and [[Fuel Oil]] — to generate [[RF Energy|RF]]. It runs hot, so it needs a water coolant supply to avoid a thermal shutdown.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Fuel Engine can be broken with any tool, though a pickaxe is fastest.

== Usage ==

Pipe a liquid fuel into the fuel tank and water into the coolant tank (4,000 mB each) with [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipes]]. While burning it generates '''10–40 RF/t''' from a configurable face. Generation produces waste heat; the coolant carries it away. If the engine overheats past 250&nbsp;°C it triggers a '''thermal shutdown''' — right-click it with a [[Wrench]] to reset. A [[Jade]] readout shows its temperature, output, and fuel and coolant levels.

=== Fuels ===

Each fuel carries a different amount of energy and burns at a different heat:

=== Configuration ===

Use a [[Wrench]] to rotate the output face and to reset a thermal shutdown.
