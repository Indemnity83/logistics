The '''Glass Tank''' is a fluid storage block that buffers liquids for a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid network]]. It holds '''16,000 mB''' (16 buckets) of a single fluid type, and stacked tanks merge into one larger reservoir.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Glass Tank can be broken with any tool — it breaks almost instantly.

== Usage ==

The Glass Tank stores '''16,000 mB (16 buckets)''' of one fluid type. It accepts insert and extract on all faces — connect a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] to any side. A dynamic block-entity renderer shows the fluid's actual sprite and color at its current fill height through the glass. You can also fill or drain it by hand with a {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}} (and other fluid-container items) by right-clicking.

=== Stacking ===

Tanks placed in a vertical column form one logical reservoir whose capacity is the sum of all tanks, filling '''bottom-up'''. Stack several to build a tall reservoir — "scale is the feature," done as tileable blocks rather than a multiblock.

Buffer the output of a [[Fluid Pump|Pump]] or [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] before feeding it onward, and because each tank holds one fluid type, keep separate columns for different fluids. Use an [[Insertion Fluid Pipe]] or [[Merger Fluid Pipe]] to control how fluid enters the tank.
