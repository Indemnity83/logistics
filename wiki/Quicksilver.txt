'''Quicksilver''' is a material extracted from cinnabar (and, occasionally, gold ore) in a [[Macerator]]. It has no use yet — recipes that consume it are coming soon.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] grinds cinnabar and its variants into Quicksilver ''(Minecraft 26.2 and newer)''. Higher-volume blocks yield more:

{{Grinding|head=1 |Input=Cinnabar |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Polished Cinnabar |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Cinnabar Stairs |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Cinnabar Wall |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |foot=1 |Input=Cinnabar Slab |Output=Quicksilver,2}}

Quicksilver also drops as a 10% byproduct when grinding [[Gold Dust|gold ore]].

== Usage ==

Quicksilver currently has no crafting uses. It is reserved for an upcoming feature.
