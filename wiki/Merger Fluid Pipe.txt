The '''Merger Fluid Pipe''' acts as a '''check valve''': it only lets fluid flow out one wrench-set face, never back in. It is the fluid-side counterpart to the [[Item Merger Pipe]], used to converge several inputs into one one-way output.

Fluid pipes form a '''standalone''' transport system, separate from the item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]: each connected line carries one fluid type and moves it by pressure equalization. Passive transport is free — no power required.

'''Yields:''' 1× Merger Fluid Pipe (shapeless: [[Item Merger Pipe]] + Slime Ball)

==Behavior==

* '''Directs fluid one way out a wrench-set face''' — a directional check valve
* Fluid may only leave through the chosen output face; it cannot flow back in that direction
* Other faces act as inputs, letting multiple lines converge into a single one-way output
* Passive transport is '''free''' (no RF)
* Carries '''one fluid type''' per connected line

==Configuration==
Use a [[Wrench]] to set the output face. Right-click the pipe to cycle which face the valve points out of; fluid then flows only in that direction.

==Tips==
* Use to merge several incoming fluid lines into one without back-flow
* Place at a tank's input to ensure fluid only ever flows into the tank, never drains back out
* Pair with [[Insertion Fluid Pipe|insertion]] pipes for tightly-controlled fill behavior
