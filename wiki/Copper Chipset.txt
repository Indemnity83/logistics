'''Copper Chipset''' is a fabricated component built in the [[Sequential Fabricator]]. Chipsets are an alternative to [[Gears|gears]] for crafting [[Modules|modules]] and [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipes]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Sequential Fabricator ===

{{Sequential Fabricator|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Copper Ingot |Output=Copper Chipset |notes=15,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

The Copper Chipset is not used in any recipe yet — it is reserved for future content.
