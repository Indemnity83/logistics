'''Liquid Redstone''' is a molten fluid produced by melting redstone in a [[Crucible]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crucible ===

{{Crucible|head=1 |Input=Redstone Block |Output=Liquid Redstone |Amount=900}}
{{Crucible |foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Output=Liquid Redstone |Amount=100}}

== Usage ==

Liquid Redstone has no processing use yet — it is stored and piped like any other fluid. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Bucket ===

The '''Liquid Redstone Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}.
