'''Recipes''' describe how to craft Logistics blocks and items. On this wiki, crafting recipes are displayed with the <code>{{[[Template:Grid Crafting Table|Grid Crafting Table]]}}</code> template, which renders a 3×3 crafting grid and the resulting output.

Every page that documents a craftable block or item includes its recipe inline and is tagged under [[:Category:Recipes]].

==Browsing Recipes==

* '''[[:Category:Recipes]]''' - Every page that contains a crafting recipe.

==Recipe Template==

To add a recipe to a page, use the [[Template:Grid Crafting Table|Grid Crafting Table]] template. Grid positions are named by column (A–C) and row (1–3):

<pre>
{{Grid Crafting Table
|A2=Planks |B2=Glass |C2=Planks
|Output=Item Extractor Pipe |OutputCount=8
}}
</pre>

Omit any empty slots. Use <code>Output</code> for the result item and <code>OutputCount</code> when the recipe yields more than one.
