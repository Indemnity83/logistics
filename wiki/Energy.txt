'''Energy''' covers the engines and RF power system in Logistics. Engines generate RF energy to power automation machines. All engines require a direct redstone signal to operate and output energy from one configurable face.

==Energy System==
* '''[[RF Energy]]''' - Understanding the power system

==Engines==
* '''[[Redstone Engine]]''' - Basic engine, converts redstone signal to small steady power
* '''[[Stirling Engine]]''' - Burns fuel for substantial power output, can overheat
