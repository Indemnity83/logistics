The '''Tin Gear''' is a mid-tier crafting component made from [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]]. It's equivalent in tier to iron and copper gears.

==Recipe==

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B1=Tin Ingot
|A2=Tin Ingot |B2=Copper Gear |C2=Tin Ingot
|B3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Tin Gear
}}

'''Yields:''' 1× Tin Gear (requires [[Tin Ingot]] + [[Copper Gear]])

==Uses==

Tin Gears are crafting components for machines and engines (specific uses TBD).

==Progression==

Tin Gears represent mid-tier crafting materials:
* [[Wooden Gear]] → Basic tier
* [[Stone Gear]] → Stone tier
* [[Iron Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Copper Gear]] → Mid tier
* '''Tin Gear''' → Mid tier (this item)
* [[Bronze Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Gold Gear]] → Advanced tier
* [[Diamond Gear]] → Advanced tier
* [[Netherite Gear]] → End tier

==Tips==

* Requires tin ingots from [[Tin Ore]]
* Same tier as iron and copper gears
* 1:1 tin-to-gear ratio (4 ingots = 1 gear)
