'''Fuel Oil''' is a fluid refined from [[Crude Oil]] in a [[Refinery]]. Refining also vents [[Tar]] as a byproduct.

== Obtaining ==

=== Refinery ===

{{Refinery|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Crude Oil |InputAmount=200 |Output=Fuel Oil |Amount=150 |Byproduct=Tar |ByproductChance=50%}}

== Usage ==

Fuel Oil is burned in a [[Fuel Engine]] to generate [[RF Energy|RF]] — the most energy-dense of the engine's fuels. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Bucket ===

The '''Fuel Oil Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}.
