'''Bronze ingots''' are an alloy of copper and [[Tin Ingot|tin]], used as the base material for [[Machine Core|machine cores]], [[Sturdy Casing|casings]], [[Bronze Gear|gears]], and [[Valves|valves]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Chest loot ===

{{LootChestItem|bronze-ingot}}

=== Crafting ===

Alloy three copper with one [[Tin Ingot|tin]] (shapeless):

|name=Bronze Ingot

|A3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Bronze Ingot,4

== Trivia ==

* Bronze is humanity's first widespread alloy — mixing a little tin into copper makes a metal harder than either, which is why the mod gates its [[Machine Core|machine cores]] behind it. The three-to-one copper-to-tin recipe is roughly the ratio of real bronze.
