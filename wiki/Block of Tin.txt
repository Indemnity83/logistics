A '''block of tin''' is a storage block equivalent to nine [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

A block of tin can be mined only with a stone pickaxe or better. Mined without a pickaxe it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

A block of tin stores tin ingots compactly. It can be unpacked back into nine ingots at any time.
