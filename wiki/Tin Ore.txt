'''Tin ore''' is a mineral block found underground. It is a source of [[Raw Tin|raw tin]], which is smelted into [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]].

'''Deepslate tin ore''' is a variant that generates in the deepslate layer; it drops the same raw tin but is slightly harder to break.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Tin ore drops 2–5 '''[[Raw Tin|raw tin]]''' when mined with a stone pickaxe or better; the count is increased by Fortune. Mined with Silk Touch it drops itself instead. Deepslate tin ore behaves identically.

=== Natural generation ===

Tin ore generates in every Overworld biome during the world's underground-ore step, in veins of up to '''10''' blocks. It generates in two batches.

The upper batch places one vein per chunk between Y 0 and Y 112, tapering off toward either end and most common around Y 56. It produces regular tin ore in stone, granite, diorite, andesite, and tuff.

The lower batch is far more abundant — 13 veins per chunk between Y −16 and Y 0, most common around Y −8 — and produces deepslate tin ore where it replaces deepslate. Veins generate normally even where exposed to air, so they show on cave walls.
