The '''Battery''' is an RF storage block. It is charged by [[Engines|engines]] through [[Cables]] and holds that energy for later use. To power a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]], feed a Battery's RF into a [[Power Junction]] — pipes do not draw from a Battery directly.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Battery is mined with a pickaxe of any tier; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

The Battery buffers RF on your power grid. Engines charge it through cables, and it discharges through cables to whatever needs power — most often a machine such as a [[Kiln]], [[Macerator]], or [[Sawmill]], or a [[Power Junction]]. A Battery is the grid's reserve rather than its first choice: on a shared cable, machines are served before Batteries are topped up, and a Battery gives only once the generators on that cable are spent.

* Holds '''100,000 RF''' by default
* Moves at most '''1,000 RF/t''' in or out of any one side, and actively pushes at most '''200 RF/t''' into each adjacent machine
* Charge level is shown as a fill bar on the placed block and as a green charge bar on the item
* A bank of Batteries on one cable is drawn from together, each contributing in proportion to what it can give, so the bank empties evenly rather than one cell at a time
* Batteries wired to the same cable level out: charge runs from a fuller cell to an emptier one and stops once their fill fractions match

The capacity and both transfer limits live in <code>config/logistics/power.battery.json</code>, and can be changed in-game with the operator-only <code>/logistics config power.battery &lt;key&gt; &lt;value&gt;</code> command:

{{Config|head=1|capacity|Total RF storage.|100000|Integer}}
{{Config|max_io|Max RF/t inserted or extracted per side.|1000|Integer}}
{{Config|foot=1|output_per_side|Max RF/t actively pushed into each adjacent machine.|200|Integer}}

=== Power ===

A Battery is charged with RF produced by an [[Engines|engine]] and delivered through [[Copper Cable|power cables]]:

* [[Redstone Engine]] / [[Stirling Engine]] / [[Creative Engine]] generate RF
* [[Copper Cable]], [[Gold Cable]], and [[Ender Cable]] carry that RF to the Battery at increasing transfer rates

Note that extraction pipes (the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]) are paced by an adjacent '''engine''' instead, not the network Battery.
