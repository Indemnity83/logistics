The '''Item Merger Pipe''' is a [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] that sends every input to one chosen output.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of Glass flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Iron Ingot |B2=Glass |C2=Iron Ingot
|Output=Item Merger Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

The Item Merger Pipe forces every item that enters it out through a single wrench-selected face, giving a guaranteed direction instead of random routing. Use it to funnel several input streams into one destination.

Set the output face with a [[Wrench]].
