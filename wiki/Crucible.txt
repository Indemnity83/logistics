The '''Crucible''' is an RF-powered machine that melts solid blocks and items into [[Fluids|fluids]], filling an internal tank that feeds a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid network]]. It turns ore blocks, nether materials, and ice into molten metals, [[Crude Oil]], lava, and water.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Crucible is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

Shares the standard machine base — a [[Machine Frame]], a [[Redstone Reception Coil]], and two [[Copper Gear|copper gears]] — with a Magma Block and Nether Bricks on top.

== Usage ==

Place a block or item in the single input slot; the Crucible melts it over time and deposits the resulting fluid into its 10,000 mB output-only tank. Drain the tank with a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] or [[Pump]], or connect [[Fluid Pipes]] directly. The front face shows a gauge of the stored fluid.

=== Power ===

The Crucible runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 40,000 RF and accepts up to 128 RF/t, spending 40 RF/t while melting. Each recipe carries a total RF cost — melting a stack of glowstone is far more expensive than melting ice — so connect a [[Stirling Engine]] or a strong RF source.

=== Recipes ===

Recipes appear in the Just Enough Items (JEI) viewer. Amounts are the millibuckets deposited per input item.
