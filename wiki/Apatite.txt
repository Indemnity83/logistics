'''Apatite''' is a gem obtained from mining [[Apatite Ore|apatite ore]]. It is used for [[Apatite Valve|apatite valves]], and can be compacted into a '''Block of Apatite''' or ground into '''[[Dusts|Apatite Dust]]''' in the [[Macerator]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Mining ===

Mine [[Apatite Ore]] to drop apatite gems directly (more with Fortune).

=== Crafting ===

Apatite can be packed into and unpacked from its storage block:

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B2=Apatite Block
|Output=Apatite |OutputCount=9
}}

'''From a block:''' 1× Apatite Block = 9× Apatite.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Apatite |B1=Apatite |C1=Apatite
|A2=Apatite |B2=Apatite |C2=Apatite
|A3=Apatite |B3=Apatite |C3=Apatite
|Output=Apatite Block
}}

'''To a block:''' 9× Apatite = 1× Apatite Block.

== Usage ==

Apatite is a gem-type material used for valves and processing.

=== Valves ===

Craft an [[Valves|Apatite Valve]] via an Apatite [[Cores|Core]]. The valve craft requires a premium fuel such as a blaze rod or lava.

=== Grinding ===

Grind [[Apatite Ore]] in the [[Macerator]] for Apatite Dust (see [[Dusts]]).

=== Storage ===

Nine apatite pack into a Block of Apatite for compact storage.
