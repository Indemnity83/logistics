The '''Sawmill''' is an RF-powered machine that cuts logs and other wooden items into planks, yielding [[Sawdust]] as a byproduct. It also pulps loose plant matter into [[Pulped Biomass]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Sawmill is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

Shares the standard machine base — a [[Machine Frame]], a [[Redstone Reception Coil]], and two [[Copper Gear|copper gears]] — with an [[Iron Axe]] and planks on top.

== Usage ==

Place a log or other wooden item in the input slot; the Sawmill cuts it into planks over time and rolls its [[Sawdust]] byproduct on completion. It has a three-slot inventory — input from the top and sides, primary and byproduct outputs drawn from the bottom.

=== Power ===

The Sawmill runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 10,000 RF and accepts up to 128 RF/t. Each recipe carries an RF cost (2,000–3,000 RF); connect a [[Stirling Engine]] or any RF source.

=== Recipes ===

==== Wood → planks ====

Every wood type works the same way; oak is shown as an example. Logs and bamboo blocks give the most planks and always drop [[Sawdust]]; processed items give fewer planks and a smaller sawdust chance.

{{Milling|head=1 |Input=Oak Log |ingredients=Logs / Bamboo blocks (any type) |Output=Oak Planks,6 |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=100%}}
{{Milling |Input=Oak Boat |ingredients=Boats |Output=Oak Planks,4 |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=125%}}
{{Milling |Input=Oak Door |ingredients=Doors |Output=Oak Planks |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=50%}}
{{Milling |foot=1 |Input=Oak Stairs |ingredients=Stairs / Fences / Fence gates |Output=Oak Planks |Byproduct=Sawdust |ByproductChance=25%}}

==== Plants → Pulped Biomass ====

Loose plant matter pulps into [[Pulped Biomass]] — one per recipe, with denser plants taking more input:

{{Milling|head=1 |Input=Oak Leaves |ingredients=Leaves, saplings, kelp, moss, seagrass, seeds (×8) |Output=Pulped Biomass}}
{{Milling |Input=Sugar Cane |ingredients=Cactus, sugar cane, vines, tall grass (×6) |Output=Pulped Biomass}}
{{Milling |foot=1 |Input=Wheat |ingredients=Crops, flowers, mushrooms, fern (×4) |Output=Pulped Biomass}}
