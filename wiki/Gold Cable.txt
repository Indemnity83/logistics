The '''Gold Cable''' is the mid-tier [[RF Energy|RF]] power cable. It carries energy from an [[Engines & Power|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or machine at double the throughput of a [[Copper Cable]].

'''Yields:''' 8× Gold Cable

==Behavior==

* Transfers RF from a connected [[Engines & Power|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or machine
* Transfer rate: '''60 RF/t''' — twice as fast as a [[Copper Cable]]
* Cables connect to each other and to adjacent power blocks, forming a power network
* A good middle ground between cheap [[Copper Cable|Copper]] and high-throughput [[Ender Cable|Ender]] cables

==Cable Tiers==

The three cables behave identically except for how much RF they can move per tick (Copper < Gold < Ender):

* '''[[Copper Cable]]''' - 30 RF/t
* '''[[Gold Cable]]''' - 60 RF/t
* '''[[Ender Cable]]''' - 120 RF/t
