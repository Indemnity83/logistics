'''Chipsets''' are fabricated components built in the [[Sequential Fabricator]] from {{Mcw|Redstone Dust|redstone}} and one tier material. Each chipset is an alternative to the matching [[Gears|gear]] when crafting [[Modules|modules]] and [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipes]] — an automation-powered path that trades a machine and RF for the hand-crafted gear.

== Fabrication ==

Every chipset is made in the [[Sequential Fabricator]], which consumes redstone plus one tier material and a growing amount of [[RF Energy|RF]]. Select the chipset to build from the machine's GUI:

{{Sequential Fabricator|head=1 |Input=Redstone |Output=Redstone Chipset |notes=10,000 RF}}
{{Sequential Fabricator |Input=Redstone |Input2=Copper Ingot |Output=Copper Chipset |notes=15,000 RF}}
{{Sequential Fabricator |Input=Redstone |Input2=Iron Ingot |Output=Iron Chipset |notes=20,000 RF}}
{{Sequential Fabricator |Input=Redstone |Input2=Gold Ingot |Output=Gold Chipset |notes=30,000 RF}}
{{Sequential Fabricator |Input=Redstone |Input2=Echo Shard |Output=Echo Chipset |notes=40,000 RF}}
{{Sequential Fabricator |Input=Redstone |Input2=Diamond |Output=Diamond Chipset |notes=80,000 RF}}
{{Sequential Fabricator|foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Netherite Ingot |Output=Netherite Chipset |notes=120,000 RF}}

The [[Copper Chipset]] and [[Echo Chipset]] are not used in any recipe yet — they are reserved for future content.
