The '''Tin Gear''' was a crafting component in the [[Gears|gear]] progression, crafted from four tin ingots around a [[Copper Gear]]. The gear chain now runs straight from the Copper Gear to the [[Iron Gear]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

It was crafted from four [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]] around a [[Copper Gear]]:

|showname=0
|B1=Tin Ingot
|A2=Tin Ingot |B2=Copper Gear |C2=Tin Ingot
|B3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Tin Gear
}}
