'''Tin Ingot''' is a metal obtained by smelting [[Tin Ore|raw tin]]. It's used to create bronze alloy and tin-based components.

==Recipe==

'''Smelting:'''
* Raw Tin (from [[Tin Ore]]) → Tin Ingot
* Furnace or blast furnace

==Usage==

'''Bronze Alloy:'''
* Tin + Copper → [[Bronze Ingot]]

'''[[Kiln]] Crafting:'''
* [[Tin Valve]] - 5× tin ingot required

'''Gears:'''
* [[Tin Gear]] - 4× tin ingot

'''Storage:'''
* 9× Tin Ingot → Tin Block

==Tips==

* Common metal, easy to obtain
* Essential for bronze production
* Needed for tin valve crafting
* Stock up for gear and valve production
