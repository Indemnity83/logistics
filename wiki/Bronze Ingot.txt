'''Bronze Ingot''' is an alloy created from copper and tin. It's used for the [[Machine Core]], [[Sturdy Casing]], bronze valves, and gears.

'''Related forms:''' Bronze has a full storage set — '''Block of Bronze''' (9 ingots) and '''Bronze Nugget''' (1/9 ingot) — plus '''Bronze Dust''' from the [[Macerator]].

==Usage==

'''Machine components:'''
* [[Machine Core]] - 8× bronze ingot
* [[Sturdy Casing]] - 8× bronze ingot

'''Valves:'''
* [[Materials#Bronze Valve|Bronze Valve]] - via a Bronze [[Cores|Core]]

'''Gears:'''
* [[Bronze Gear]] - 4× bronze ingot

'''Dust:'''
* Grind in the [[Macerator]] → Bronze Dust (see [[Dusts]])

'''Storage:'''
* 9× Bronze Ingot → Block of Bronze
* 9× Bronze Nugget → 1× Bronze Ingot

==Related Items==

* '''Block of Bronze''' - Compact storage (9 ingots)
* '''Bronze Nugget''' - 1/9 of an ingot
* '''Bronze Dust''' - Ground form from the [[Macerator]] (see [[Dusts]])

==Tips==

* Alloy of copper + tin
* Used for mid-tier components and machine crafting
* Stock up for mid-tier progression
