A '''block of bronze''' is a storage block equivalent to nine [[Bronze Ingot|bronze ingots]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

A block of bronze can be mined only with a stone pickaxe or better. Mined without a pickaxe it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

A block of bronze stores bronze ingots compactly. It can be unpacked back into nine ingots at any time.
