The '''Copper Cable''' is the basic [[RF Energy|RF]] power cable. It carries energy from an [[Engines & Power|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or other machines, letting power be routed across a distance instead of relying on a direct face-to-face connection.

'''Yields:''' 8× Copper Cable

==Behavior==

* Transfers RF from a connected [[Engines & Power|engine]] to a [[Battery]] or machine
* Transfer rate: '''30 RF/t''' — the slowest of the three cable tiers
* Cables connect to each other and to adjacent power blocks, forming a power network
* The cheapest cable; use [[Gold Cable]] or [[Ender Cable]] when you need more throughput

==Cable Tiers==

The three cables behave identically except for how much RF they can move per tick (Copper < Gold < Ender):

* '''[[Copper Cable]]''' - 30 RF/t
* '''[[Gold Cable]]''' - 60 RF/t
* '''[[Ender Cable]]''' - 120 RF/t
