The '''Bypass Fluid Pipe''' connects only to other fluid pipes, never to tanks or machines. It is the fluid-side counterpart to the [[Item Passthrough Pipe]], used to route a fluid line past a fluid handler without drawing from or filling it.

Fluid pipes form a '''standalone''' transport system, separate from the item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]: each connected line carries one fluid type and moves it by pressure equalization. Passive transport is free — no power required.

'''Yields:''' 1× Bypass Fluid Pipe (shapeless: [[Item Passthrough Pipe]] + Slime Ball)

==Behavior==

* '''Connects only to other fluid pipes''', never to tanks or machines
* Runs a fluid line straight past an adjacent fluid handler without inserting into or extracting from it
* Passive transport is '''free''' (no RF)
* Carries '''one fluid type''' per connected line

==Tips==
* Use to route a pipe across the face of a tank or machine you don't want it to touch
* Lets you keep a clean transport run through a crowded build without unwanted connections
* Where you ''do'' want a controlled connection, use a [[Copper Fluid Pipe]] (connects to everything) or an [[Insertion Fluid Pipe]] (fills neighbors first)
