The '''Creative Engine''' is a power source that produces an unlimited supply of [[RF Energy|RF]] for testing and creative-mode builds. It lets you power a [[Battery]], the logistics network, or any machine without worrying about fuel or engine output.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Creative Engine can be broken with any tool, though a pickaxe is fastest.

{{Breaking
|tier=none
|name1=Creative Engine |icon1=Grid Creative Engine.png |hardness1=5
}}

'''Creative only — not craftable.''' The Creative Engine has no crafting recipe and is intended for testing and creative mode.

== Usage ==

* Outputs a steady, configurable amount of RF every tick — effectively infinite power
* Never runs out of fuel and never overheats
* Output is delivered through [[Copper Cable|power cables]] just like the [[Redstone Engine]] and [[Stirling Engine]]
* Useful for charging a [[Battery]] instantly while testing a logistics network
