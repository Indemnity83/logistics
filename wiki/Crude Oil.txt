'''Crude Oil''' is a dark liquid found in surface seeps and locked inside oil-bearing blocks. Unlike the mod's other custom fluids it can be '''placed in the world''', picked up in a bucket, and pooled — it flows sluggishly, spreading a short distance before dropping off.

== Obtaining ==

=== Oil seeps ===

Crude Oil pools naturally in rare '''oil seeps''' — small surface lakes that generate across the Overworld, ringed by [[Oil Sand]], [[Oil Red Sand]], or [[Oil Shale]] depending on the biome. Scoop it up with a bucket or a [[Pump]].

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] melts oil-bearing blocks and [[Bitumen]] into Crude Oil:

{{Crucible|head=1 |Input=Oil Sand;Oil Red Sand;Oil Shale |ingredients=[[Oil Sand]], [[Oil Red Sand]], or [[Oil Shale]] |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=1000}}
{{Crucible |foot=1 |Input=Bitumen |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=250}}

== Usage ==

Crude Oil has no processing use yet — it is collected, piped, and stored. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Bucket ===

The '''Crude Oil Bucket''' places and picks up the fluid like a water or lava bucket, and empties back to an ordinary bucket when poured into a tank or machine.
