A '''block of echonite''' is a storage block equivalent to nine [[Echonite Ingot|echonite ingots]]. It is also the core of a [[Batteries|Deep Battery]], so it is a crafting component as much as a storage block.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

A block of echonite can be mined only with a stone pickaxe or better. Mined without one it drops nothing.
