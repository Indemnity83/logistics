'''Netherite Chipset''' is a fabricated component built in the [[Sequential Fabricator]]. Chipsets are an alternative to [[Gears|gears]] for crafting [[Modules|modules]] and [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipes]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Sequential Fabricator ===

{{Sequential Fabricator|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Netherite Ingot |Output=Netherite Chipset |notes=120,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

A Netherite Chipset can stand in for its gear when crafting:

* [[Chassis Logistics Pipe MK5]]
