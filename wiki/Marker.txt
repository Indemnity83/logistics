The '''Marker''' is a placement aid used to define a custom mining area for the [[Laser Quarry]]. Place markers to outline the region you want to excavate instead of relying on the quarry's default 16×16 footprint.

==Recipe==

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Lapis Dust
|A2=Stick
|Output=Marker |OutputCount=2
}}

'''Yields:''' 2× Marker (1× [[Dusts|Lapis Dust]] over 1× Stick)

==Behavior==

Markers work together to mark out a rectangular mining boundary:

* Place '''three markers in a right-triangle (L-shape)''' to define two perpendicular edges of the area
* When activated, the markers project the resulting rectangular boundary
* The area must meet a '''minimum size'''; too-small layouts are rejected
* A successful activation reports the defined area size (e.g. ''area defined: 9x9'')
* A [[Laser Quarry]] placed against the marked boundary mines that custom region

Markers will refuse activation if there are not three markers in a right triangle, or if the resulting area is below the minimum size.

==Setup==

# '''Place markers''' in a right-triangle (L-shape) to outline two edges of the desired area
# '''Activate''' the markers to define the rectangular boundary
# '''Place a [[Laser Quarry]]''' adjacent to the marked boundary
# The quarry uses the marked area instead of the default 16×16 footprint
# Markers are consumed/returned when the quarry takes over the area

==Tips==

* Use markers to avoid mining your base, important terrain, or other quarries
* Lapis Dust comes from the [[Macerator]] (grind Lapis Ore or Lapis Block), so set up processing first
* Without markers the [[Laser Quarry]] simply mines its default 16×16 area
