'''Slag''' is the low-grade waste left over from processing in an [[Alloy Smelter]]. It is not useless — combined with [[Pulped Biomass]] it recycles back into dirt.

== Obtaining ==

Slag drops as a chance byproduct from many [[Alloy Smelter]] recipes — dust smelting, bronze from dust, stone bricks, and most metal-item recycling. Feeding an ore alongside [[Rich Slag]] also leaves ordinary Slag behind.

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ===

Slag and [[Pulped Biomass]] combine into dirt:
