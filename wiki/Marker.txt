The '''Marker''' is a placement aid used to define a custom mining area for the [[Laser Quarry]]. Place markers to outline the region you want to excavate instead of relying on the quarry's default 16×16 footprint.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Lapis Dust
|A2=Stick
|Output=Marker |OutputCount=2
}}

'''Yields:''' 2× Marker (1× [[Dusts|Lapis Dust]] over 1× Stick). Lapis Dust comes from the [[Macerator]] (grind Lapis Ore or Lapis Block), so set up processing first.

== Usage ==

Markers work together to mark out a rectangular mining boundary for the [[Laser Quarry]].

=== Defining an area ===

# '''Place three markers in a right-triangle (L-shape)''' to outline two perpendicular edges of the desired area.
# '''Activate''' the markers to project the resulting rectangular boundary.
# '''Place a [[Laser Quarry]]''' adjacent to the marked boundary; it mines the custom region instead of the default 16×16 footprint.

The area must meet a '''minimum size'''; too-small layouts are rejected. A successful activation reports the defined area size (e.g. ''area defined: 9x9''). Markers refuse activation if there are not three markers in a right triangle, or if the resulting area is below the minimum size.

=== Tips ===

* Use markers to avoid mining your base, important terrain, or other quarries.
* Without markers the [[Laser Quarry]] simply mines its default 16×16 area.
