'''Machines''' perform tasks automatically using RF energy.

==Mining==
* '''[[Laser Quarry]]''' - Automatically mines 16×16 area below, requires RF power

==Processing==
* '''[[Macerator]]''' - Grinds items into dust; 70+ recipes covering ores, plants, and dyes
* '''[[Kiln]]''' - RF-powered electric furnace; processes any vanilla smelting recipe
