'''Tin Ore''' is a common ore found underground. Smelt it to create [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]] for crafting bronze and tin valves.

==Obtaining==

'''Mining:'''
* Found underground in the Overworld
* Y-level distribution: (TBD - similar to copper/iron)
* Drops: Raw Tin
* Requires stone pickaxe or better

'''Deepslate variant:'''
* Deepslate Tin Ore
* Found in deepslate layers
* Same drops, harder to mine

==Usage==

'''Smelting:'''
* Raw Tin → [[Tin Ingot]]
* Used in furnaces or blast furnaces

'''Storage:'''
* Raw Tin Block (9× raw tin)
* Compact storage

==Tips==

* Mine with fortune pickaxe for more raw tin
* Common ore - easy to find
* Needed for bronze alloy ([[Bronze Ingot]])
* Required for [[Tin Valve]]

* [[Bronze Ingot]] - Tin + copper alloy
