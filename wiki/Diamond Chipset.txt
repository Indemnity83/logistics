'''Diamond Chipset''' is a fabricated component built in the [[Sequential Fabricator]]. Chipsets are an alternative to [[Gears|gears]] for crafting [[Modules|modules]] and [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipes]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Sequential Fabricator ===

{{Sequential Fabricator|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Diamond |Output=Diamond Chipset |notes=80,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

A Diamond Chipset can stand in for its gear when crafting:

* [[Chassis Logistics Pipe MK4]]
* [[Crafter Module|Crafter MKIII Module]]
* [[Extractor Module|Extractor Module MKIII]]
* [[Provider Module|Provider MKII Module]]
* [[Quicksort Module]]
* [[Requester Logistics Pipe]]
