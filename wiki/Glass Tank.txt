The '''Glass Tank''' is a fluid storage block that buffers liquids for a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid network]]. It holds '''16,000 mB''' (16 buckets) of a single fluid type, and stacked tanks merge into one larger reservoir.

'''Yields:''' 1× Glass Tank (a ring of 8 Glass)

==Behavior==

* Stores '''16,000 mB (16 buckets)''' of one fluid type
* Accepts insert and extract on all faces — connect a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] to any side
* '''Stack vertically to merge''': tanks placed in a column form one logical reservoir whose capacity is the sum of all tanks, filling '''bottom-up'''
* A dynamic block-entity renderer shows the fluid's actual sprite and color at its current fill height through the glass
* Fill or drain by hand with a bucket (and other fluid-container items) — right-click to add or remove fluid

==Tips==
* Stack several tanks to build a tall reservoir — "scale is the feature," done as tileable blocks rather than a multiblock
* Buffer the output of a [[Pump]] or [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] before feeding it onward
* Because each tank holds one fluid type, keep separate columns for different fluids
* Use an [[Insertion Fluid Pipe]] or [[Merger Fluid Pipe]] to control how fluid enters the tank
