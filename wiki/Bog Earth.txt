'''Bog Earth''' is a waterlogged soil that generates in swamps. Dried in a furnace it becomes [[Peat]], a renewable fuel.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Bog Earth breaks fastest with a shovel and always drops itself.

{{Breaking
|tool=shovel
|tier=none
|name1=Bog Earth |icon1=Grid Bog Earth.png |hardness1=0.5
}}

=== Natural generation ===

Bog Earth generates in '''swamp''' biomes, spreading in patches through the mud, dirt, and grass at the water's edge. Because swamps keep generating it, Bog Earth — and the [[Peat]] made from it — is renewable.
