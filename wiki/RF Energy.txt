'''RF (Redstone Flux)''' is the energy system used by Logistics. [[Engines]] generate RF, [[Copper Cable|cables]] distribute it, and machines and the logistics network consume it to operate.

There are '''two separate power paths''' in the mod — they do not share a power source:

==Path 1: Powering the logistics network==

The logistics network is powered from a stored reservoir, not directly off an engine:

# An [[Engines|engine]] ([[Redstone Engine]], [[Stirling Engine]], or [[Creative Engine]]) generates RF
# [[Copper Cable|Power cables]] carry that RF to a [[Battery]]
# The [[Logistics Pipes]] and chassis modules draw RF from the connected Battery each time they route, supply, or craft

'''When the network runs out of power:'''
* Routing, supplying, and crafting all stop
* Items already in transit are '''dropped'''
* Logistics pipes render '''green when powered''' and '''red when not''', so you can see network status at a glance

Power is restored as soon as a charged [[Battery]] is connected again.

==Path 2: Extraction pacing==

Extraction pipes do '''not''' use the Battery. Instead, each is paced by an '''adjacent engine''':

* The [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] pull faster the more RF the adjacent engine supplies
* This is the original, mechanical power model — the engine touches the pipe directly
* No Battery or logistics network is involved

==Cables==

Cables carry RF from an engine to a [[Battery]] or machine. They connect to each other and to adjacent power blocks, and differ only in throughput (Copper < Gold < Ender):

* '''[[Copper Cable]]''' - 30 RF/t
* '''[[Gold Cable]]''' - 60 RF/t
* '''[[Ender Cable]]''' - 120 RF/t

==Engine Types==

Different engines generate different amounts of RF:

'''[[Redstone Engine]]:'''
* Small, steady output
* Powered by redstone signal only
* Cannot overheat
* Good for low-power needs

'''[[Stirling Engine]]:'''
* Substantial output
* Powered by fuel (coal, charcoal, lava, etc.)
* Can overheat if run too long
* Good for high-power machines like quarries

'''[[Creative Engine]]:'''
* Infinite output for testing and creative mode
* Never runs out of fuel, never overheats
* Not craftable

==Tips==

* Keep a charged [[Battery]] on the network at all times — an empty Battery drops in-transit items
* Use higher-throughput cables ([[Gold Cable]], [[Ender Cable]]) to feed a high-demand network
* Engines require a redstone signal to run
* Place an engine directly against an [[Item Extractor Pipe]] to pace its extraction
