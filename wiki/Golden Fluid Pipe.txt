The '''Golden Fluid Pipe''' is the fastest passive [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] — 80 mB/t. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Golden Transport Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Seal the [[Golden Transport Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime ball}} or [[Tar]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|showname=0
|A1=Golden Transport Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Golden Fluid Pipe
}}
{{Crafting
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Golden Transport Pipe |B1=Tar
|Output=Golden Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

Golden fluid pipes passively carry fluid at '''80 mB/t''', eight times a copper pipe. Use them on high-demand runs; the slowest pipe on a line still caps the whole line's throughput.
