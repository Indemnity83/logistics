# Tin Ore

**Tin Ore** is a common ore found underground. Smelt it to create [tin ingots](tin-ingot.md) for crafting bronze and tin valves.

## Obtaining

**Mining:**
- Found underground in the Overworld
- Y-level distribution: (TBD - similar to copper/iron)
- Drops: Raw Tin
- Requires stone pickaxe or better

**Deepslate variant:**
- Deepslate Tin Ore
- Found in deepslate layers
- Same drops, harder to mine

## Usage

**Smelting:**
- Raw Tin → [Tin Ingot](tin-ingot.md)
- Used in furnaces or blast furnaces

**Storage:**
- Raw Tin Block (9× raw tin)
- Compact storage

## Tips

- Mine with fortune pickaxe for more raw tin
- Common ore - easy to find
- Needed for bronze alloy ([Bronze Ingot](bronze-ingot.md))
- Required for [Tin Valve](valve-tin.md)
