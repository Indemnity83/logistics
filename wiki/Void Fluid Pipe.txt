The '''Void Fluid Pipe''' is a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that destroys any fluid reaching it. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Item Void Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Combine the [[Item Void Pipe]] with a Slime Ball (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Void Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Void Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

The Void Fluid Pipe destroys any fluid that flows into it, acting as a bottomless sink. Because a fluid line always flows toward a sink, place it deliberately so it doesn't drain tanks you meant to keep.
