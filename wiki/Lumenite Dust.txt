'''Lumenite Dust''' is [[Amethyst Dust|amethyst dust]] infused with [[Liquid Glowstone]] in a [[Transposer]]. It is the entry point to the [[Lumenite]] family — there is no ore and no other route in. Smelt it for a [[Lumenite Ingot]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Transposer ===

The [[Transposer]] draws 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Glowstone]] from its tank to infuse one dust:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Amethyst Dust |FluidIn=Liquid Glowstone |InAmount=1000 |Output=Lumenite Dust
|ingredients=One [[Amethyst Dust]] plus 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Glowstone]] in the tank
|notes=3,000 RF}}

=== Grinding ===

A [[Macerator]] recovers the dust from finished lumenite:

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0
|Input=Block of Lumenite
|Output=Lumenite Dust,9
}}

{{Grinding|showname=0|foot=1
|Input=Lumenite Ingot
|Output=Lumenite Dust,1
}}
