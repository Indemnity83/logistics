The '''Item Filter Pipe''' is the sorting [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] — it routes items by type.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of {{Mcw|Glass}} flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Diamond |B2=Glass |C2=Diamond
|Output=Item Filter Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

Each face of the Item Filter Pipe has its own item filter; right-click for the GUI. Matching items route to that face, unmatched items go to any unfiltered face, and items with nowhere to go drop.

It is the core of a sorting system — combine it with the [[Item Insertion Pipe]] for storage and the [[Item Void Pipe]] for overflow.
