The '''Laser Quarry''' is a powered mining machine that automatically excavates an area below it down to bedrock. It builds its own frame and mines continuously when powered with [[RF Energy|RF]]. By default it mines a 16×16 area, but you can use [[Marker|markers]] to define custom boundaries of any size.

Requires a [[Machine Core]], 5× Iron Ingot, and 3× [[Diamond Gear]].

== Usage ==

When placed and powered, the Laser Quarry constructs a mining frame around the target area, then excavates layer by layer down to bedrock, running indefinitely until it bottoms out. Mined blocks output from the top of the quarry into a chest or pipe. Mining speed scales with power throughput, and the quarry stops if power is interrupted.

=== Mining area ===

'''Default (no markers):''' mines a 16×16 area centered on the quarry's placement.

'''Custom area (with [[Marker|markers]]):'''

# Place three [[Marker|markers]] in a right-triangle (L-shape) to outline the area
# Activate the markers to define the rectangular boundary
# Place the quarry adjacent to the marked boundary
# The quarry consumes the markers and mines that boundary instead of the default 16×16

This lets you define exactly what area to mine, avoiding buildings, important terrain, or other quarries. See [[Marker]] for full placement rules.

=== Power ===

The Laser Quarry consumes [[RF Energy|RF]] continuously while mining. A [[Stirling Engine]] is the recommended source — connect its output face directly to the quarry. More RF throughput means faster mining; a well-fed quarry mines faster than a power-starved one, and the quarry stops entirely without power.

=== Setup ===

# '''Place the quarry''' at the desired location (mining happens below)
# '''Connect power''' — attach a [[Stirling Engine]] or other RF source
# '''Connect collection''' — place a chest or pipe on top to receive items
# '''Provide power''' — the frame builds, then mining begins automatically

=== Item collection ===

Mined items output from the top of the quarry into any connected inventory or pipe (no extractor needed).

<pre>
[Copper Transport Pipe] → [Filter Pipe] → [Storage]
         ↑
  [Laser Quarry]
</pre>

Use [[Item Filter Pipe|Item Filter Pipes]] to sort valuable ores and an [[Item Void Pipe]] to discard cobblestone and dirt overflow. Set up sorting before starting large-scale mining.

=== Jade HUD ===

With [https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade Jade] installed, looking at a Laser Quarry shows its live status in the HUD — including its working state and mining progress — so you can monitor it without opening the GUI.
