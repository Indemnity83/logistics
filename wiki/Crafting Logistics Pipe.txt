The '''Crafting Logistics Pipe''' is a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]] that crafts requested items on demand.

== Usage ==

The Crafting Logistics Pipe fulfills requests for items that aren't in stock by crafting them. Configure a 3×3 recipe in its GUI; when the output is requested, the pipe pulls the ingredients from providers, crafts, and delivers the result. Chain several to build multi-step crafting trees — one pipe can consume what another produces.

== Power ==

Like every [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]], it draws [[RF Energy|RF]] from a connected [[Battery]] — it renders '''green''' while powered and '''red''' when the network has no charge, in which case routing stops and items in transit drop.
