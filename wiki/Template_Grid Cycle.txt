A slot that cycles through several items, powered by [[Module:Cycle]]. Use it where a recipe accepts
more than one item in a position — e.g. an ore or its deepslate variant — or for the smelting "any
fuel" slot. One item renders as a plain icon; two to six cycle. Styling: [[Template:Grid Cycle/styles.css]].

Usage:
 {{Grid Cycle|Tin Ore|Deepslate Tin Ore}}

Empty positional parameters are ignored, so it is safe to pass optional slots:
 {{Grid Cycle|{{{Input}}}|{{{Input2|}}}|{{{Input3|}}}}}
