The '''Fluid Extractor Pipe''' is a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that pulls fluid out of an adjacent tank or machine. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Item Extractor Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Combine the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball}} (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Extractor Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Fluid Extractor Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

The Fluid Extractor Pipe pulls fluid from an adjacent tank or machine and pushes it down the line. Like the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] it must be driven by an adjacent engine — a [[Stirling Engine]] pulls faster than a [[Redstone Engine]], and a [[Creative Engine]] runs at full speed. With no engine it stops; passive pipes alone can't pull fluid from a handler. To draw fluid sources straight from the world, use a [[Fluid Pump|Pump]] instead.

A {{Mcw|Cauldron|cauldron}} counts as a tank: a powered Fluid Extractor Pipe drains one and leaves it empty. Cauldrons are all-or-nothing, though — they part with a whole level at a time, 1,000 mB for lava and 333⅓ mB for each of water's three levels — so the pipe needs room for a full level before it can take anything.
