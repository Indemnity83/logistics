Renders a prominent "removed content" notice box at the top of a page whose subject no longer
exists in the current version of the mod. The page body is kept (describing the item as it last
existed) but flagged as historical; pair this with <code>[[Category:Removed]]</code> and a final
<code>{{History}}</code> row noting the removal. Deliberately does '''not''' point at a
replacement — a removed item does not necessarily have one, and implying it is misleading.

Usage:
 {{Removed|v0.8.3|item}}
 {{Removed|v0.8.3|valve}}

Parameters:
 1 — the version the subject was removed in, e.g. "v0.8.3"
 2 — the noun for the subject: "item", "block", "machine", "valve", … (default: "item")

Styling lives in [[Template:Removed/styles.css]].
