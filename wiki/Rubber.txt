'''Rubber''' is a flexible crafting component used to insulate [[Copper Cable|power cables]]. It is produced in two steps: a '''Rubber Mix''' is crafted from raw ingredients, then smelted into a finished '''Rubber Chunk'''.

==Rubber Mix==

The Rubber Mix is the raw, unprocessed form.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Slime Ball |B1=Coal Dust
|Output=Rubber Mix
}}

'''Yields:''' 1× Rubber Mix (shapeless: Slime Ball + [[Dusts|Coal Dust]])

Coal Dust comes from the [[Macerator]] (grind Coal, Charcoal, or Coal Ore).

==Rubber Chunk==

Smelting cures the Rubber Mix into finished Rubber Chunks.

'''Smelting:''' Smelt 1× Rubber Mix in a [[Kiln]] or furnace → '''2× Rubber Chunk'''

==Usage==

Rubber Chunks insulate power cables:

* [[Copper Cable]] - Standard RF transport cable
* [[Gold Cable]] - Higher-rate cable
* [[Ender Cable]] - Highest-rate cable

==Tips==

* Smelting one Rubber Mix yields '''two''' Rubber Chunks, so set up a [[Macerator]] → [[Kiln]] line for cheap, bulk rubber
* Slime Balls are also used for [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] crafting — keep a slime farm running
