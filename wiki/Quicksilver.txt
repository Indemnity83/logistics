'''Quicksilver''' is a material extracted from cinnabar (and, occasionally, gold ore) in a [[Macerator]]. It serves as a premium flux in the [[Alloy Smelter]], boosting ore yields.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] grinds cinnabar and its variants into Quicksilver ''(Minecraft 26.2 and newer)''. Higher-volume blocks yield more:

{{Grinding|head=1 |Input=Cinnabar |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Polished Cinnabar |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Cinnabar Stairs |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |Input=Cinnabar Wall |Output=Quicksilver,4}}
{{Grinding |foot=1 |Input=Cinnabar Slab |Output=Quicksilver,2}}

Quicksilver also drops as a 10% byproduct when grinding [[Gold Dust|gold ore]].

== Usage ==

=== Ore processing ===

In the [[Alloy Smelter]], Quicksilver works as a flux that smelts an ore into '''three''' ingots and returns a bonus product — a gold ingot from copper, an iron ingot from tin, and extra [[Rich Slag]] from gold:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1 |Input=Copper Ore;Deepslate Copper Ore |Input2=Quicksilver |Output=Copper Ingot |OutputCount=3 |Byproduct=Gold Ingot |ByproductChance=100%}}
{{Alloy Smelter |Input=Tin Ore;Deepslate Tin Ore |Input2=Quicksilver |Output=Tin Ingot |OutputCount=3 |Byproduct=Iron Ingot |ByproductChance=100%}}
{{Alloy Smelter |foot=1 |Input=Gold Ore;Deepslate Gold Ore |Input2=Quicksilver |Output=Gold Ingot |OutputCount=3 |Byproduct=Rich Slag |ByproductChance=75%}}
