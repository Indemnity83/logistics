'''Tin ore''' is a mineral block found underground. It is a source of [[Raw Tin|raw tin]], which is smelted into [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]].

[[File:Grid Deepslate Tin Ore.png|32px|link=]] '''Deepslate tin ore''' is a variant that generates in the deepslate layer; it drops the same raw tin but is slightly harder to break.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Tin ore drops 2–5 '''[[Raw Tin|raw tin]]''' when mined with a stone pickaxe or better; the count is increased by Fortune. Mined with Silk Touch it drops itself instead. Deepslate tin ore behaves identically.

=== Natural generation ===

Tin ore generates in every Overworld biome during the underground-ore step, in veins of up to '''10''' blocks. It appears in two depth bands — the deepslate variant is far more abundant:

{| class="wikitable"
! Variant !! Height (Y) !! Distribution !! Veins per chunk
|-
| Tin Ore || 0 to 112 || trapezoid (peaks near Y 56) || 1
|-
| Deepslate Tin Ore || −16 to 0 || trapezoid (peaks near Y −8) || 13
|}

Veins generate normally even where exposed to air, so they show on cave walls.
