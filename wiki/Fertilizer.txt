'''Fertilizer''' is a farming material made from [[Apatite (gem)|apatite]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|showdescription=1
|name=Fertilizer |description=From the gem
|A1=Apatite |B1=Sand |C1=Sand
|Output=Fertilizer,4}}
{{Crafting|foot=1
|name=Fertilizer |description=From dusts, for double the yield
|A1=Apatite Dust |B1=Saltpeter |C1=Pulped Biomass
|Output=Fertilizer,8}}

Both recipes are shapeless. Any mod's apatite and saltpeter work, and any kind of sand.

== Usage ==

Fertilizer has no use yet. It shares the <code>#logistics:fertilizers</code> tag with {{Mcw|Bone Meal|bone meal}}.
