'''Gold Chipset''' is a fabricated component built in the [[Sequential Fabricator]]. Chipsets are an alternative to [[Gears|gears]] for crafting [[Modules|modules]] and [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipes]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Sequential Fabricator ===

{{Sequential Fabricator|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Gold Ingot |Output=Gold Chipset |notes=30,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

A Gold Chipset can stand in for its gear when crafting:

* [[Active Supplier Module]]
* [[Basic Logistics Pipe]]
* [[Chassis Logistics Pipe MK3]]
* [[Crafter Module|Crafter MKII Module]]
* [[Enchantment Sink Module]]
* [[Extractor Module|Extractor Module MKII]]
* [[Mod Item Sink Module]]
* [[Provider Logistics Pipe]]
* [[Provider Module]]
