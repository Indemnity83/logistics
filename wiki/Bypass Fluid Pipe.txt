The '''Bypass Fluid Pipe''' is a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that connects only to pipes, never tanks or machines. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Item Passthrough Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Combine the [[Item Passthrough Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball}} (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Passthrough Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Bypass Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

The Bypass Fluid Pipe connects only to other fluid pipes, never to tanks or machines. Use it to run a fluid line straight past a handler without filling or draining it.
