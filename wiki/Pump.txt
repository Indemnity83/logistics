The '''Pump''' (also called the '''Fluid Pump''') extracts fluid source blocks from the world and feeds them into an adjacent [[Glass Tank|tank]] or [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]]. It is the end-to-end consumer of the fluid system — the simplest way to get water or lava into your network.

'''Yields:''' 1× Pump (uses an [[Iron Gear]], a Bucket, two [[Glass Tank|Glass Tanks]], Iron Ingots, and Redstone)

==Behavior==

* '''Drains fluid source blocks''' from the world below it into its internal buffer
* Consumes '''100 RF per source block''' drained — no power, no pumping
* Holds a '''16,000 mB (16 buckets)''' internal buffer
* Outputs up to '''62.5 mB/t''' into an adjacent tank or fluid pipe
* Works with any vanilla or modded fluid source on both Fabric and NeoForge

==Power==
The Pump runs on RF and has its own internal buffer — it does '''not''' need an engine pressed against it the way a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] does. Supply it with RF from your power system to keep it draining; with no power it stops.

==Tips==
* Place a [[Glass Tank]] or [[Copper Fluid Pipe]] against the pump's output to carry away what it draws
* Buffer the output in stacked [[Glass Tank|tanks]] so the pump can keep draining even when the network is briefly full
* Use a [[Golden Fluid Pipe]] (80 mB/t) on the output if you need to move fluid faster than one pump produces
* To pull fluid out of a machine's tank instead of the world, use a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]
