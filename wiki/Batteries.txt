'''Batteries''' buffer [[RF Energy|RF]] on a power grid. [[Engines]] charge them through [[Cables]], and they discharge through cables to whatever needs power — most often a machine such as a [[Kiln]], [[Macerator]], or [[Sawmill]], or a [[Power Junction]]. They come in four tiers that behave identically apart from how much they hold and how fast they move it.

A battery is the grid's reserve rather than its first choice: on a shared cable, machines are served before batteries are topped up, and a battery gives only once the generators on that cable are spent.

== Variants ==

Every tier is the same shape — two [[Caterium Nugget|nuggets]] in the top corners, a storage block core, and {{Mcw|Clay Ball|clay balls}} filling the rest. The core sets the tier:

Each tier holds four times the charge of the one below it and moves it twice as fast, so a Deep Battery is worth sixty-four Basic Batteries of storage at eight times the throughput. The three upper cores are [[Caterium|infused alloys]], each made by infusing a dust with a fluid in a [[Transposer]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Every tier is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing. A broken battery keeps its stored charge.

== Usage ==

* Charge level is shown as a fill bar on the placed block and as a green charge bar on the item
* A bank of batteries on one cable is drawn from together, each contributing in proportion to what it can give, so the bank empties evenly rather than one cell at a time
* Batteries wired to the same cable level out: charge runs from a fuller cell to an emptier one and stops once their fill fractions match

To power a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]], feed a battery's RF into a [[Power Junction]] — pipes do not draw from a battery directly.

Note that extraction pipes (the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]) are paced by an adjacent '''engine''' instead, not by a battery.

=== Configuration ===

Each tier has its own config file, <code>config/logistics/power.battery.&lt;tier&gt;.json</code>, with the same three keys. Change them in-game with the operator-only <code>/logistics config power.battery.&lt;tier&gt; &lt;key&gt; &lt;value&gt;</code> command:

{{Config|head=1|capacity|Total RF storage.|100000 / 400000 / 1600000 / 6400000|Integer}}
{{Config|max_io|Max RF/t inserted or extracted per side.|1000 / 2000 / 4000 / 8000|Integer}}
{{Config|foot=1|output_per_side|Max RF/t actively pushed into each adjacent machine.|200 / 400 / 800 / 1600|Integer}}
