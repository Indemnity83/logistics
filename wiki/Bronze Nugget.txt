'''Bronze Nugget''' is one-ninth of a [[Bronze Ingot]], handy for fine crafting and storage.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

One [[Bronze Ingot]] unpacks into nine Bronze Nuggets, and nine nuggets craft back into one ingot.
