The '''Steam Engine''' is a boiler that burns solid fuel to heat water into steam, building pressure that it converts into [[RF Energy|RF]]. It needs both a fuel supply and a steady feed of water.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Steam Engine is mined with an iron pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

Load solid fuel into the firebox and pipe water into the boiler with a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]]. The firebox heats the boiler; once it reaches boiling temperature the water flashes to steam and pressure climbs. At its operating pressure the engine outputs a flat '''40 RF/t''' from a configurable face. Output stops if it runs out of either fuel or water, and the boiler slowly cools when idle. A [[Jade]] readout shows the firebox state, boiler heat, pressure, and output.

=== Fuel ===

The firebox accepts any furnace fuel — coal, charcoal, wood, and the mod's [[Peat]], [[Bitumen]], and [[Tar]]. Water is drawn from its internal tank (4,000 mB), so keep it fed from a [[Fluid Pump|Pump]] or [[Glass Tank]].

=== Configuration ===

Use a [[Wrench]] to rotate the output face.
