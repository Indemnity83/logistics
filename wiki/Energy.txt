'''Energy''' covers the engines, cables, storage, and RF power system in Logistics. Engines generate RF energy, cables distribute it, a [[Battery]] stores it, and a [[Power Junction]] feeds it into the logistics network. All engines require a direct redstone signal to operate and output energy from one configurable face.

==Energy System==
* '''[[RF Energy]]''' - Understanding the power system

==Engines==
RF is generated by three '''[[Engines]]''' — the [[Redstone Engine]] (small, steady), the [[Stirling Engine]] (fuel-burning, high output), and the [[Creative Engine]] (infinite, creative-only).

==Cables==
Cables distribute RF from engines to a [[Battery]], [[Power Junction]], or machine at increasing transfer rates:
* '''[[Copper Cable]]''' - 30 RF/t
* '''[[Gold Cable]]''' - 60 RF/t
* '''[[Ender Cable]]''' - 120 RF/t

==Storage==
* '''[[Battery]]''' - Stores RF for later use

==Network input==
* '''[[Power Junction]]''' - The single point where power enters a logistics network

==Powering the logistics network==

The [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]] is powered only through a [[Power Junction]]. An [[Engines|engine]] generates RF, [[Cables|cables]] carry it (optionally by way of a [[Battery]]) to a Power Junction placed against the network, and the pipes draw from the junction to route, supply, and craft items. Logistics pipes render '''green when powered''' and '''red when not''' — an unpowered network stops working and drops items already in transit until power returns.

Note that the extraction pipes (the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]) are paced by an adjacent engine instead, not the network Battery.
