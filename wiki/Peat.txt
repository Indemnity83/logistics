'''Peat''' is a solid fuel dried from [[Bog Earth]] in a furnace. It burns longer than coal, and because bog earth regenerates in swamps it is a renewable fuel source.

== Obtaining ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt [[Bog Earth]] in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]] to dry it into Peat:

{{Smelting|head=1|foot=1
|name=Peat
|Input=Bog Earth
|Output=Peat}}

== Usage ==

=== Fuel ===

Peat is a furnace fuel and powers the [[Stirling Engine]]. Each piece burns for 2,000 ticks — longer than coal's 1,600 — smelting 10 items.
