'''Caterium nuggets''' are pieces of caterium worth one-ninth of a [[Caterium Ingot|caterium ingot]]. Two of them trim the corners of a [[Batteries|Conductive Battery]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1
|name=Caterium Nugget
|B2=Caterium Ingot
|Output=Caterium Nugget,9
}}

== Usage ==

Nine nuggets craft back into a [[Caterium Ingot|caterium ingot]].
