'''Caterium Dust''' is [[Gold Dust|gold dust]] infused with [[Liquid Redstone]] in a [[Transposer]]. It is the entry point to the [[Caterium]] family — there is no ore and no other route in. Smelt it for a [[Caterium Ingot]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Transposer ===

The [[Transposer]] draws 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Redstone]] from its tank to infuse one dust:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Gold Dust |FluidIn=Liquid Redstone |InAmount=1000 |Output=Caterium Dust
|ingredients=One [[Gold Dust]] plus 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Redstone]] in the tank
|notes=2,000 RF}}

=== Grinding ===

A [[Macerator]] recovers the dust from finished caterium:

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0
|Input=Block of Caterium
|Output=Caterium Dust,9
}}

{{Grinding|showname=0|foot=1
|Input=Caterium Ingot
|Output=Caterium Dust,1
}}
