The '''Redstone Engine''' is a basic power generator that converts a redstone signal into a small, steady [[RF Energy|RF]] output. It is the simplest engine in Logistics and cannot overheat.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Redstone Engine is mined fastest with an axe, but breaks with any tool.

== Usage ==

The Redstone Engine generates RF while powered by a redstone signal (lever, torch, redstone dust, etc.). Energy outputs from one configurable face and transfers directly to the adjacent machine. It has no GUI and no fuel — just place, point, and power. It cannot overheat, so it is always safe to leave running.

=== Configuration ===

Use a [[Wrench]] to set the output face: right-click the engine and the output face cycles through directions. The face touching the machine must be the engine's output face.

=== Power output ===

The Redstone Engine produces low RF, suitable for light-duty machines, early-game automation, and testing setups. For high-power needs like the [[Laser Quarry]], use a [[Stirling Engine]] instead. In creative mode, the [[Creative Engine]] supplies unlimited RF with no fuel or redstone.
