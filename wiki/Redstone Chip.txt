'''Redstone Chip''' is an electronic [[Chips|chip]] used to craft [[Modules|modules]], built on a [[Chips#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Wafer]] base.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

|A3=Ender Dust |B3=Silicon Wafer |C3=Ender Dust
|Output=Redstone Chip,4
}}
