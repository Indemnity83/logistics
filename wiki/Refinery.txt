The '''Refinery''' is an RF-powered machine that distills one [[Fluids|fluid]] into another, drawing from an input tank and depositing the product in a separate output tank. It refines [[Liquid Biomass]] into [[Bio Fuel]] and [[Crude Oil]] into [[Fuel Oil]], venting [[Tar]] as a byproduct.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Refinery is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

Shares the standard machine base — a [[Machine Frame]], a [[Redstone Reception Coil]], and two [[Copper Gear|copper gears]] — with a {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}} and two [[Glass Tank|glass tanks]] on top.

== Usage ==

Pipe a fluid into the input tank; the Refinery processes it over time and deposits the product into its output tank, from which a [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]] or [[Fluid Pump|Pump]] can draw it. Recipes that carry a byproduct also drop a solid item into the machine's output. Every recipe appears in the Just Enough Items (JEI) viewer.

=== Power ===

The Refinery runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 20,000 RF and accepts up to 128 RF/t, spending 20 RF/t while working. Each recipe costs a total of 5,000 RF; connect a [[Stirling Engine]] or any RF source. Its input tank holds 4,000 mB and its output tank 10,000 mB.
