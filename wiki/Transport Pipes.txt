'''Transport Pipes''' are the item-carrying pipes of the Logistics mod. They move items between inventories and form the backbone of a network: every transport pipe connects to the others and — except the [[Item Passthrough Pipe]] — to adjacent inventories. They differ in speed and in the one special job each one does. For network-aware routing (requests, crafting, stock-keeping) see [[Logistics Pipes]]; for fluids see [[Fluid Pipes]].

== Usage ==

Transport pipes connect to each other and to adjacent inventories automatically. At a junction with several exits, an item picks a direction at '''random''', so a plain network spreads items unpredictably — the [[Item Merger Pipe|merger]], [[Item Filter Pipe|filter]], and [[Item Insertion Pipe|insertion]] pipes override that when you need control. Speed varies by material: [[Stone Transport Pipe|stone]] is very slow, [[Copper Transport Pipe|copper]] is the standard, and a powered [[Golden Transport Pipe|golden]] pipe is fastest.

[[Marking Fluid]] color-codes [[Copper Transport Pipe|copper pipes]] so that same-color pipes connect, different colors don't, and unmarked pipes connect to anything — letting independent networks share the same space.
