'''Gears''' are crafting components used throughout the mod's machines, engines, and [[Logistics Pipes]]. They form a progression chain: every gear after the wooden one is crafted from four of its tier material around the previous gear, so building a high-tier gear means climbing the whole ladder.

== Usage ==

Gears are ingredients, not placeable blocks. Machines, engines, and logistics pipes call for a gear of an appropriate tier — for example, a [[Logistics Pipes#Basic Logistics Pipe|Basic Logistics Pipe]] needs a Gold Gear, and the engines and processing machines use the lower tiers. Because each gear contains the one below it, keeping a stock of the cheaper gears makes the expensive ones quicker to build.
