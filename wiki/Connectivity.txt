Pipes and cables connect automatically to adjacent blocks based on simple rules, forming the networks that carry items, fluids, and power. Understanding connectivity helps you build clean, organized systems.

==Connection Rules==

Each transport system connects automatically within its own '''family''' — to others of its kind, and to the things it moves to or from:

* '''Item pipes''' connect to other item pipes ([[Transport Pipes]] and [[Logistics Pipes]]) and to inventories — chests, furnaces, hoppers, barrels, and any mod block exposing item storage.
* '''Fluid pipes''' connect to other [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipes]] and to tanks and fluid-handling machines.
* '''[[Cables]]''' connect to other cables and to power blocks — [[Redstone Engine|engines]], a [[Battery]], a [[Power Junction]], and the machines that draw [[RF Energy|RF]].

The three families are '''independent''': item pipes, fluid pipes, and cables each form their own network and never connect to one another, even sitting side by side. None of them connect to solid or non-handler blocks, or to air.

==Visual Indicators==

A connected face shows a '''connector''' extending toward the neighbor; no connector means no connection on that face.

'''Pipe connectors:'''
* '''Transparent''' — connects to another pipe
* '''Opaque''' — connects to a handler (inventory or tank), or marks an active face (merger/extractor pipes)

[[Cables]] connect the same way, but render as a thin wire that runs to each adjacent cable or power block rather than a boxy connector — the form looks different, the auto-connecting behavior is the same.

==Special Connection Behaviors==

Some pipes override the default "connect to everything compatible" behavior:

'''[[Item Passthrough Pipe]]''' (and the fluid [[Bypass Fluid Pipe]]):
* Connect only to other pipes, never to handlers
* Use to run a line past a chest, tank, or machine without tapping it

'''[[Item Merger Pipe]]:'''
* Output face marked with an opaque connector; items cannot enter through it
* Configure the output direction with a [[Wrench]]

'''[[Item Extractor Pipe]]:'''
* Extraction face marked with an opaque connector; only one face extracts
* Configure the extraction face with a [[Wrench]]

==Network Segmentation==

To keep two separate networks of the same family from merging:

'''[[Marking Fluid]]''' (item pipes only):
* Color-code [[Copper Transport Pipe|Copper Transport Pipes]]
* Pipes with different colors won't connect to each other
* A colored pipe still connects to uncolored pipes normally
* Sneak + empty hand removes the marking

'''Physical separation''' (works for pipes and cables alike):
* Place a non-connecting block between the two networks, or leave a gap
* Build the systems apart
