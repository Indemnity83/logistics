# Diamond Valve

The **Diamond Valve** is a Tier 4 valve crafted in the [Kiln](../automation/kiln.md). It requires lava buckets for reliable operation.

**[Kiln](../automation/kiln.md) crafting:**
- 5× Diamond + 2× Redstone + 250mb Molten Glass
- **Process time:** 220 ticks
- **Energy demand:** 240 energy/tick
- **Yields:** 4× Diamond Valve

## Fuel Requirements

**Tier 4 - Lava recommended**

Energy demand: 240 energy/tick
- **Blaze Rod:** **Insufficient** (~20-25 capacity)
- **Lava Bucket:** Recommended (~75 capacity provides margin)

Lava buckets required.

## Usage

Valves are crafting components for future features. Diamond valves are expensive due to diamond cost.

## Tips

- Requires 5 diamonds per craft
- Lava buckets strongly recommended
- Expensive but yields 4 valves
- Plan diamond usage carefully
