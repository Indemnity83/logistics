# Apatite Ore

**Apatite Ore** is an ore that drops [apatite](apatite.md) gems. Used for apatite valves and advanced components.

## Obtaining

**Mining:**
- Found underground in the Overworld
- Y-level distribution: (TBD)
- Drops: Apatite (gem)
- Requires iron pickaxe or better (likely)

## Usage

**Direct drops:**
- Drops [Apatite](apatite.md) gems directly when mined
- No smelting needed

**Fortune:**
- Fortune enchantment increases apatite drops

**Storage:**
- Apatite Block (9× apatite)

## Tips

- Drops gems directly (like diamond/emerald)
- Fortune pickaxe recommended
- Needed for [Apatite Valve](valve-apatite.md)
- Stock up for Tier 3 valve production
