'''Apatite''' is a gem material found underground in the Overworld. It is mined from apatite ore, which drops the gem directly, and is used mainly to craft the [[Apatite Valve]].

== Blocks and items ==

* [[Apatite Ore]] — an ore found underground that drops apatite when mined
* [[Apatite (gem)|Apatite]] — the gem dropped by apatite ore
* '''Block of Apatite''' — a block used for compact apatite storage
* [[Dusts|Apatite Dust]] — produced by grinding apatite ore in a [[Macerator]]

== Uses ==

* [[Apatite Valve]] — the gem's main use, crafted via an Apatite [[Cores|Core]]
