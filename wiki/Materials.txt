'''Materials''' are the crafting components, ores, and metals used throughout Logistics. Browse them by family.

== Crafting components ==

* [[File:Grid Iron Gear.png|24px|link=Gears]] '''[[Gears]]''' — the gear progression, Wooden through Netherite
* [[File:Grid Diamond Core.png|24px|link=Cores]] '''[[Cores]]''' — the cores that craft valves
* [[File:Grid Bronze Valve.png|24px|link=Valves]] '''[[Valves]]''' — the valves that build logistics pipes
* [[File:Grid Redstone Chip.png|24px|link=Chips]] '''[[Chips]]''' — electronic components for modules
* [[File:Grid Iron Dust.png|24px|link=Dusts]] '''[[Dusts]]''' — ground materials from the [[Macerator]]
* [[File:Grid Quartz Crystal.png|24px|link=Quartz Crystal]] '''[[Quartz Crystal]]''' — the frame of every valve
* [[File:Grid Machine Frame.png|24px|link=Machine Frame]] '''[[Machine Frame]]''' and '''[[Redstone Reception Coil]]''' — machine components
* [[File:Grid Rubber Chunk.png|24px|link=Rubber]] '''[[Rubber]]''' — insulates power cables

== Ores & metals ==

* [[File:Grid Tin Ingot.png|24px|link=Tin]] '''[[Tin]]''' — ore, ingot, nugget, storage blocks, and dust
* [[File:Grid Bronze Ingot.png|24px|link=Bronze]] '''[[Bronze]]''' — the copper-and-tin alloy
* [[File:Grid Apatite.png|24px|link=Apatite]] '''[[Apatite]]''' — the gem and its ore

== Oil & fuels ==

* [[File:Grid Oil Sand.png|24px|link=Oil Sand]] '''[[Oil Sand]]''', '''[[Oil Red Sand]]''', '''[[Oil Shale]]''' — oil-bearing worldgen blocks
* [[File:Grid Crude Oil.png|24px|link=Crude Oil]] '''[[Crude Oil]]''' — refined from oil blocks in a [[Crucible]]
* [[File:Grid Bitumen.png|24px|link=Bitumen]] '''[[Bitumen]]''' and '''[[Tar]]''' — ground oil products; both burn as fuel
* [[File:Grid Peat.png|24px|link=Peat]] '''[[Peat]]''' — a renewable fuel dried from [[Bog Earth]]
