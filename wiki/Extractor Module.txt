The '''Extractor Module''' is a [[Logistics Pipes#Chassis Logistics Pipe|Chassis Logistics Pipe]] module that actively pulls items from an adjacent inventory and injects them into the [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]. Three mark variants exist, with higher marks extracting at greater speed or quantity.

== Usage ==

When installed in a [[Logistics Pipes#Chassis Logistics Pipe|Chassis Logistics Pipe]], this module pulls items from an adjacent inventory and routes them into the logistics network. It functions similarly to the standalone [[Item Extractor Pipe]] but is network-aware and can be combined with other modules in the same chassis.

* Extracts items from an adjacent inventory into the logistics network.
* Configurable item filter — extract only specific item types.
* Higher mark variants extract faster or in larger quantities per tick.
* Can be configured to route extracted items to a specific network destination.

=== Tips ===

* Pair with a [[Provider Module]] in the same chassis to both extract and advertise from one inventory.
* Configure the item filter to ensure only the right items leave a mixed-content chest.
* MK3 is ideal for high-output machines (blast furnaces, high-tier kilns) that produce items faster than MK1 can handle.
