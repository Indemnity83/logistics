A '''block of lumenite''' is a storage block equivalent to nine [[Lumenite Ingot|lumenite ingots]]. It is also the core of a [[Batteries|Resonant Battery]], so it is a crafting component as much as a storage block.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

A block of lumenite can be mined only with a stone pickaxe or better. Mined without one it drops nothing.
