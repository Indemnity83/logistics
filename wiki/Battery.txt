The '''Battery''' is an RF storage block. It is charged by [[Engines|engines]] through [[Cables]] and holds that energy for later use. To power a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]], feed a Battery's RF into a [[Power Junction]] — pipes do not draw from a Battery directly.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Battery can be broken with any tool, though a pickaxe is fastest.

== Usage ==

The Battery buffers RF on your power grid. Engines charge it through cables, and it discharges through cables to whatever needs power — most often a machine such as a [[Kiln]], [[Macerator]], or [[Sawmill]], or a [[Power Junction]].

* Charge level is shown as a fill bar on the placed block and as a green charge bar on the item
* If several Batteries serve one grid, they are drawn in the order they were connected

=== Power ===

A Battery is charged with RF produced by an [[Engines|engine]] and delivered through [[Copper Cable|power cables]]:

* [[Redstone Engine]] / [[Stirling Engine]] / [[Creative Engine]] generate RF
* [[Copper Cable]], [[Gold Cable]], and [[Ender Cable]] carry that RF to the Battery at increasing transfer rates

Note that extraction pipes (the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]) are paced by an adjacent '''engine''' instead, not the network Battery.
