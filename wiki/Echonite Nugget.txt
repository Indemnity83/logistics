'''Echonite nuggets''' are pieces of echonite worth one-ninth of a [[Echonite Ingot|echonite ingot]]. Two of them trim the corners of a [[Batteries|Deep Battery]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1
|name=Echonite Nugget
|B2=Echonite Ingot
|Output=Echonite Nugget,9
}}

== Usage ==

Nine nuggets craft back into a [[Echonite Ingot|echonite ingot]].
