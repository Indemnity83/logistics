'''Echo Chip''' is an electronic [[Chips|chip]] used to craft [[Modules|modules]], built on a [[Chips#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Wafer]] base.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

The binder differs by version — a resin clump on Minecraft 1.21.11 and newer, or a slime ball on 1.21.1:

{{Crafting|head=1|showname=0|showdescription=1
|A1=Ender Dust |B1=Resin Clump |C1=Ender Dust
|A2=Ender Dust |B2=Echo Dust |C2=Ender Dust
|A3=Ender Dust |B3=Silicon Wafer |C3=Ender Dust
|Output=Echo Chip,4
|description=For Minecraft 1.21.11 and beyond.
}}
{{Crafting|foot=1|showname=0|showdescription=1
|A1=Ender Dust |B1=Slime Ball |C1=Ender Dust
|A2=Ender Dust |B2=Echo Dust |C2=Ender Dust
|A3=Ender Dust |B3=Silicon Wafer |C3=Ender Dust
|Output=Echo Chip,4
|description=For Minecraft 1.21.1.
}}
