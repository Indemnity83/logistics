'''Oil''' is a family of fuels and materials that comes from oil-bearing blocks generated around the world. The blocks are ground in a [[Macerator]] into [[Bitumen]] and [[Tar]], or melted in a [[Crucible]] into [[Crude Oil]] — which a [[Refinery]] distills into [[Fuel Oil]].

== Blocks and items ==

* [[File:Grid Oil Sand.png|24px|link=Oil Sand]] '''[[Oil Sand]]''' — an oil-soaked sand block that generates in sandy biomes
* [[File:Grid Oil Red Sand.png|24px|link=Oil Red Sand]] '''[[Oil Red Sand]]''' — the badlands red-sand variant
* [[File:Grid Oil Shale.png|24px|link=Oil Shale]] '''[[Oil Shale]]''' — an oil-bearing rock that generates underground
* [[File:Grid Bitumen.png|24px|link=Bitumen]] '''[[Bitumen]]''' — a solid oil product ground from the oil blocks; melts to [[Crude Oil]] and burns as fuel
* [[File:Grid Tar.png|24px|link=Tar]] '''[[Tar]]''' — a sticky product ground from the sandy oil blocks, also a [[Refinery]] byproduct

== Fluids ==

* [[File:Fluid Crude Oil.gif|24px|link=Crude Oil]] '''[[Crude Oil]]''' — a placeable fluid, melted from the oil blocks or [[Bitumen]] in a [[Crucible]]
* [[File:Fluid Fuel Oil.gif|24px|link=Fuel Oil]] '''[[Fuel Oil]]''' — refined from Crude Oil in a [[Refinery]]

== Uses ==

* [[File:Grid Synthetic Polymer.png|24px|link=Synthetic Polymer]] '''[[Synthetic Polymer]]''' — crafted from [[Tar]] and [[Coal Dust]] on the way to [[Rubber]]
* [[File:Grid Copper Fluid Pipe.png|24px|link=Fluid Pipes]] '''Pipe sealant''' — [[Tar]] seals a transport pipe into a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]], like a slime ball
