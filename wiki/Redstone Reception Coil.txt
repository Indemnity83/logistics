The '''Redstone Reception Coil''' enables energy to be taken in and stored by [[Machines]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|B1=Redstone
|B2=Gold Ingot
|B3=Redstone
|Output=Redstone Reception Coil
}}

== Usage ==

Every machine recipe uses one Redstone Reception Coil in the bottom-centre slot, alongside a [[Machine Frame]] and a pair of gears:

* [[Kiln]]
* [[Macerator]]
* [[Sawmill]]
* [[Laser Quarry]]
* [[Fluid Pump]]
* [[Power Junction]]
