The '''Crop Farm''' is an RF-powered farm that tends the field you plant around it. It harvests fully grown crops, replants them, and collects the harvest into its inventory.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Crop Farm is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

|name1=Crop Farm |icon1=Grid Crop Farm.png |hardness1=3.5
}}

== Usage ==

The Crop Farm does not lay out a field for you. Till and plant the field yourself within its '''Reach''' and it takes over from there.

=== Reach ===

The Reach runs 3 blocks out from the Crop Farm on every side, corners included (a 7×7 area), from one block below the Crop Farm to one block above it.

=== Crops ===

The Crop Farm tends {{Mcw|Wheat}}, {{Mcw|Carrot|carrots}}, {{Mcw|Potato|potatoes}} and {{Mcw|Beetroot}}. Other plants and crops on ground other than farmland, dirt or grass are left alone.

=== Tending ===

When a crop is fully grown, the Crop Farm harvests it and replants the same crop where it stood, taken from the harvest first and then from its inventory. If the ground has become dirt or grass, it tills it back into farmland first. Every crop it harvests becomes a '''Plot''' that it keeps replanting. It never plants anywhere it has not harvested, so the field's layout is yours.

If a Plot cannot be replanted — wheat and beetroot occasionally drop no seed — it waits as an '''Empty Plot''' until a matching seed turns up in the inventory. Keep a few spare seeds in the Crop Farm to refill them. A Plot is forgotten once something else grows or is placed there, or the ground becomes anything other than farmland, dirt or grass (a dirt path, for example); breaking the Crop Farm forgets all of its Plots.

The Crop Farm does not water the field. Keep water near the farmland as you would for any field.

=== Output ===

The harvest goes into a 15-slot inventory. Pipes and hoppers can insert into it from any side and pull from any slot. When the inventory is full the Crop Farm keeps working and drops what does not fit at the crop. Pulling everything out, seeds included, leaves Empty Plots with nothing to refill them.

=== Power ===

The Crop Farm runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 20,000 RF and accepts up to 32 RF/t. It pays a small upkeep every tick for each Plot it keeps (Empty Plots included), plus a cost for each block it changes:

{| class="wikitable"
! Action !! RF
|-
| Upkeep, per Plot || 0.25 RF/t
|-
| Harvesting a crop || 100
|-
| Tilling a block || 50
|-
| Planting a seed || 50
|}

Looking for work is free. Its [[Side Tabs#Energy|Energy]] tab shows how much RF it is using.

=== Status ===

The lamp on the block and in the GUI shows what the Crop Farm is doing:

{| class="wikitable"
! Lamp !! Status
|-
| Green || '''Working''', or '''Idle''' when nothing in the Reach is ready to harvest
|-
| Amber || '''Waiting for seeds''' (Empty Plots need seeds), '''Inventory full''' (dropping items), or '''Paused''' by redstone
|-
| Red || '''No power''', or '''Blocked''' when it is not allowed to change a block (for example, claim protection)
|}

The lamp is dark when the energy buffer is empty. With [https://modrinth.com/mod/jade Jade] installed, the HUD shows the farm's status, Reach and Plots.

=== Redstone control ===

The Crop Farm ignores redstone by default. Its [[Side Tabs#Redstone Control|Redstone Control]] tab can make a signal pause it or run it. Engines do not pass their redstone signal on, so a lever on an engine sitting on the Crop Farm switches only the engine.

=== Configuration ===

The RF costs and the farm's buffer live in <code>config/logistics/forestry.farm.json</code> and can be changed in-game with the operator-only <code>/logistics config forestry.farm &lt;key&gt; &lt;value&gt;</code> command:

{{Config|head=1|rf_upkeep_crop_farm|RF per tick for each Plot the Crop Farm maintains|0.25|Decimal}}
{{Config|rf_harvest_crop|RF per crop block harvested|100|Integer}}
{{Config|rf_till|RF per block tilled|50|Integer}}
{{Config|rf_plant|RF per seed planted|50|Integer}}
{{Config|tier.basic.energy_capacity|Internal RF buffer|20000|Integer}}
{{Config|foot=1|tier.basic.max_energy_input|Maximum RF accepted per tick|32|Integer}}
