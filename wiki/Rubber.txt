'''Rubber''' is a flexible crafting component used to insulate [[Cables|power cables]]. It is produced in two steps: a '''Rubber Mix''' is crafted from raw ingredients, then smelted into a finished '''Rubber Chunk'''.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

The Rubber Mix is the raw, unprocessed form:

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Slime Ball |B1=Coal Dust
|Output=Rubber Mix
}}

'''Yields:''' 1× Rubber Mix (shapeless: Slime Ball + [[Coal Dust]]). Coal Dust comes from the [[Macerator]] (grind Coal, Charcoal, or Coal Ore).

=== Smelting ===

Smelt 1× Rubber Mix in a [[Kiln]] or furnace to cure it into '''2× Rubber Chunk'''.

== Usage ==

Rubber Chunks insulate power cables:

* [[Cables|Copper Cable]] - Standard RF transport cable
* [[Cables|Gold Cable]] - Higher-rate cable
* [[Cables|Ender Cable]] - Highest-rate cable

=== Tips ===

* Smelting one Rubber Mix yields '''two''' Rubber Chunks, so set up a [[Macerator]] → [[Kiln]] line for cheap, bulk rubber.
* Slime Balls are also used for [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] crafting — keep a slime farm running.
