'''Tin''' is a metal found underground in the Overworld. It generates in tin ore, which drops raw tin when mined; the raw tin is smelted into tin ingots and crafted into components.

== Blocks and items ==

* [[Tin Ore]] — an ore found underground, with a Deepslate Tin Ore variant in the deepslate layer
* [[Raw Tin]] — an item that drops from tin ore when mined
* [[Tin Ingot]] — an item created by smelting raw tin
* '''Tin Nugget''' — one-ninth of a tin ingot
* '''Block of Raw Tin''' — a block used for compact raw tin storage
* '''Block of Tin''' — a block used for compact tin ingot storage
* [[Dusts|Tin Dust]] — produced by grinding tin ore in a [[Macerator]] (ore doubling)

== Uses ==

* [[Tin Gear]] — a crafting component in the gear progression
* [[Bronze Ingot]] — a tin-and-copper alloy
