Renders a grinding-style recipe (the [[Macerator]] and [[Sawmill]]). The input sits top-left with
its experience below, an arrow points right to the primary output (with its ×count on the right),
and an optional '''chance byproduct''' hangs in a second slot beneath the output — its drop chance
sits under the arrow, right-aligned toward the byproduct — the way the in-game machine shows a
primary and secondary output. The secondary slot is always shown (empty when a recipe has no
byproduct) so the machine's second output is clear. Styling reuses
[[Template:Grid Crafting Table/styles.css]] for the panel, slots, and arrow, plus
[[Template:Grid Grinding Table/styles.css]] for the layout, count, chance, and experience.

Usage:
 {{Grid Grinding Table
 |Input=Iron Ore |Output=Iron Dust |OutputCount=2
 |Byproduct=Tin Dust |ByproductChance=10%
 |XP=0.7
 }}

Parameters:
 Input           — the item being processed (rendered via {{Grid Cycle}}; ";"-separated alternatives cycle)
 Output          — the resulting item
 OutputCount     — quantity produced (optional)
 Byproduct       — a secondary chance output (optional)
 ByproductChance — its drop chance, e.g. "10%" (optional)
 XP              — experience granted (optional; the Sawmill grants none)

Depends on [[Template:Grid]], [[Template:Grid Cycle]].
