The '''Fluid Supplier Pipe''' is a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]] that keeps an adjacent machine's fluid tank stocked by ordering [[Fluid Packet|fluid packets]] from the network — the fluid counterpart of a [[Supplier Logistics Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|B1=Gold Gear
|A2=Bucket |B2=Supplier Logistics Pipe |C2=Bucket
|Output=Fluid Supplier Pipe
}}

Substitute a [[Gold Chipset]] for the [[Gold Gear]] to craft it with a [[Sequential Fabricator]].

== Usage ==

Right-click the pipe with a bucket of the fluid you want to set the filter, or [[Wrench|shift-right-click]] with an empty hand to clear it. Every second it checks the adjacent tank against a configurable '''target amount''' and orders the shortfall as fluid packets from a [[Fluid Provider Pipe|provider]]; incoming packets are buffered internally and deposited into the tank as they're paid for with RF, never overfilling past the target.

=== Configuration ===

Right-click with a [[Wrench]] to open the Fluid Supplier's own GUI:

* '''Target amount''' — type a value or use the +1/+10/+100/+1000 (and matching −) buttons, up to '''32,000 mB'''.
* '''Fulfillment''' — '''Partial''' (default) accepts whatever's currently available; toggled off, it only orders when the network can supply the entire shortfall at once.
* '''Min. Threshold''' — None (default), 1, 2, or 5 buckets: how large the deficit must be before it bothers placing an order at all, to avoid trickling in tiny top-ups.

The filter locks — and can't be changed — while any fluid is held in the buffer or still on order.

== Power ==

Like every [[Logistics Pipes|logistics pipe]], it draws [[RF Energy|RF]] through a [[Power Junction]] — it renders '''green''' while powered and '''red''' when the network has no charge, in which case routing stops. Undeliverable [[Fluid Packet|fluid packets]] are voided rather than dropped as ground items. Each fluid packet it accepts costs a flat RF charge, paid down one packet at a time as power allows; only paid-off fluid moves from the buffer into the tank.
