'''Logistics''' is built to share its materials with other tech mods rather than keep a parallel set of its own. It does that through '''conventional tags''' — the <code>c:</code> tag namespace that Fabric and NeoForge mods agree on — so a dust is a dust whoever made it.

This cuts both ways. Logistics '''publishes''' its items into those tags, so another mod's recipes can take them; and Logistics '''recipes read the tags''' rather than naming its own items, so another mod's equivalents work here. You do not need to pick a side: one iron dust is as good as another.

== What Logistics accepts ==

Logistics recipes are written against tags, so an ingredient from any mod that publishes the same tag is accepted. In practice that means:

* '''Rubber''' — <code>#c:ingots/rubber</code>, so rubber from [https://modrinth.com/mod/techreborn Tech Reborn] or [https://modrinth.com/mod/create-tfmg Create: The Factory Must Grow] insulates [[Cables]] just as well as ours.
* '''Dusts''' — <code>#c:dusts/*</code> for bronze, coal, quartz, sulfur, iron, copper, gold, tin, lapis, amethyst, echo, diamond, netherite, saltpeter and the infused alloys. [https://modrinth.com/mod/techreborn Tech Reborn], [https://modrinth.com/mod/mekanism Mekanism] and friends all publish these, so their dusts smelt in a [[Kiln]] exactly like ours.
* '''Gears''' — <code>#c:gears/*</code> for wood, stone, copper, iron, bronze, gold, diamond and netherite. A gear from [https://modrinth.com/mod/modern-industrialization Modern Industrialization] or [https://modrinth.com/mod/immersiveengineering Immersive Engineering] drops straight into our machines, pipes and engines — including the [[Machine Frame]] and the [[Gears|gear]] upgrades.
* '''Tin and bronze''' — <code>#c:ingots/*</code>, <code>#c:nuggets/*</code>, <code>#c:storage_blocks/*</code> and <code>#c:raw_materials/tin</code>, so any mod's tin or bronze works in the [[Macerator]] and in the [[Tin Valve]] and [[Bronze Valve]] crafts.
* '''Ores''' — <code>#c:ores/*</code>, so another mod's tin ore macerates here.
* '''Apatite''' — <code>#c:gems/apatite</code> and <code>#c:ores/apatite</code>.
* '''Slag''' — <code>#c:slag</code>.
* '''Flour''' — <code>#c:flours/wheat</code>, so [https://modrinth.com/mod/create Create]'s wheat flour bakes in the bread, cake and cookie crafts.
* '''Vanilla staples''' — <code>#c:sands</code> and <code>#c:glass_blocks</code>, so coloured and modded variants work where plain sand or glass would.

Both spellings of the plant-oil and ethanol tags are published, because different mods settled on different ones. Between them these put [[Crude Oil]], [[Seed Oil]], [[Bio Fuel]] and [[Fuel Oil]] in front of other mods' generators and refineries.

== Burning other mods' fuels ==

The [[Fuel Engine]] picks its fuel by tag, not by fluid, so any mod's fuel burns at the matching tier. A fluid in more than one tag burns at the best of them:

! Tag !! RF per bucket !! Heat

|-
| <code>#c:biofuel</code> || 80,000 RF || Cool
|-
| <code>#c:crude_oil</code> / <code>#c:oil</code> || 40,000 RF || Hot
|}

== Renamed and removed items ==

Two items changed identity in v0.8.12, and both convert automatically — existing stacks and placed blocks resolve to the replacement, so no world or recipe breaks:

* '''[[Saltpeter]]''', formerly '''Niter''' — renamed to the name other tech mods use.
* '''Copper Nugget''' — Logistics used to add its own, duplicating the vanilla item. It now uses the vanilla nugget, and publishes that into <code>#c:nuggets/copper</code>. On '''Minecraft 1.21.1''' the mod keeps its own nugget, because vanilla has none at that version.

== HUD and recipe browsers ==

Logistics machines report their state to [https://modrinth.com/mod/jade Jade], and every machine recipe — including the engines' fuels — appears in [https://modrinth.com/mod/jei Just Enough Items].
