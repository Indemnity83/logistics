The '''Sequential Fabricator''' is an RF-powered machine that assembles [[Chipsets|chipsets]] from a sequence of ingredients. Each chipset is a fixed recipe of {{Mcw|Redstone Dust|redstone}} plus one tier material, and the machine's output is selected in its GUI.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Sequential Fabricator is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

Shares the standard machine base — a [[Machine Frame]], a [[Redstone Reception Coil]], and two [[Copper Gear|copper gears]] — with a [[Netherite Gear]] and {{Mcw|Obsidian|obsidian}} on top.

== Usage ==

Toggle any number of [[Chipsets|chipsets]] to queue in the machine's GUI, then feed it the ingredients. The Sequential Fabricator builds one at a time, cycling round-robin through the queued chipsets whose ingredients are currently available — completing and ejecting each before advancing to the next — so a queue stocked with enough material for several chipsets keeps running unattended without re-selecting. A queued chipset the machine can't currently craft is skipped until its materials return. Every recipe appears in the Just Enough Items (JEI) viewer.

=== Power ===

The Sequential Fabricator runs on [[RF Energy|RF]]. It holds 100,000 RF and accepts up to 128 RF/t, spending 80 RF/t while working. Each chipset carries its own RF cost — from 10,000 RF for a [[Redstone Chipset]] up to 120,000 RF for a [[Netherite Chipset]] — so connect a strong RF source.
