Renders a [[Transposer]] recipe. The Transposer trades an item against the fluid in its internal
tank, so a recipe has an item and/or a fluid on each side: the input item and any fluid '''drawn
from''' the tank on the left, then the resulting item and any fluid '''deposited into''' the tank on
the right, each fluid labelled with its amount in millibuckets.

Fluids are drawn with their static <code>Grid &lt;Fluid&gt;.png</code> swatch rather than the
animated gif used by [[Template:Grid Crucible Table]]. That is deliberate: the Transposer page
carries far more recipes than the Crucible's, and the gif lookup needs an <code>#ifexist:</code>,
which counts against Fandom's 100-expensive-parser-call budget per page. Swatches keep the page
well clear of the cap.

Usage:
 {{Grid Transposer Table
 |Input=Bucket |FluidIn=Water |InAmount=1000 |Output=Water Bucket
 }}
 {{Grid Transposer Table
 |Input=Wheat Seeds |FluidOut=Seed Oil |OutAmount=50
 }}

Parameters:
 Input     — the item placed in the input slot (via {{Grid Cycle}}; ";"-separated alternatives cycle)
 FluidIn   — fluid drawn from the tank (optional)
 InAmount  — millibuckets drawn, e.g. "1000"
 Output    — the resulting item (optional — some recipes only produce fluid)
 FluidOut  — fluid deposited into the tank (optional)
 OutAmount — millibuckets deposited, e.g. "50"

Depends on [[Template:Grid]], [[Template:Grid Cycle]].
