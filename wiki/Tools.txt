'''Tools''' are used to configure and interact with pipes, engines, and machines.

==Configuration Tools==
* '''[[Wrench]]''' - Configure pipes and machines, rotate output faces

==Network Tools==
* '''[[Marking Fluid]]''' - Color-code copper pipes to segment networks

==Quarry Tools==
* '''[[Marker]]''' - Define a custom [[Laser Quarry]] mining area

==See Also==
* [[Pipes]] - Items configured with tools
* [[Energy]] - Engines configured with wrench
* [[Machines]] - Machines configured and bounded with these tools
