The '''Copper Gear''' is a mid-tier crafting component used to create advanced machines and automation equipment. It's equivalent in tier to the [[Iron Gear]] but uses copper instead.

==Progression==

Copper Gears represent mid-tier crafting materials:
* [[Wooden Gear]] → Basic tier
* [[Stone Gear]] → Stone tier
* [[Iron Gear]] → Mid tier
* '''Copper Gear''' → Mid tier (this item)
* [[Diamond Gear]] → Advanced tier

==Tips==

* Copper alternative to iron gear
* Needed for advanced automation (quarry)
* Stock up before building large-scale automation
* 1:1 copper-to-gear ratio (4 ingots = 1 gear)
* Equivalent tier to iron gear
