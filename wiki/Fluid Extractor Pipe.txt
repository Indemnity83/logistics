The '''Fluid Extractor Pipe''' is a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] that pulls fluid out of an adjacent tank or machine. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Item Extractor Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Combine the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] with a Slime Ball (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Item Extractor Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Fluid Extractor Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

The Fluid Extractor Pipe pulls fluid from an adjacent tank or machine and pushes it down the line. Like the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] it must be driven by an adjacent engine — a [[Stirling Engine]] pulls faster than a [[Redstone Engine]], and a [[Creative Engine]] runs at full speed. With no engine it stops; passive pipes alone can't pull fluid from a handler. To draw fluid sources straight from the world, use a [[Pump]] instead.
