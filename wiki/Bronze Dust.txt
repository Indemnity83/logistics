'''Bronze Dust''' is a dust produced by grinding a [[Bronze Ingot|bronze ingot]], or crafted directly from [[Copper Dust|copper]] and [[Tin Dust|tin]] dust.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0|foot=1
|Input=Bronze Ingot
|Output=Bronze Dust,1
}}

=== Crafting ===

Combine three [[Copper Dust]] with one [[Tin Dust]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Copper Dust |B2=Copper Dust |C2=Copper Dust
|A3=Tin Dust
|Output=Bronze Dust,4
}}

== Usage ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt it in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]]:

{{Smelting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Bronze Dust
|Output=Bronze Ingot
}}

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Used to craft the [[Crafter Module|Crafter modules]].
