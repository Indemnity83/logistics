'''Machines''' perform tasks automatically using RF energy.

==Mining==
* '''[[Laser Quarry]]''' - Automatically mines the area below, requires RF power; default 16×16 or a custom area marked out with the [[Marker]] tool

==Processing==
* '''[[Macerator]]''' - Grinds items into dust; ores (with chance byproducts), recycling, and dyes
* '''[[Kiln]]''' - RF-powered electric furnace; smelts any vanilla smelting recipe (no fuel — uses RF), with recipe-book GUI
* '''[[Sawmill]]''' - Cuts logs and wooden items into planks, yielding [[Sawdust]]

==Power==
* '''[[Power Junction]]''' - Feeds RF from cables or a [[Battery]] into a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]
