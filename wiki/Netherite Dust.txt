'''Netherite Dust''' is a dust used to craft the [[Cores|Netherite Core]]. It currently has no grinding recipe, so it cannot be obtained in survival.

== Obtaining ==

This dust has no obtaining recipe in the current version.

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ingredient ===

Intended to craft the [[Cores|Netherite Core]].
