The '''Item Insertion Pipe''' is a [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] that prefers inserting items into adjacent inventories.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of Glass flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Quartz |B2=Glass |C2=Quartz
|Output=Item Insertion Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

The Item Insertion Pipe prefers to insert items into adjacent inventories that have space; if none can accept the item, it stays on the network and continues.

Pair it with the [[Item Filter Pipe]] and [[Item Void Pipe]] to build sorted storage with overflow handling.
