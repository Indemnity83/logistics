The '''Iron Gear''' is a mid-tier crafting component used to create advanced machines and automation equipment. It requires more valuable materials than early-game gears.

==Progression==

Iron Gears represent mid-tier crafting materials:
* [[Wooden Gear]] → Basic tier
* [[Stone Gear]] → Stone tier
* '''Iron Gear''' → Mid tier (this item)
* [[Copper Gear]] → Mid tier
* [[Diamond Gear]] → Advanced tier

==Tips==

* Requires iron - more valuable than wooden/stone gears
* Needed for advanced automation (quarry)
* Stock up before building large-scale automation
* 1:1 iron-to-gear ratio (4 ingots = 1 gear)
