'''Copper nuggets''' are pieces of copper worth one-ninth of a {{Mcw|Copper Ingot|copper ingot}}, handy for fine crafting and storage.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|B2=Copper Ingot
|Output=Copper Nugget,9
}}

== Usage ==

Nine copper nuggets craft back into a {{Mcw|Copper Ingot|copper ingot}}.
