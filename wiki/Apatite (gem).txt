'''Apatite''' is a gem mined from [[Apatite Ore|apatite ore]], used mainly to craft [[Apatite Valve|apatite valves]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Mining ===

[[Apatite Ore]] drops 1–6 apatite when mined with a stone pickaxe or better; the count is increased by Fortune. See [[Apatite Ore#Breaking|Apatite Ore]] for the full breaking table.
