'''Crude Oil''' is a dark liquid found in surface seeps and locked inside oil-bearing blocks. Unlike the mod's other custom fluids it can be '''placed in the world''', picked up in a {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}, and pooled — it flows sluggishly, spreading a short distance before dropping off.

== Obtaining ==

=== Oil seeps ===

Crude Oil pools naturally in rare '''oil seeps''' — small surface lakes that generate across the Overworld, ringed by [[Oil Sand]], [[Oil Red Sand]], or [[Oil Shale]] depending on the biome. Scoop it up with a bucket or a [[Fluid Pump|Pump]].

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] liquefies oil-bearing blocks and [[Bitumen]] into Crude Oil:

{{Crucible|head=1 |Input=Oil Sand;Oil Red Sand;Oil Shale |ingredients=[[Oil Sand]], [[Oil Red Sand]], or [[Oil Shale]] |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=1000}}
{{Crucible |foot=1 |Input=Bitumen |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=250}}

== Usage ==

Crude Oil is refined into [[Fuel Oil]] in a [[Refinery]], or burned directly in a [[Fuel Engine]] for [[RF Energy|RF]]. A [[Transposer]] soaks sand and gravel in it to make [[Oil Sand]], [[Oil Red Sand]], and [[Oil Shale]]. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Swimming in it ===

Crude Oil is unpleasant to be in. While your eyes are below the surface it clouds your vision almost completely, and it applies {{Mcw|Nausea}}, {{Mcw|Poison}}, and {{Mcw|Slowness}} III for as long as you stay under. The effects refresh every few seconds and wear off shortly after you surface, so a brief dip costs a little health rather than a life — but swimming a deep seep without milk or a way out is a poor idea.

=== Bucket ===

The '''Crude Oil Bucket''' places and picks up the fluid like a {{Mcw|Water|water}} or {{Mcw|Lava|lava}} bucket, and empties back to an ordinary bucket when poured into a tank or machine.
