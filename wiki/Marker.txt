The '''Marker''' is a placement aid used to define a custom mining area for the [[Laser Quarry]]. Place markers to outline the region you want to excavate instead of relying on the quarry's default 16×16 footprint.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A1=Lapis Dust
|A2=Stick
|Output=Marker,1
}}

== Usage ==

Markers work together to mark out a rectangular mining boundary for the [[Laser Quarry]].

=== Defining an area ===

# '''Place three markers in a right-triangle (L-shape)''' to outline two perpendicular edges of the desired area.
# '''Activate''' the markers with the [[Wrench]] to project the resulting rectangular boundary.
# '''Place a [[Laser Quarry]]''' adjacent to the marked boundary; it mines the custom region instead of the default 16×16 footprint.

A successful activation reports the defined area size (e.g. ''area defined: 9x9''). Activation fails if there are not three markers in a right triangle, or if the resulting area is below the minimum size. Wrenching an active marker again deactivates the connected set.

Markers have no collision — you can walk through them — and break instantly with any tool, dropping themselves. Use them to keep the quarry off your base, important terrain, or a neighbouring quarry; without markers the [[Laser Quarry]] just mines its default 16×16 area.
