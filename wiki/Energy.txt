'''Energy''' covers the engines, cables, storage, and RF power system in Logistics. Engines generate RF energy, cables distribute it, a [[Batteries|battery]] stores it, and a [[Power Junction]] feeds it into the logistics network. Engines push energy out of one configurable face, and most run only with a redstone signal until their [[Side Tabs#Redstone Control|Redstone Control]] tab is set otherwise.

==Energy System==
* '''[[RF Energy]]''' - Understanding the power system

==Engines==
RF is generated by seven '''[[Engines]]''':
* '''[[Redstone Engine]]''' - Small, steady output from a redstone signal
* '''[[Stirling Engine]]''' - Burns solid fuel
* '''[[Steam Engine]]''' - Boils water with solid fuel
* '''[[Magmatic Engine]]''' - Burns lava
* '''[[Fuel Engine]]''' - Burns liquid fuel, cooled with water
* '''[[Reaction Engine]]''' - A bufferless burst from a fluid and a catalyst
* '''[[Creative Engine]]''' - Unlimited, creative-only

==Cables==
Cables distribute RF from engines to a [[Batteries|battery]], [[Power Junction]], or machine. They share the four [[Tiers|energy tiers]] with batteries:
* '''[[Cables|Basic Cable]]''' - 30 RF/t
* '''[[Cables|Conductive Cable]]''' - 60 RF/t
* '''[[Cables|Resonant Cable]]''' - 120 RF/t
* '''[[Cables|Deep Cable]]''' - 240 RF/t

==Storage==
* '''[[Batteries]]''' - Store RF on a cable grid, in four tiers

==Network input==
* '''[[Power Junction]]''' - The single point where power enters a logistics network

==Powering the logistics network==

The [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]] is powered only through a [[Power Junction]]. An [[Engines|engine]] generates RF, [[Cables|cables]] carry it (optionally by way of a [[Batteries|battery]]) to a Power Junction placed against the network, and the pipes draw from the junction to route, supply, and craft items. Logistics pipes render '''green when powered''' and '''red when not''' — an unpowered network stops working and drops items already in transit until power returns.

The extraction pipes (the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]) do not draw from the Power Junction; each is paced by an adjacent engine instead.
