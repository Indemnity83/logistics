The '''Kiln''' is an RF-powered electric furnace that '''fires''' any vanilla smelting recipe — anything you can smelt in a furnace, the Kiln makes too. It runs those recipes at the same speed as a furnace, but on [[RF Energy|RF]] instead of fuel (just 1 RF/tick, no fuel to manage), which makes it ideal for automated setups. There are no Kiln-only recipes; see an item's '''Smelting''' section for what it produces.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Kiln is mined with a stone pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

The Kiln is a direct replacement for a vanilla furnace in automated setups. Place an item in the input slot and the Kiln smelts it using RF energy, depositing the result in the output slot. It processes any vanilla smelting recipe (ores, raw metals, food, etc.), and the recipe book in the GUI lets you browse every valid recipe.

Input is accepted from the top and sides; output is drawn from the bottom, the same as a vanilla furnace.

=== Power ===

The Kiln runs on [[RF Energy|RF]] — there is no fuel slot. It holds 10,000 RF, accepts up to 128 RF/tick, and consumes only 1 RF/tick while active, so a single [[Stirling Engine]] can power many kilns at once. Connect a power source to any side or top face.

=== Setup ===

# '''Place the Kiln''' in your automation area
# '''Connect power''' — attach a [[Stirling Engine]] or any RF source to a side or top face
# '''Feed input''' — pipe items in from the top or sides (e.g. an [[Item Extractor Pipe]] on a chest)
# '''Collect output''' — pipe from the bottom into storage

The Kiln smelts continuously as long as it has power and input items.

=== Common uses ===

* Smelting ore → ingot in automated pipelines
* Processing raw metals extracted by the [[Laser Quarry]]
* Smelting [[Quartz Dust]] into [[Quartz Crystal]] (required for [[Materials#Valves|Valve]] crafting)
* Smelting [[Chips#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Mix]] into [[Chips#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Wafer]] (required for [[Chips]])
* Pairing with the [[Macerator]]: grind ores into dust, then smelt the dust into ingots for higher yields
