'''Lumenite''' is an infused alloy — a deep violet metal with no ore of its own. It is made by infusing [[Amethyst Dust|amethyst dust]] with [[Liquid Glowstone]] in a [[Transposer]], then smelting the result. Lumenite exists to build the '''resonant''' rung of the power ladder: the [[Batteries|Resonant Battery]] and the [[Cables|Resonant Cable]].
