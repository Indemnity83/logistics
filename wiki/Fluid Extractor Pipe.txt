The '''Fluid Extractor Pipe''' actively pulls liquids out of an adjacent tank or machine and into a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid network]]. It is the fluid-side counterpart to the [[Item Extractor Pipe]], and like it, the extractor must be driven by an adjacent [[Redstone Engine|engine]].

Fluid pipes form a '''standalone''' transport system, separate from the item [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]: each connected line carries one fluid type and moves it by pressure equalization. While passive pipes move fluid for free, '''active extraction costs power'''.

'''Yields:''' 1× Fluid Extractor Pipe (shapeless: [[Item Extractor Pipe]] + Slime Ball)

==Behavior==

* '''Extracts fluid from tanks and machines when driven by an engine''' — pulls from the adjacent fluid handler and pushes it down the connected fluid line
* Pacing is set by the adjacent engine: a [[Stirling Engine]] pulls faster than a [[Redstone Engine]]; a [[Creative Engine]] runs at full speed
* With no engine power it stops — passive pipes alone cannot pull fluid out of a tank or machine
* Carries '''one fluid type''' per connected line

==Power==
The Fluid Extractor Pipe is gated by an adjacent '''engine''', the same model as the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] (this is the Tier 1 extraction power path, not the Tier 3 network's [[Battery]]). Place a [[Redstone Engine]], [[Stirling Engine]], or [[Creative Engine]] against the pipe to drive it.

==Tips==
* Point the extractor at a machine's output tank, then run [[Copper Fluid Pipe|copper]] or [[Golden Fluid Pipe|golden]] pipes to a [[Glass Tank]] to buffer the result
* For pulling fluid sources straight out of the world, use a [[Pump]] instead — it has its own RF buffer and doesn't need an engine touching it
* A [[Stirling Engine]] keeps a steady pull without redstone pulsing
