Renders a [[Crucible]] recipe: a solid input on the left, an arrow, and the molten '''fluid'''
output shown as its animated fluid icon (''Fluid <name>.gif'', falling back to a {{Grid}} swatch
for fluids without a gif, e.g. lava/water) with the produced amount in millibuckets to the right —
the way the machine melts one item into its fluid tank. Styling reuses
[[Template:Grid Crafting Table/styles.css]] for the panel, slots, and arrow, plus
[[Template:Grid Crucible Table/styles.css]] for the mB amount.

 Output — the resulting fluid (rendered via {{Grid}} using its bucket icon)
 Amount — millibuckets produced, e.g. "1000"
