Renders a [[Sequential Fabricator]] recipe: an ordered row of ingredient slots on the left, an arrow,
and the assembled item output on the right with its ×count — the way the machine consumes a sequence
of ingredients to build one item. Only the ingredient slots that are supplied are drawn (slots 2–6
appear as needed), so the widget grows to the recipe's length. Styling reuses
[[Template:Grid Crafting Table/styles.css]] for the panel, slots, and arrow, plus
[[Template:Grid Fabricator Table/styles.css]] for the ×count.

Usage:
 {{Grid Fabricator Table
 |Input=Redstone |Input2=Iron Ingot |Output=Iron Chipset
 }}

Parameters:
 Input .. Input6 — the ordered ingredients (rendered via {{Grid Cycle}}; ";"-separated alternatives cycle)
 InputCount ..   — a count for the matching slot (optional; disables cycling for that slot)
 Output          — the assembled output item
 OutputCount     — quantity produced (optional)

Depends on [[Template:Grid]], [[Template:Grid Cycle]].
