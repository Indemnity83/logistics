'''Lumenite nuggets''' are pieces of lumenite worth one-ninth of a [[Lumenite Ingot|lumenite ingot]]. Two of them trim the corners of a [[Batteries|Resonant Battery]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1
|name=Lumenite Nugget
|B2=Lumenite Ingot
|Output=Lumenite Nugget,9
}}

== Usage ==

Nine nuggets craft back into a [[Lumenite Ingot|lumenite ingot]].
