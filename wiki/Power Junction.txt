The '''Power Junction''' is the single point where electrical power enters a [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]. [[Cables]], engines, and [[Battery|batteries]] feed RF into the junction, and the network draws what it needs out of the junction's buffer. A network with no Power Junction has no power; a [[Battery]] placed directly on the pipes does not feed them.

== Usage ==

Place the Power Junction so it touches both your power grid and your [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]. It buffers the two sides:

* Holds up to '''1,000,000 RF'''.
* Accepts up to '''128 RF/t''' from adjacent [[Cables]], [[Engines]], or a [[Battery]].
* Lets the network pull up to '''1,000 RF/t''' back out.

The front face shows a charge indicator that fills as the buffer charges. Run your power into a junction, and the junction into the network — without one, the network cannot be powered.
