'''Tin Nugget''' is one-ninth of a [[Tin Ingot]], handy for fine crafting and storage.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

One [[Tin Ingot]] unpacks into nine Tin Nuggets, and nine nuggets craft back into one ingot.
