'''Tin Ingot''' is a metal obtained by smelting [[Tin Ore|raw tin]]. It is used to create bronze alloy and tin-based components.

'''Related forms:''' Tin Ingot has a full set of storage and processing forms — '''Block of Tin''' (9 ingots), '''Tin Nugget''' (1/9 ingot), '''Raw Tin''' and the '''Block of Raw Tin''', and '''Tin Dust''' from the [[Macerator]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt Raw Tin (from [[Tin Ore]]) in a furnace or blast furnace to produce one Tin Ingot.

== Usage ==

Tin Ingot is the basis of bronze and several tin components.

=== Alloying ===

Combine tin with copper to craft [[Bronze Ingot|bronze ingots]].

=== Gears ===

Craft a [[Tin Gear]] from 4× tin ingot.

=== Grinding ===

Grind tin in the [[Macerator]] for Tin Dust (see [[Dusts]]).

=== Storage ===

* 9× Tin Ingot → Block of Tin
* 9× Tin Nugget → 1× Tin Ingot
