Renders a grinding recipe (e.g. in the [[Macerator]]): a single input slot, an arrow, and the
output with its experience below. Styling reuses [[Template:Grid Crafting Table/styles.css]] for
the slot, arrow, and count, plus [[Template:Grid Grinding Table/styles.css]] for the layout and
experience row.

Usage:
 {{Grid Grinding Table
 |Input=Tin Ore
 |Output=Tin Dust |OutputCount=2
 }}

Parameters:
 Input       — the item being ground (rendered via {{Grid}})
 Output      — the resulting item
 OutputCount — quantity produced (optional)
 XP          — experience granted (optional)

Depends on [[Template:Grid]].
