The '''Golden Transport Pipe''' is a [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] that accelerates items while powered.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of Glass flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Gold Ingot |B2=Glass |C2=Gold Ingot
|Output=Golden Transport Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

While powered by redstone, the Golden Transport Pipe speeds items up, and the boost persists as those items travel on through ordinary pipes.

Chain several powered golden pipes for a cumulative boost. Unpowered, it behaves like a [[Copper Transport Pipe]].
