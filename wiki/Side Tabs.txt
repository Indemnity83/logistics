'''Side tabs''' are the fold-out panels on the edges of a machine or engine GUI. Click a tab to open it and click it again to close it. Each side shows one open tab at a time, and the GUI remembers which tabs you left open for the rest of the session. If the window is too narrow for a panel to fit, the tab says ''Not enough room'' instead of opening.

They appear on every machine and engine with a GUI: the [[Macerator]], [[Alloy Smelter]], [[Crucible]], [[Refinery]], [[Kiln]], [[Sawmill]], [[Sequential Fabricator]], [[Transposer]], [[Crop Farm]] and [[Arboretum]], and the [[Stirling Engine|Stirling]], [[Steam Engine|Steam]], [[Magmatic Engine|Magmatic]], [[Fuel Engine|Fuel]] and [[Reaction Engine|Reaction]] engines.

== Information ==

The Information tab, on the left, says in a few lines what the block does and what it needs.

== Energy ==

The Energy tab, on the left, shows the block's power at a glance:

{| class="wikitable"
! Line !! Machines !! Engines
|-
| Rate || '''Usage''' — the RF/t it is drawing right now || '''Generation''' — the RF/t it is producing right now
|-
| Limit || '''Max Input''' — the most RF/t it accepts || '''Max Output''' — the most RF/t it can send
|-
| Buffer || colspan="2" | '''Stored''' — RF in its buffer, out of its capacity
|}

== Redstone Control ==

The Redstone Control tab, on the right, sets how the block responds to a redstone signal. There are three modes:

{| class="wikitable"
! Mode !! Behavior
|-
| '''Ignored''' || Always runs, regardless of redstone.
|-
| '''Low''' || Runs only without a redstone signal.
|-
| '''High''' || Runs only with a redstone signal.
|}

Machines and farms default to '''Ignored''', so they run as soon as they have power. Engines default to '''High''', so a lever on an engine switches it on as it always has. The mode is saved with the block.

The tab also shows the '''Signal''' the block is receiving, the signal its mode requires, and whether that leaves it '''Enabled''' or '''Disabled'''.

A [[Reaction Engine]] that is turned off mid-reaction still runs that reaction to the end.

The [[Redstone Engine]] and [[Creative Engine]] have no GUI, so they have no side tabs. Both run only while powered by redstone.

=== Engines do not pass signals on ===

The Stirling, Steam, Magmatic, Fuel, Reaction and Creative engines react to a redstone signal on themselves but do not pass it to the blocks around them. A lever on one of these engines switches that engine only, so a machine or farm next to it is not paused or started by the same lever.
