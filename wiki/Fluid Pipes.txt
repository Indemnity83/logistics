'''Fluid Pipes''' move liquids through a '''standalone''' fluid system, separate from the item-carrying [[Logistics Pipes|logistics network]]. Fluids never join the item [[Pipe Networks|network graph]] — they get their own local transport, extraction, and storage. Each fluid pipe is the liquid counterpart of an item [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]], and is crafted from one by combining it with a Slime Ball.

== Usage ==

* '''One fluid per line''' — each connected run carries a single fluid type and won't accept another until it drains (no mixing).
* '''Pressure equalization''' — fluid flows from fuller pipes toward emptier ones and toward sinks, capped each tick by the pipe's transfer rate.
* '''Passive transport is free''' — the stone, copper, golden, insertion, merger, bypass, and void pipes move fluid with no power. Only '''active extraction''' (the [[#Extraction|Fluid Extractor Pipe]] and the [[Pump]]) costs energy.
* Works with any mod's fluid storage on both Fabric and NeoForge.

=== Transport ===

The '''Stone''', '''Copper''', and '''Golden''' fluid pipes are passive movers carrying fluid between tanks, machines, and other pipes at 10, 10, and 80 mB/t respectively. The slowest pipe on a line caps its throughput, so use golden pipes on high-demand runs. Copper fluid pipes oxidize cosmetically like vanilla copper.

=== Extraction ===

The '''Fluid Extractor Pipe''' pulls fluid out of an adjacent tank or machine and pushes it down the line. Like the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] it must be driven by an adjacent [[Redstone Engine|engine]] — a [[Stirling Engine]] pulls faster than a [[Redstone Engine]], and a [[Creative Engine]] runs at full speed. With no engine it stops; passive pipes alone can't pull fluid from a handler. To draw fluid sources straight from the world, use a [[Pump]] instead (it has its own RF buffer).

=== Insertion ===

The '''Insertion Fluid Pipe''' fills adjacent tanks and machines first, passing fluid further down the line only once they are full. Chain several to top off a row of tanks in sequence.

=== Merging ===

The '''Merger Fluid Pipe''' is a directional check valve: fluid may only leave through a wrench-set output face and can't flow back in. Use it to converge several input lines into one one-way output, or at a tank's input so fluid never drains back out.

=== Bypassing handlers ===

The '''Bypass Fluid Pipe''' connects only to other fluid pipes, never to tanks or machines — run a fluid line straight past a handler without filling or draining it.

=== Voiding ===

The '''Void Fluid Pipe''' destroys any fluid that flows into it, acting as a bottomless sink. Because a line always flows toward it, place it deliberately so it doesn't drain tanks you meant to keep.
