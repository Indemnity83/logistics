'''Chips''' are advanced electronic components used to craft [[Modules|modules]]. The chip tier determines the module's mark (MK1, MK2, or MK3). All chips are built on a [[#Silicon Wafer|Silicon Wafer]] base.

==Silicon Wafer==

The Silicon Wafer is the substrate for all chips, made in two steps.

'''Step 1 — Silicon Mix (shapeless crafting):''' 3× [[Dusts#Quartz Dust|Quartz Dust]] + 1× [[Dusts|Coal Dust]] → 1× Silicon Mix

'''Step 2 — Silicon Wafer (smelting):''' Smelt Silicon Mix in a [[Kiln]] or furnace → 1× Silicon Wafer

==Chip Recipes==

All chips share the same shape — a frame of [[Dusts|Ender Dust]] around a Resin Clump, a defining dust, and a Silicon Wafer. Only the center slot changes, and all chips yield '''4× per craft'''.

==Progression Note==

Chips are mid-to-late game components. To craft them you need:
# [[Macerator]] — to produce Ender Dust, Quartz Dust, Coal Dust, and other dusts
# [[Kiln]] — to smelt Silicon Mix into Silicon Wafer
# Source of Resin Clumps (vanilla item, found in mangrove swamps)
