'''Echonite Dust''' is [[Echo Dust|echo dust]] infused with [[Liquid Ender]] in a [[Transposer]]. It is the entry point to the [[Echonite]] family — there is no ore and no other route in. Smelt it for a [[Echonite Ingot]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Transposer ===

The [[Transposer]] draws 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Ender]] from its tank to infuse one dust:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Echo Dust |FluidIn=Liquid Ender |InAmount=1000 |Output=Echonite Dust
|ingredients=One [[Echo Dust]] plus 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Ender]] in the tank
|notes=4,000 RF}}

=== Grinding ===

A [[Macerator]] recovers the dust from finished echonite:

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0
|Input=Block of Echonite
|Output=Echonite Dust,9
}}

{{Grinding|showname=0|foot=1
|Input=Echonite Ingot
|Output=Echonite Dust,1
}}
