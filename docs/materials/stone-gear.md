## Progression

Stone Gears represent the second tier of crafting materials:
- [Wooden Gear](wooden-gear.md) → Basic tier
- **Stone Gear** → Stone tier (this item)
- [Iron Gear](iron-gear.md) → Mid tier
- [Copper Gear](copper-gear.md) → Mid tier
- [Diamond Gear](diamond-gear.md) → Advanced tier

## Tips

- Craft from common cobblestone or stone
- Needed for Stirling Engine (better than Redstone Engine)
- Stock up before building power infrastructure
- Simple 1:1 cobblestone-to-gear ratio
