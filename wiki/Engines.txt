'''Engines''' are [[RF Energy|RF]] generators that power machines, the logistics network (through a [[Power Junction]]), and the [[Fluid Extractor Pipe|fluid extractor]]. The mod has three, from a trickle of power to an unlimited supply.

== Engines ==

* [[File:Grid Redstone Engine.png|24px|link=Redstone Engine]] '''[[Redstone Engine]]''' — a basic generator that converts a redstone signal into a small, steady RF output; cannot overheat
* [[File:Grid Stirling Engine.png|24px|link=Stirling Engine]] '''[[Stirling Engine]]''' — a high-power generator that burns fuel for substantial RF; can overheat without cooling
* [[File:Grid Creative Engine.png|24px|link=Creative Engine]] '''[[Creative Engine]]''' — an unlimited RF source for testing and creative-mode builds
