The '''Battery''' is a power-storage block that energizes a [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]. [[Basic Logistics Pipe|Logistics pipes]] draw [[RF Energy|RF]] from a connected Battery to route, supply, and craft items. Without a charged Battery the network stops working entirely — and items already in transit are dropped — until power is restored.

== Usage ==

The Battery acts as the energy reservoir for the [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]] it connects to. [[Basic Logistics Pipe|Logistics pipes]] and [[Modules|modules]] draw RF from it each time they route, supply, or craft.

* An unpowered network '''stops routing/supplying/crafting and drops in-transit items''' until a charged Battery is connected
* Logistics pipes show '''green when powered''' and '''red when not''', so you can see at a glance whether the network has energy
* Charge level is shown as a fill bar on the placed block and as a green charge bar on the item
* If several Batteries serve one network, they are drawn in the order they were connected

=== Power ===

A Battery is charged with RF produced by an [[Engines|engine]] and delivered through [[Copper Cable|power cables]]:

* [[Redstone Engine]] / [[Stirling Engine]] / [[Creative Engine]] generate RF
* [[Copper Cable]], [[Gold Cable]], and [[Ender Cable]] carry that RF to the Battery at increasing transfer rates

Note that extraction pipes (the [[Item Extractor Pipe]] and [[Fluid Extractor Pipe]]) are paced by an adjacent '''engine''' instead, not the network Battery.
