'''Gold Dust''' is ground from gold ore (or a gold ingot or block) in a [[Macerator]]. Its only use is to be smelted back into gold.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0

|XP=1.0
}}

{{Grinding|showname=0

|Output=Gold Dust,1
|XP=1.0
}}

{{Grinding|showname=0

|Output=Gold Dust,9
}}

{{Grinding|showname=0|foot=1

|Output=Gold Dust,1
}}

== Usage ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt it in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]]:

{{Smelting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Gold Dust
|Output=Gold Ingot
}}

=== Infusing ===

A [[Transposer]] infuses it with [[Liquid Redstone]] into [[Caterium Dust]], the conductive rung of the power ladder:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Gold Dust |FluidIn=Liquid Redstone |InAmount=1000 |Output=Caterium Dust
|ingredients=One [[Gold Dust]] plus 1,000 mB of [[Liquid Redstone]] in the tank
|notes=2,000 RF}}
