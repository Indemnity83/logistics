'''Tin Ore''' is a common ore found underground. Smelt its drop to create [[Tin Ingot|tin ingots]] for crafting bronze and tin components. A '''Deepslate Tin Ore''' variant occurs in deepslate layers.

==Obtaining==

'''Mining:'''
* Found underground in the Overworld
* Y-level distribution: (TBD - similar to copper/iron)
* Drops: Raw Tin
* Requires stone pickaxe or better

'''Deepslate variant:'''
* Deepslate Tin Ore
* Found in deepslate layers
* Same drops, harder to mine

==Usage==

'''Smelting:'''
* Raw Tin → [[Tin Ingot]]
* Used in furnaces or blast furnaces

'''Grinding:'''
* Tin Ore / Deepslate Tin Ore → 2× Tin Dust in the [[Macerator]] (ore doubling, see [[Dusts]])

'''Storage:'''
* Block of Raw Tin (9× Raw Tin) - compact storage

==Related Items==

* '''Deepslate Tin Ore''' - Deepslate-layer variant, same drop
* '''Raw Tin''' - Mined drop
* '''Block of Raw Tin''' - Compact storage for Raw Tin
* '''Tin Dust''' - Ground form from the [[Macerator]] (see [[Dusts]])

==Tips==

* Mine with a Fortune pickaxe for more Raw Tin
* Common ore - easy to find
* Needed for bronze alloy ([[Bronze Ingot]])
* Grind in the [[Macerator]] to double your output
