The '''Copper Transport Pipe''' is the standard-speed backbone of an item network.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of Glass flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Copper Ingot |B2=Glass |C2=Copper Ingot
|Output=Copper Transport Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

Copper is the default transport pipe — faster than [[Stone Transport Pipe|stone]], cheaper than a powered [[Golden Transport Pipe|golden]] pipe — and forms most of a network's length.

Copper pipes oxidize cosmetically like vanilla copper (wax with a honeycomb, scrape with an axe); oxidation has no effect on transport.

They also accept [[Marking Fluid]], which color-codes pipes so that same-color pipes connect, different colors don't, and unmarked pipes connect to anything — letting independent networks share the same space.
