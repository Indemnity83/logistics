'''Tin Ingot''' is a metal obtained by smelting [[Raw Tin]]. It is used to create [[Bronze Ingot|bronze]] alloy and tin-based components.

'''Related forms:''' Tin Ingot has a full set of storage and processing forms — '''Block of Tin''' (9 ingots), '''Tin Nugget''' (1/9 ingot), '''[[Raw Tin]]''' and the '''Block of Raw Tin''', and '''[[Dusts|Tin Dust]]''' from the [[Macerator]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Tin Ingots unpack from their storage forms:

{{Crafting|head=1|showdescription=1
|name=Tin Ingot |B2=Tin Block |Output=Tin Ingot,9 |description=Unpacked from a block.}}
{{Crafting|foot=1
|name=Tin Ingot |A1=Tin Nugget |B1=Tin Nugget |C1=Tin Nugget |A2=Tin Nugget |B2=Tin Nugget |C2=Tin Nugget |A3=Tin Nugget |B3=Tin Nugget |C3=Tin Nugget |Output=Tin Ingot |description=Assembled from nuggets.}}

=== Smelting ===

Smelting raw tin or tin ore in a furnace or blast furnace is the most common way to obtain tin ingots:

{{Smelting|foot=1

== Usage ==

Tin Ingot is the basis of bronze and several tin components.

=== Crafting ingredient ===

{{Crafting|head=1|showdescription=1

{{Crafting
|name=[[Tin Gear]] |B1=Tin Ingot |A2=Tin Ingot |B2=Copper Gear |C2=Tin Ingot |B3=Tin Ingot |Output=Tin Gear |description=A tin-tier gear.}}
{{Crafting

=== Grinding ===

Grinding tin in a [[Macerator]] yields [[Dusts|Tin Dust]] — ore doubling makes it the cheapest route to dust:

|Input=Tin Ore |ingredients=[[Tin Ore]] or Deepslate Tin Ore |Output=Tin Dust,2}}
{{Grinding|foot=1
