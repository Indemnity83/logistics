'''Oil Red Sand''' is the badlands variant of [[Oil Sand]] — an oil-soaked red sand block. Grind it for [[Bitumen]] or melt it down for [[Crude Oil]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Oil Red Sand breaks fastest with a shovel and always drops itself.

{{Breaking
|tool=shovel
|tier=none
|name1=Oil Red Sand |icon1=Grid Oil Red Sand.png |hardness1=0.5
}}

=== Natural generation ===

Oil Red Sand generates in large surface blobs throughout '''badlands''' biomes, and rings the [[Crude Oil]] pools of oil seeps there.

== Usage ==

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] melts Oil Red Sand into [[Crude Oil]]:

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Red Sand |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=1000}}

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] grinds it into [[Bitumen]], with a chance of [[Tar]]:

{{Grinding|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Red Sand |Output=Bitumen,3 |Byproduct=Tar |ByproductChance=50%}}
