The '''Stone Transport Pipe''' is the cheapest [[Transport Pipes|transport pipe]] — very slow, with a tiny buffer.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Crafted eight at a time from a pane of {{Mcw|Glass}} flanked by its defining material:

{{Crafting
|head=1
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A2=Stone |B2=Glass |C2=Stone
|Output=Stone Transport Pipe,8
}}

== Usage ==

Stone pipes move items much more slowly than a [[Copper Transport Pipe]] and hold only a small buffer, so they suit short runs and early-game networks where cost matters more than speed.

Like every transport pipe it connects to other pipes and to adjacent inventories automatically, and at a junction items pick a random direction unless a routing pipe overrides it.
