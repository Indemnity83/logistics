'''Humus''' is a rich soil that grows every sapling into the same small, tidy tree. It is the ground the [[Arboretum]] tends.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Humus breaks fastest with a shovel and always drops itself.

== Usage ==

=== Tree growth ===

Any sapling planted on Humus, with or without a farm nearby, grows the same small tree: a straight trunk 4 to 6 logs tall under a round canopy, in the sapling's own wood and leaves. That keeps every tree to a known size, so a grove can be packed close together without trees growing into each other.

* Dark oak and pale oak grow from a single sapling.
* A 2×2 group of saplings grows four small trees, never one large one.
* Mangroves and azaleas grow without roots, and mangroves bear fully grown propagules.
* No beehives, vines or other decorations.

Saplings grow at the normal rate, and Humus is never used up. Humus counts as dirt, so anything that can be planted on dirt can be planted on it.

=== Arboretum ===

The [[Arboretum]] only fells trees standing on Humus. Lay Humus where you want its trees; trees on any other ground are safe from it.
