The '''Stone Fluid Pipe''' is the cheapest [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] — passive transport at 10 mB/t. It is the fluid counterpart of the [[Stone Transport Pipe]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Seal the [[Stone Transport Pipe]] with a {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime ball}} or [[Tar]] (shapeless):

{{Crafting
|head=1
|showname=0
|A1=Stone Transport Pipe |B1=Slime Ball
|Output=Stone Fluid Pipe
}}
{{Crafting
|foot=1
|showname=0
|A1=Stone Transport Pipe |B1=Tar
|Output=Stone Fluid Pipe
}}

== Usage ==

Stone fluid pipes passively move fluid between tanks, machines, and other pipes at '''10 mB/t''' with no power. The slowest pipe on a line caps its throughput, so use [[Golden Fluid Pipe|golden pipes]] on high-demand runs.
