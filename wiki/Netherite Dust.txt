'''Netherite Dust''' is ground from a {{Mcw|Netherite Ingot|netherite ingot}} in a [[Macerator]], and smelts back into an ingot — a lossless way to store or split netherite.

=== Grinding ===

{{Grinding|head=1|showname=0|foot=1

|Output=Netherite Dust,1
}}

== Usage ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt Netherite Dust in a furnace, blast furnace, or a [[Kiln]] to recover a {{Mcw|Netherite Ingot|netherite ingot}}:

{{Smelting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0

|Output=Netherite Ingot
}}
