'''Tin Ingot''' is a metal obtained by smelting [[Raw Tin]]. It is used to create [[Bronze Ingot|bronze]] alloy and tin-based components.

'''Related forms:''' Tin Ingot has a full set of storage and processing forms — '''Block of Tin''' (9 ingots), '''Tin Nugget''' (1/9 ingot), '''[[Raw Tin]]''' and the '''Block of Raw Tin''', and '''[[Dusts|Tin Dust]]''' from the [[Macerator]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Tin Ingots unpack from their storage forms:

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B2=Tin Block
|Output=Tin Ingot |OutputCount=9
}}

'''From a block:''' 1× [[Block of Tin]] = 9× Tin Ingot.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Tin Nugget |B1=Tin Nugget |C1=Tin Nugget
|A2=Tin Nugget |B2=Tin Nugget |C2=Tin Nugget
|A3=Tin Nugget |B3=Tin Nugget |C3=Tin Nugget
|Output=Tin Ingot
}}

'''From nuggets:''' 9× [[Tin Nugget]] = 1× Tin Ingot.

=== Smelting ===

Smelting raw tin or tin ore in a furnace or blast furnace is the most common way to obtain tin ingots:

{{Smelting

== Usage ==

Tin Ingot is the basis of bronze and several tin components.

=== Crafting ingredient ===

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A2=Copper Ingot |B2=Copper Ingot |C2=Copper Ingot
|A3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Bronze Ingot |OutputCount=4
}}

'''Shapeless:''' 3× [[Copper Ingot]] + 1× Tin Ingot = 4× [[Bronze Ingot]].

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B1=Tin Ingot
|A2=Tin Ingot |B2=Copper Gear |C2=Tin Ingot
|B3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Tin Gear
}}

'''Gear:''' 4× Tin Ingot around a [[Copper Gear]] = 1× [[Tin Gear]].

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Tin Ingot |B1=Tin Ingot |C1=Tin Ingot
|A2=Tin Ingot |B2=Tin Ingot |C2=Tin Ingot
|A3=Tin Ingot |B3=Tin Ingot |C3=Tin Ingot
|Output=Tin Block
}}

'''To a block:''' 9× Tin Ingot = 1× [[Block of Tin]].

{{Grid Crafting Table
|B2=Tin Ingot
|Output=Tin Nugget |OutputCount=9
}}

'''To nuggets:''' 1× Tin Ingot = 9× [[Tin Nugget]].

=== Grinding ===

Grind tin in the [[Macerator]] for Tin Dust (see [[Dusts]]).
