'''Machines''' perform tasks automatically using RF energy.

==Mining==
* '''[[Laser Quarry]]''' - Automatically mines the area below, requires RF power; default 16×16 or a custom area
* '''[[Marker]]''' - Placement aid that defines a custom [[Laser Quarry]] mining area

==Processing==
* '''[[Macerator]]''' - Grinds items into dust; 70+ recipes covering ores, plants, and dyes
* '''[[Kiln]]''' - RF-powered electric furnace; smelts any vanilla smelting recipe (no fuel — uses RF), with recipe-book GUI
