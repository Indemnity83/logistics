'''Cores''' are intermediate crafting components used to craft [[Materials#Valves|Valves]]. Each valve type requires its matching core, paired with a [[#Quartz Crystal|Quartz Crystal]]. Cores are made at the crafting table from the corresponding base material and a binder (usually Redstone).

==Core Recipes==

All cores yield '''4× per craft''' and share the same shape — the base material top and bottom with a binder in the center slot.

==Quartz Crystal==

Quartz Crystal is not a "core" but is the other required ingredient in all valve recipes.

{{Grid Crafting Table
|A1=Quartz Dust |B1=Quartz Dust |C1=Quartz Dust
|Output=Quartz Crystal
}}

'''Recipe:''' Smelt [[Dusts#Quartz Dust|Quartz Dust]] in a [[Kiln]] or furnace → 1× Quartz Crystal

Quartz Dust is obtained by grinding Nether Quartz or Quartz Ore in the [[Macerator]].

'''Progression note:''' Quartz Crystal is gated behind the Macerator, so valve crafting requires a Macerator to be operational first.
