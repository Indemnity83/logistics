'''Tar''' is a sticky oil product recovered from the sandy oil blocks in a [[Macerator]]. Its stickiness stands in for a slime ball in a few recipes, and it burns as a furnace fuel.

== Obtaining ==

=== Grinding ===

Tar drops as a 50% byproduct when the [[Macerator]] grinds [[Oil Sand]] or [[Oil Red Sand]] into [[Bitumen]]:

{{Grinding|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Sand;Oil Red Sand |ingredients=[[Oil Sand]] or [[Oil Red Sand]] |Output=Bitumen,3 |Byproduct=Tar |ByproductChance=50%}}

== Usage ==

=== Crafting ===

Tar's stickiness makes it a stand-in for slime and coal in several recipes:

{{Crafting|head=1
|name=Sticky Piston
|B1=Tar
|B2=Piston
|Output=Sticky Piston}}
{{Crafting
|name=Lead
|A1=String |B1=String
|A2=String |B2=Tar
|C3=String
|Output=Lead,2}}
{{Crafting|foot=1
|name=Torch
|B1=Tar
|B2=Stick
|Output=Torch,4}}

=== Fuel ===

Tar is a furnace fuel and powers the [[Stirling Engine]], burning for 800 ticks — half of coal — smelting 4 items.
