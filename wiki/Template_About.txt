A hatnote for the top of an article, clarifying what the page covers and pointing to related
pages. Powered by [[Module:Hatnote]]; styling lives in [[Template:Hatnote/styles.css]].

Usage:
 {{About|the item that pulls from inventories}}
 {{About|USE1|USE2|PAGE2|USE3|PAGE3}}

Renders:
 ''This article is about USE1. For USE2, see [[PAGE2]]. For USE3, see [[PAGE3]].''

Parameters (positional):
 1        — what THIS article is about (the "This article is about ___." clause)
 2, 3     — a "For ___, see [[___]]." pair (description, target page)
 4, 5 …   — further pairs, as many as needed

A trailing description with no target page renders as "For ___." with no link.
