'''Bio Fuel''' is a fluid refined from [[Liquid Biomass]] in a [[Refinery]]. Because liquid biomass is made from renewable plant matter, Bio Fuel is fully renewable.

== Obtaining ==

=== Refinery ===

{{Refinery|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Liquid Biomass |InputAmount=200 |Output=Bio Fuel |Amount=100}}

== Usage ==

Bio Fuel is burned in a [[Fuel Engine]] to generate [[RF Energy|RF]] — a renewable power source. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Bucket ===

The '''Bio Fuel Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}.
