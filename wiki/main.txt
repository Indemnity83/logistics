==About Logistics==

'''Logistics''' is a Minecraft automation mod that adds transport pipes, fluid pipes, tanks, power systems, and related tools for moving resources around a world.

The mod is developed by '''Indemnity83''' and is available through common Minecraft mod distribution platforms.
