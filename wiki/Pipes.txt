Pipes are the core of the Logistics mod, transporting items and fluids through your network. Pipes connect automatically to adjacent inventories and to each other, forming networks that move items from extractors to destinations.

== Transport Pipes ==

[[Transport Pipes]] move items between inventories — the everyday backbone of a network. They range from the slow Stone pipe to the redstone-accelerated Golden pipe, plus special pipes for extracting, merging, filtering, inserting, bypassing, and voiding items.

== Logistics Pipes ==

[[Logistics Pipes]] are item pipes that join a logistics '''network''': they advertise stock, fulfill requests, automate crafting, and keep inventories topped up. The group includes the Provider, Requester, Supplier, Crafting, Process, and Satellite pipes, plus the modular Chassis pipe.

== Fluid Pipes ==

Fluids use a '''separate''' pipe set and a standalone transport system — they never join the item network. See [[Fluid Pipes]] for the passive transport pipes, the engine-driven Fluid Extractor, the behavior pipes, and the [[Glass Tank]] and [[Pump]].
