==Pipes==

The core of Logistics - transporting items through your network.

===Tier 1: Mechanical Pipes===
* '''[[Stone Transport Pipe]]''' - Very slow transport
* '''[[Copper Transport Pipe]]''' - Standard transport backbone
* '''[[Item Extractor Pipe]]''' - Pull from inventories
* '''[[Item Merger Pipe]]''' - Converge to single output
* '''[[Golden Transport Pipe]]''' - Speed boost with redstone
* '''[[Item Passthrough Pipe]]''' - Bypass inventories
* '''[[Item Void Pipe]]''' - Delete items

===Tier 2: Smart Pipes===
* '''[[Item Filter Pipe]]''' - Route by item type
* '''[[Item Insertion Pipe]]''' - Prefer inventories with space

===Tier 3: Network Logistics===
* '''[[Basic Logistics Pipe]]''' - Network backbone
* '''[[Provider Logistics Pipe]]''' - Expose inventory to network
* '''[[Supplier Logistics Pipe]]''' - Auto-maintain stock levels
* '''[[Requester Logistics Pipe]]''' - Request items on demand
* '''[[Crafting Logistics Pipe]]''' - Craft items on demand
* '''[[Process Logistics Pipe]]''' - Automate processing machines
* '''[[Satellite Logistics Pipe]]''' - Named remote destinations
* '''[[Chassis Logistics Pipe]]''' (MK1–MK5) - Modular, module-driven behavior

==Modules==

Modules are installed into [[Chassis Logistics Pipe|Chassis Logistics Pipes]] to define their behavior. Mix and match modules to create custom logistics nodes.

* '''[[Blank Module]]''' - Base crafting component for all modules
* '''[[Extractor Module]]''' / '''[[Advanced Extractor Module]]''' - Pull items from inventories
* '''[[Provider Module]]''' - Advertise inventory contents to the network
* '''[[Supplier Module]]''' - Maintain inventory stock levels (Passive/Active)
* '''[[Crafter Module]]''' - Craft items on demand within the chassis
* '''[[Quicksort Module]]''' - Sort items into designated storage
* '''[[Terminus Module]]''' - Mark a network dead-end
* '''[[Item Sink Module]]''' / '''[[Polymorphic Sink Module|Polymorphic Sink]]''' / '''[[Enchantment Sink Module|Enchantment Sink]]''' / '''[[Mod Item Sink Module|Mod Item Sink]]''' - Accept items by type, content, or mod namespace
