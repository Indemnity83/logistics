'''Synthetic Polymer''' is a crafting component made from [[Tar]] and [[Coal Dust]]. It is smelted with [[Sulfur Dust]] in an [[Alloy Smelter]] to make [[Rubber]] — a fully mineral route that needs no {{Mcw|Slime Ball|slime balls}}.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

{{Crafting|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|A2=Tar |B2=Coal Dust
|Output=Synthetic Polymer
}}

== Usage ==

=== Alloy Smelter ===

Smelt a Synthetic Polymer with [[Sulfur Dust]] to make '''two''' [[Rubber]]:

{{Alloy Smelter|head=1|foot=1
|name=[[Rubber]]
|Input=Synthetic Polymer |Input2=Sulfur Dust |Output=Rubber |OutputCount=2
}}
