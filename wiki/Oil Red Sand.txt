'''Oil Red Sand''' is the badlands variant of [[Oil Sand]] — an oil-soaked red sand block. Extract [[Bitumen]] from it, or liquefy it into [[Crude Oil]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

Oil Red Sand breaks fastest with a shovel and always drops itself.

{{Breaking
|tool=shovel
|tier=none
|name1=Oil Red Sand |icon1=Grid Oil Red Sand.png |hardness1=0.5
}}

=== Natural generation ===

Oil Red Sand generates in large surface blobs throughout '''badlands''' biomes, and rings the [[Crude Oil]] pools of oil seeps there.

=== Transposer ===

Soaking red sand in [[Crude Oil]] recreates the block, so it is renewable once an oil supply is running:

{{Transposer|head=1|foot=1|showname=0
|Input=Red Sand |FluidIn=Crude Oil |InAmount=1000 |Output=Oil Red Sand
|notes=4,000 RF}}

== Usage ==

=== Crucible ===

The [[Crucible]] liquefies Oil Red Sand into [[Crude Oil]]:

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Red Sand |Output=Crude Oil |Amount=1000}}

=== Grinding ===

The [[Macerator]] extracts [[Bitumen]] from it, with a chance of [[Tar]]:

{{Grinding|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Oil Red Sand |Output=Bitumen,3 |Byproduct=Tar |ByproductChance=50%}}
