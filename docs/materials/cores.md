# Cores

**Cores** are intermediate crafting components used to craft [Valves](index.md#valves). Each valve type requires its matching core. Cores are made at the crafting table from the corresponding material and Redstone (or other binders).

Quartz Crystal is the other key ingredient in valve crafting and is covered below.

## Core Recipes

All cores yield **4× per craft**.

**Recipe:** Smelt [Quartz Dust](dusts.md#quartz-dust) in a [Kiln](../automation/kiln.md) or furnace → 1× Quartz Crystal

Quartz Dust is obtained by grinding Nether Quartz or Quartz Ore in the [Macerator](../automation/macerator.md).

**Progression note:** Quartz Crystal is gated behind the Macerator, so valve crafting requires a Macerator to be operational first.
