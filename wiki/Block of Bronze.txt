'''Block of Bronze''' is a storage block that compacts nine [[Bronze Ingot|bronze ingots]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crafting ===

Craft from nine [[Bronze Ingot|bronze ingots]] arranged in a 3×3 grid; the block unpacks back into nine ingots.
