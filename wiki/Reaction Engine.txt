The '''Reaction Engine''' combines a liquid reactant with a solid reagent to release a huge burst of [[RF Energy|RF]] over a short reaction. It is '''bufferless''' — the energy is pushed straight into the network as it is produced, and any that the network cannot accept is lost — so pair it with a [[Batteries|battery]] or a large draw.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

The Reaction Engine is mined with a diamond pickaxe or better; without one it drops nothing.

== Usage ==

Pipe a reactant fluid into the tank (4,000 mB) with a [[Fluid Pipes|fluid pipe]] and supply the matching solid reagent. When both are present the engine consumes them and generates its full energy across the reaction time, delivering it to the network as it goes. Because there is no internal buffer, size your cabling and storage to absorb the burst. A [https://modrinth.com/mod/jade Jade] readout shows the reactant, pressure, and generation.

=== Reactions ===

Each reaction consumes 1,000 mB of the reactant plus one reagent:

{| class="wikitable"
! Reactant !! Reagent !! Energy !! Time
|-
| {{Mcw|Lava}} || {{Mcw|Gilded Blackstone}} || 80,000 RF || 800 ticks
|-
| [[Liquid Ender]] || {{Mcw|Echo Shard}} || 100,000 RF || 500 ticks
|-
| [[Liquid Glowstone]] || {{Mcw|Enchanted Golden Apple}} || 200,000 RF || 500 ticks
|}

Every reaction is listed in the Just Enough Items (JEI) recipe browser, with the RF it returns and how long it runs.

=== Redstone control ===

By default the Reaction Engine starts a reaction only with a redstone signal, such as a lever on the engine. Its [[Side Tabs#Redstone Control|Redstone Control]] tab can set it to ignore redstone, or to run only without a signal. The signal is not passed on to neighboring blocks, so a lever on the engine does not also switch the machine it powers. Its [[Side Tabs#Energy|Energy]] tab shows the RF/t it is generating. A reaction that has started always runs to the end, even if the signal is removed.

=== Configuration ===

Use a [[Wrench]] to rotate the output face.
