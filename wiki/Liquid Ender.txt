'''Liquid Ender''' is a molten fluid produced by melting ender pearls in a [[Crucible]].

== Obtaining ==

=== Crucible ===

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Ender Pearl |Output=Liquid Ender |Amount=250}}

== Usage ==

Liquid Ender has no processing use yet — it is stored and piped like any other fluid. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Bucket ===

The '''Liquid Ender Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary {{Mcw|Bucket|bucket}}.
