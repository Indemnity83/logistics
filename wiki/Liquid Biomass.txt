'''Liquid Biomass''' is a molten fluid produced by melting [[Pulped Biomass]] in a [[Crucible]]. Because pulped biomass is made from renewable plant matter, Liquid Biomass is fully renewable.

== Obtaining ==

=== Crucible ===

{{Crucible|head=1|foot=1 |Input=Pulped Biomass |Output=Liquid Biomass |Amount=100}}

== Usage ==

Liquid Biomass has no processing use yet — it is stored and piped like any other fluid. Move it with [[Fluid Pipes]] and buffer it in a [[Glass Tank]].

=== Bucket ===

The '''Liquid Biomass Bucket''' is a container-only item: it fills and drains tanks and machines, but cannot be poured into the world, emptying back to an ordinary bucket.
