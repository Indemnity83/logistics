'''Raw Tin''' is the item dropped by [[Tin Ore]]. Smelt it into a [[Tin Ingot]] for crafting [[Bronze Ingot|bronze]] and tin components.

== Obtaining ==

=== Breaking ===

[[Tin Ore]] and Deepslate Tin Ore drop Raw Tin when mined with a '''stone pickaxe''' or better; the Fortune enchantment increases the drop. Grinding the ore in a [[Macerator]] instead yields '''2× [[Dusts|Tin Dust]]''' (ore doubling).

=== Crafting ===

Nine Raw Tin pack into a '''Block of Raw Tin''' for compact storage, and the block unpacks back into nine.

== Usage ==

=== Smelting ===

Smelt Raw Tin in a furnace or blast furnace to produce one [[Tin Ingot]].
