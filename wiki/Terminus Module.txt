The '''Terminus Module''' is a [[Chassis Logistics Pipe]] module that marks the chassis as a '''network terminus''' — a defined end-point in the [[Pipe Networks|logistics network]]. Items routed to this terminus are held or redirected according to the module's configuration.

The matching [[Chipsets|chipset]] can replace the gear — use an [[Iron Chipset]] in place of the [[Iron Gear]] to craft it with a [[Sequential Fabricator]].

== Usage ==

When installed in a [[Chassis Logistics Pipe]], the Terminus Module prevents items from passing further through the network at this pipe. Items that arrive here are deposited into an adjacent inventory. It effectively creates a dead-end in the network, ensuring items don't loop or get lost.

* Marks the chassis as a network dead-end.
* Items arriving at this point are deposited into an adjacent inventory.
* Prevents routing loops in complex networks.
* Can be combined with filter modules to create a selective terminus.

Place it at the physical end of a pipe run to stop items from bouncing back into the network, and combine it with an [[Item Sink Module]] to deposit specific item types at the terminus.
